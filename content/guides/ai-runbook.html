01

Scope & ground rules

Products in scope: AI Front Desk (After Hours and Full Time), AI SMS Agent, AI Webchat and Call Intelligence. Automated Reminders and Automated Forms are not AI products, even when the client says they are.

RULE 01 “The AI did it” is not a diagnosis

Most AI tickets turn out to be Online Scheduling, EHR sync, call flow or Automated Reminders. Prove the AI agent is involved before routing to the AI team, misrouting is the single biggest cause of delay.

RULE 02 Existing SOPs still apply in full

AI Front Desk changes nothing in the Online Scheduling SOP or the call-flow SOP. AI is a layer added at the end of the call tree, not a replacement for either process.

RULE 03 Authorised still means notify

SMS buffer changes, call-flow edits and emergency AI removal are all T1 actions, and all require an intimation to AI Support so every team knows the current configuration.

RULE 04 No call link, no ticket

Any suspected AI behaviour issue needs patient name, patient callback number, date and time, and the call link from the AI Agent module. Tickets without them come back to T1.

Where you work in the product: Settings > AI Agent (agent settings, Test the Call Flow, SMS Agent settings) · Call Flow and Call Flow Templates · AI Agent Insights · Settings > Billing > Invoice > Overages. Raise an access request if any of the four is missing.

Routing map

Work the gates top to bottom. The order matters: the upper gates catch the tickets that look like AI faults but are not, so a ticket only reaches the AI team once the cheaper explanations are ruled out.

05

Triage cases

Fourteen cases covering everything that reaches T1 on an AI account. Complete every check in order before routing, a case is only escalated once its checks are documented as clean.

CASE 01Online Scheduling wearing an AI costumeHigh

“Appointments aren’t booking correctly.” · “We had a double booking.” · “It never synced to the EHR.” · “The AI put it in the wrong slot.”

  1. Treat this as an Online Scheduling issue and apply that SOP in full, exactly as for a non-AI client.
  2. Verify provider, operatory and column mapping, and the availability rules.
  3. Verify the appointment types and durations exposed to online booking.
  4. Verify the EHR/PMS sync: agent connected and running, errors or queued items, time of the last successful sync.
  5. Reproduce with the specific appointments, date, time, patient, provider, and compare Adit against the EHR.
  6. Check for a disabled double-booking rule or block-out, and for manual EHR bookings that never synced back.
  7. Only once 1-6 are clean and documented, consider whether the fault is AI-specific (for example the agent captured the wrong appointment type from the caller).
Route to
Online Scheduling SOP owner. AI Support only if checks 1-6 are clean and the fault is provably AI-side.
Attach
Patient name · appointment date, time and provider · EHR screenshot · Adit screenshot · sync status or log · call link if the booking came through the AI agent.
CASE 02Out-of-office, overrides and call-tree changesNormal

“Set up an out-of-office for the holiday.” · “Change our call tree.” · “Route after-hours calls differently.”

  1. Confirm this is a standard call-flow request. Having AI Front Desk changes nothing about how the tree is built.
  2. The one difference: where a non-AI client ends the tree in voicemail, an AI Front Desk client ends it in the AI Agent number, AI Front Desk replaces the voicemail.
  3. Get the number from Settings > AI Agent > Test the Call Flow.
  4. Confirm it sits as an external element at the end of the tree. Everything above it follows the existing SOP.
  5. Make the change yourself, or hand to VoIP T1 with the AI number stated on the ticket.
  6. Test the flow with a live call, then record the before and after states.
Route to
T1 Support, or VoIP T1 Support.
Attach
Requested schedule · call tree before and after · the AI number used · test-call result.
CASE 03SMS Agent timing or on/offNormal

“It replies to patients too quickly.” · “It’s answering wrong.” · “Turn the SMS agent off.”

  1. Confirm the SMS Agent is actually enabled in Settings > AI Agent > SMS Agent settings. If it is off, go to Case 09.
  2. Replying too quickly → increase the buffer time. Too slowly → reduce it. Agree the value with the client before saving.
  3. For stop/start, toggle the SMS Agent as requested.
  4. Save, reload, and confirm the value persisted.
  5. Send the intimation to AI Support: client, change, old value, new value, requester, reason.
  6. If the complaint is about what the agent said rather than when, buffer time is the wrong lever, capture the thread and escalate.
Route to
T1 makes the change. Intimation to AI Support is mandatory. Content and accuracy complaints go to AI Support (T2).
Attach
Setting changed · old and new buffer value · requester · SMS thread screenshots if accuracy is disputed.
CASE 04Call Intelligence, any symptomHigh

“Calls aren’t being recognised.” · “Wrong user on the call.” · “Call entry not found.” · “Recording not found.” · “The data doesn’t match.”

  1. Call Intelligence is a static product. T1 does not reconfigure it, do not attempt fixes.
  2. Send the first intimation to AI Support (Antony and Liam) immediately.
  3. In parallel, capture the calls: date, time, direction, caller number, the user it was attributed to, and what the client expected.
  4. Name the sub-symptom explicitly: user recognition, call recognition, call entry not found, recording not found, or data discrepancy.
  5. Record how many calls are affected, and whether it is a one-off or ongoing since a date.
  6. If the symptom is a missing recording or missed-call mismatch, also run Case 05.
Route to
AI Support, Antony & Liam. Intimation before anything else; speed matters more than a complete diagnosis.
Attach
Call date and time · caller number · expected vs actual user · screenshots · count of affected calls · start date.
CASE 05Recordings missing or missed-call data mismatchHigh

“The recording isn’t there.” · “Our missed-call numbers don’t match.”

  1. Pin the exact calls: date, time, direction, numbers, and where the client is looking.
  2. Check whether the recording is absent everywhere or only in one view.
  3. Route to VoIP Support, they take ownership and decide whether a QA review is required.
  4. Keep AI Support informed if the same calls also show a Call Intelligence discrepancy.
Route to
VoIP Support. Copy AI Support when Call Intelligence data is affected too.
Attach
Call list with date, time and numbers · screenshots · historical or ongoing.
CASE 06AI transfers the call, office phones stay silentHigh

“The AI says it’s transferring us to the front office, but our phones never ring.”

  1. Do not escalate on sight, this is usually a call-flow template problem.
  2. Open Call Flow Templates and find the AI Agent Transfer template. AI clients always have one.
  3. Verify destination extensions or ring group, devices included, ring duration, and that the template is active and assigned.
  4. Confirm the main call flow still ends in the AI number as an external element.
  5. Confirm the destination phones are registered and online, not in DND or covered by an override.
  6. Place a test call through the AI agent and request a transfer. Watch whether the office phones ring.
  7. Only if all of the above is correct, escalate to AI Support with the test-call evidence.
Route to
T1 verifies the template. AI Support (T2) if it is already correct.
Attach
Template screenshot · destination extensions · test-call date, time and result · call link of a failed transfer.
CASE 07AI captured the wrong informationHigh

“It booked the wrong appointment type.” · “The insurance details it took are wrong.” · “It got the patient’s details wrong.”

  1. Rule out Online Scheduling first: if it is about the slot, availability, double booking or sync, this is Case 01.
  2. If it is about the information the agent collected, build the evidence pack: patient name; patient callback number; date and time of the call.
  3. Record what the AI captured against what it should have been, field by field.
  4. Attach the call link from the AI Agent module so the team can listen without going back to the client.
  5. Raise the ticket to AI Support (T2) using template T-01.
  6. Tell the client the AI team will review the call and respond on the ticket. Do not speculate about the cause.
Route to
AI Support (T2).
Attach
Patient name · callback number · call date and time · call link · captured value vs correct value.
CASE 08AI Front Desk did not answerUrgent

“Nobody answered after hours.” · “The AI didn’t pick up.”

  1. Is the AI number still the external element at the end of the tree? Compare against Test the Call Flow.
  2. Is an override or out-of-office bypassing the AI leg?
  3. Check business hours, the After Hours package only answers outside configured hours.
  4. Place a test call from Test the Call Flow and confirm the agent answers.
  5. Check usage and overage status in case the account has hit a limit.
  6. If configuration is correct and it still does not answer, escalate to AI Support with the test-call details.
Route to
T1 verifies configuration; AI Support if configuration is clean.
Attach
Test-call result · call flow screenshot · business hours configuration · example affected calls.
CASE 09Reminders and forms mistaken for the AINormal

“The AI is texting our patients.” · “The AI sent a form.”, on an account with no SMS Agent.

  1. Check whether the SMS Agent is enabled at all in Settings > AI Agent > SMS Agent settings.
  2. If it is not, the messages come from Automated Reminders and Automated Forms. This confusion is common.
  3. Prove it: match the message content and send time against the reminder/form configuration.
  4. Tell the client plainly which system sent the message and why.
  5. Route the ticket to the Automated Reminders / Forms owner, not the AI team.
  6. Only with evidence that the AI agent sent it, or sent something wrong, raise a ticket to AI Support (T2).
Route to
Automated Reminders / Forms owner. AI Support only when the AI agent is proven to be the sender.
Attach
Screenshot of the message · send timestamp · SMS Agent on/off status · matching reminder or form configuration.
CASE 10Billing dispute or overage questionNormal

“We were charged extra for the AI.” · “What is this overage?”

  1. Open Settings > Billing > Invoice and read the Overages section for the disputed period.
  2. Identify the package, After Hours (200 minutes, then $1.00/min) or Full Time (1,000 minutes, then $0.50/min).
  3. Pull actual usage from AI Agent Insights > Front Desk for the same period.
  4. Reconcile: (minutes used − included minutes) × rate, and show the client the arithmetic.
  5. Explain that included minutes reset monthly and never roll over, each month restarts at zero.
  6. Wants more minutes? Raise an upgrade ticket to AI Support. Figures genuinely don’t reconcile? Escalate with both screenshots.
Route to
T1 explains. AI Support for upgrades or genuine discrepancies.
Attach
Overages screenshot · Insights usage screenshot · package type · the figure the client expected.
CASE 11Emergency, “take the AI off our phones now”Urgent

“Remove AI Front Desk from our call flow immediately.”

  1. Confirm with the client that they want the AI leg removed now, this is a live phone change.
  2. Open the call flow settings and locate the AI Agent number at the bottom of the flow.
  3. Remove that element and put voicemail back in its place, so callers still land somewhere.
  4. Save, then test-call to confirm callers reach voicemail.
  5. Inform the AI team immediately so they can take further action.
  6. Document who asked, when, and exactly what was removed. This is not a cancellation, if they want to cancel, also run Case 13.
Route to
T1 performs it under emergency authority. Immediate notification to the AI team is mandatory.
Attach
Requester and time · call flow before and after · test-call result · reason given.
CASE 12Package upgrade or more minutesNormal

“We keep going over, can we get more minutes?” · “Move us to full time.”

  1. Confirm the current package and pull current usage from Insights.
  2. Explain the inclusions and the per-minute rate so the client can compare.
  3. Raise a ticket to AI Support with client, current package, requested package and usage figures.
  4. AI Support processes the upgrade. Do not promise a price or an effective date.
Route to
AI Support team.
Attach
Current package · requested package · last one to three months of usage.
CASE 13Cancellation requestHigh

“We want to cancel Call Intelligence / AI Front Desk / AI SMS / AI Webchat.”

  1. Do not process it, do not negotiate, do not confirm any date.
  2. Capture the client name requesting the cancellation and a proper contact email, both mandatory on the ticket.
  3. Capture the products, the stated reason and the requested effective date.
  4. Route: Call Intelligence to Aijaz and Antony; AI Front Desk, SMS or Webchat to Zarka and Aijaz.
  5. Tell the client the request has gone to the AI team, who will contact them.
Route to
Named owners only, see section 09.
Attach
Practice name · requester name · requester email · products · reason · requested date.
CASE 14Downgrade requestHigh

“We want to move to a smaller package.”

  1. Do not process or quote anything.
  2. Capture client name, contact email, current package, requested package and reason.
  3. Send directly to the AI leads: Aijaz and Zarka.
  4. If they do not respond, escalate to Antony and Padma.
  5. The AI team owns the process from that point.
Route to
AI leads, Aijaz & Zarka. Backup: Antony & Padma.
Attach
Practice name · requester and email · current and requested package · reason.

Call flow & AI

Adding AI Front Desk changes exactly one node in the call tree. Knowing which one is most of what T1 needs for call-flow, out-of-office and emergency-removal work.

07

Runbooks

Step-by-step procedures for every action T1 is authorised to perform. Record the outcome of each on the ticket.

RB-01Add or verify the AI number in the call flowT1 / VoIP T1
  1. Open Settings > AI Agent on the correct client account.
  2. Open Test the Call Flow; the number shown is the client’s AI Agent number.
  3. Copy it exactly and record it on the ticket.
  4. Open the client’s call flow.
  5. Add the number as an external element at the end of the tree, where a non-AI client has voicemail.
  6. Build the rest of the tree per the existing SOP, nothing else changes.
  7. Save, then place a test call through to the end of the tree and confirm the agent answers.
  8. Screenshot before and after; attach with the test result.
  9. Notify AI Support whenever the AI element is added, changed or removed.
RB-02Out-of-office and overrides on an AI accountT1 / VoIP T1
  1. Get the requested schedule in writing on the ticket: dates, times, desired behaviour.
  2. Apply the existing override SOP exactly as for a non-AI client.
  3. Confirm the AI number remains the last external element unless removal was requested.
  4. If the override is meant to bypass the AI, confirm that explicitly with the client and notify the AI team.
  5. Save, test inside and outside the override window where possible, and document.
  6. Not comfortable? Hand to VoIP T1 with the AI number and schedule on the ticket, never leave it unassigned.
RB-03SMS Agent buffer and enable/disableT1 Support
  1. Open Settings > AI Agent > SMS Agent settings and check whether the agent is enabled.
  2. Disabled, but the client says the AI is texting? Stop, run RB-06.
  3. Agree the exact change and target value with the client; record who asked.
  4. Note the old value, then adjust the buffer time (up to slow it down, down to speed it up) or toggle the agent.
  5. Save, reload, screenshot the persisted value.
  6. Send the intimation to AI Support: client, change, old and new value, requester, reason.
  7. Complaint about content rather than timing? Buffer is the wrong lever, escalate with the thread.
RB-04Transfer fails, office phones do not ringT1 verifies → AI Support
  1. Open Call Flow Templates and find AI Agent Transfer.
  2. Verify destinations, devices, ring duration, active state and assignment; screenshot it.
  3. Verify the main flow still ends in the AI number.
  4. Confirm the destination phones are registered, online, not in DND or overridden.
  5. Test-call through the agent and request a transfer; record date, time and result.
  6. All correct and still failing? Ticket to AI Support with the screenshots and a failed-transfer call link.
Do not escalate before steps 1-5 are complete and documented.
RB-05Evidence pack for wrong informationT1 captures → AI Support
  1. Rule out Online Scheduling (Case 01) before anything else.
  2. Capture the patient name.
  3. Capture the patient callback number, this is how the AI team finds the interaction.
  4. Capture the date and time of the call.
  5. Record captured value vs correct value, field by field.
  6. Attach the call link from the AI Agent module.
  7. Raise to AI Support (T2) with template T-01, and set the client’s expectation that the team will review the recording.
RB-06AI agent, or Automated Reminders?Reminders owner unless proven
  1. Check whether the SMS Agent is enabled for the client.
  2. If not, the sender is Automated Reminders or Automated Forms.
  3. Match content and send timestamp against that configuration; screenshot both.
  4. Explain to the client which system sent the message and why.
  5. Route to the Automated Reminders / Forms owner.
  6. Only with evidence that the AI agent sent it, raise a ticket to AI Support.
RB-07Emergency, remove AI from the call flowT1 emergency authority
  1. Confirm the request and requester on the ticket; note the time. This is a live phone change.
  2. Open call flow settings and locate the AI Agent number at the bottom of the flow.
  3. Remove that element.
  4. Replace it with voicemail, never leave the end of the tree empty.
  5. Save and test-call to confirm callers reach voicemail.
  6. Inform the AI team immediately so they can take further action.
  7. Document the before and after flow. If the client wants to cancel, additionally run the cancellation process.
RB-08Billing and overage explanationT1 Support
  1. Open Settings > Billing > Invoice > Overages for the disputed period.
  2. Identify the package and its included minutes and rate.
  3. Open AI Agent Insights > Front Desk and pull usage for the same period.
  4. Reconcile and show the arithmetic to the client.
  5. State that included minutes reset monthly and do not roll over.
  6. Upgrade wanted? Ticket to AI Support, never quote pricing yourself.
  7. Figures don’t reconcile? Escalate with invoice, overage and usage screenshots.
RB-09Call Intelligence intimationAI Support, Antony & Liam
  1. Make no configuration changes, the product is static.
  2. Send the first intimation to Antony and Liam straight away.
  3. Capture the affected calls: date, time, direction, caller number, attributed user, expected vs actual.
  4. State the sub-symptom explicitly.
  5. Note one-off vs ongoing, and the start date.
  6. Missing recording or missed-call mismatch? Also run RB-10.
RB-10Missing recordings and missed-call mismatchesVoIP Support
  1. Collect the exact calls with date, time, direction and numbers.
  2. Note where the client is looking and whether the recording is missing in every view.
  3. Route to VoIP Support, they take over and decide whether QA is required.
  4. Copy AI Support if the same calls also show a Call Intelligence discrepancy.
