1. What Adit Pay is

Payments module inside Adit, powered by Stripe. Each practice is a Stripe Custom connected account (CONFIRMED). Adit support agents have their own Stripe Dashboard logins.
Ways to collect: Text to Pay / payment link, email, Quick Text, Charge Payment on File (up to 10 cards per patient), Manual Card Entry, In-Office terminal, Offline (cash/check), payment plans, subscriptions, treatment plan checkout, online scheduling deposit, bulk payment requests from Practice Analytics patient lists, Process Insurance Payment.
Menu: Payments (tabs All, Paid, Unpaid, Refunded, Voided, Expired, Merged), Payment Plans, Subscriptions, Disputes, Payouts, Insights, Payment Reminders, Preferences.
Integrations (posting to ledger): Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, ClearDent, Tracker, EasyDental, Curve, Crystal, AbleDent, Jane, ChiroTouch, Dolphin, OfficeMate/Revolution, OrthoTrac, Cloud9, Practice-web (website logos; per-EHR depth not stated).
Mobile app: no ACH and no convenience fee (needs the multi-card layer first).

2. Convenience fee (CONFIRMED)

Setting: Adit Pay > Preferences > Payment Requests > "Pass Through Adit Fees to Patient" (one-time payments toggle).
Applies only to one-time card payments (credit, debit, Apple Pay, Google Pay). Uniform; cannot target or exclude a card type.
Card present (terminal): 2.25% + $0.20. Card not present (online, Text to Pay, requests, card on file, manual entry): 2.9% + $0.30.
Grossed up so the practice nets the full service amount: Total = (Service + fixed) / (1 - rate). $1,000 CNP = $30.18 fee, $1,030.18 total. $1,000 terminal = $23.22 fee. $100 terminal = $2.51; $100 online = $3.30.
Never on ACH/bank, cash or check, payment plans or subscriptions, CareCredit, or Collect Payment on a plan installment.
What avoids the fee is the payment method, not paying in person: the terminal still charges the card-present fee.
Not available for practices in Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, or Canada.
The fee never posts to the ledger; only the service amount posts. Refunds can return the full amount including fee.
Merging a fee-less direct payment into a request that had a fee strips the fee from that request.
Known display error: some screens/receipts showed $29.30 / $1,029.30 for $1,000; correct is $30.18.
Compliance stance: optional, practice's decision; Adit does not confirm state or card-network legality and does not write patient-facing notices. Point the practice to its legal or compliance advisor. KB: help.adit.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-are-convenience-fees-different-from-surcharges-and-why-is-this-not-a-surcharge
OPEN: HSA/FSA (one doc says never charged; Stripe cannot detect HSA/FSA, so do not promise it in writing). Older Figma text shows card present + $0.30; settled rate is + $0.20.

3. Bank / ACH (CONFIRMED unless tagged)

US only. Setting: Preferences > Payment Requests > Payment and Subscription Plans > "Allow Bank/ACH Payments".
Banks added only via instant login (Stripe Financial Connections); manual micro-deposit verification is being cut. Added via Request Payment Method, patient card > Payment Method Info > Request, or plan activation. One bank per patient ("This bank account is already on file"); cards capped at 10.
One-time bank payments allowed via Quick Text, Charge Payment on File, Text to Pay link (shown in a separate section). Not via Collect Payment, not via the terminal.
Settles in 1 to 4 business days (2 with faster settlement). Patient copy: "Bank payments typically take 1-4 business days to complete."
Balance checked at charge time on every charge; insufficient balance = not charged. Practice never sees the patient's balance.
A processing bank payment cannot be cancelled and cannot be collected another way.
Plans: activate when bank details are submitted. A failed bank instalment falls back to cards on file (cards only); staff-started charges, deposits and one-off payments never fall back. Silent recovery: if a card recovers a failed bank instalment, nobody is alerted.
Disabling ACH is blocked until active bank-based plans are moved to another method.
Returns and disputes: a bank payment can come back days later as a dispute; bank disputes cannot be won; a dispute invalidates the bank (marked Invalid), a second dispute blocks it.
Refunds on bank payments: up to 3 business days, cannot be cancelled, 180-day window; partial refunds OPEN.
Stripe emails the patient a mandate confirmation from [contact] (unbranded) to the email entered on the bank.
Fees: practice ACH fee shown in Preferences design as 0.5% + $1.75 per transaction; Adit's own Stripe cost 0.8% capped at $5; return fee $4 and dispute fee $15 observed on beta (OPEN, Finance to confirm; pass-through to practice OPEN).

4. Terminal (CONFIRMED)

Current terminal: Stripe S700 (also S710 / WisePOS E in the field). $100 one time incl. shipping and tax, 1-year warranty. Free terminal eligibility: practice cash volume above $50,000 (AditKB rule).
Register via Getting Started > Register Terminal. Card only; no ACH on the terminal.
Desktop app terminal communication errors (Bay Breeze, Sylvania, New Age, New Sight, 2026): root cause was the desktop app's network config; fixed build released. Workaround: take the payment in the browser web app; logout/login or re-register the reader sometimes helps. Error copy: "No Active Terminal Available. Please check your network connection or try to restart device."
PIN/signature validation for in-office payments: requested, open.

5. Payment requests logic (DESIGN, live)

One open request per patient: sending a new request edits the existing one and replaces the amount (no longer adds). Editing an expired request via Quick Text reactivates it; timeline logs each change. "Save and Send" re-triggers the reminder.
Changing a request to an amount equal to what was already paid marks it fully paid.
Process Insurance Payment: does not post to the patient's ledger. Insurance payments under $1.00 cannot be processed (enhancement open).
Direct payment link (website link) can show balance $0 because it is not tied to the EHR balance (PineCone Vision Center, open).

6. Refunds and payouts

Refunds are funded from the practice's future payout balance (Available + Pending). If the balance is insufficient the refund fails (recurring client complaint, Asana "Insufficient Balance to Process Refunds"). Designed (not confirmed live): debit a connected external bank with a 1.75% fee; 30-minute cancel window on a requested card refund.
Refund amount can be full or partial from Initiate Refund on the payment detail page (known issue: full refund issued instead of partial, #401502 and others, open).
Payouts: schedule set by the practice (daily, weekly, monthly; default weekly Tuesday). Daily payout shows a fee warning pop-up. Manual payout hides which transactions are bundled. Future Payouts = available + pending; In Transit = payouts in transit.
Payout bank changes: practices cannot add new payout banks after setup; support adds them through Stripe after verification. In progress: MFA / one-time code (10 minutes) for payout bank or business details changes, "Payout Account Changed" email alert, payout audit log. T+1 payout in development.

7. Disputes (card)

Practice notified by email, in-app and Disputes tab. Amount plus $15 fee deducted at creation. 7 to 21 days to respond; no response = auto-accepted, funds reversed.
Submit Evidence: Adit Pay > Disputes > patient > Submit Evidence. Decision takes about 60 to 75 days. Won: funds and $15 returned. Lost: neither returned. Accept Dispute refunds the patient and cannot be undone.
Off-boarded clients: no dashboard access; they reply to the dispute email with evidence and Adit submits it. Reminder email before the deadline.
Recurring dispute reminders: requested (open).

8. Payment plans and subscriptions

Plans wait in Inactive until the patient adds a payment method via the activation link. Pause, scheduled pause, resume and resume-date editing exist; resuming runs the next instalment immediately and moves future billing to the new date.
Deleting a card tied to a plan stops future payments (warning shown). Missed instalments can be collected via Collect Payment.
Customising plan schedules and collecting future instalments early: requested (open).

9. Posting to the EHR / ledger (largest ticket topic, 180 tickets)

Common causes seen in solved Asana tasks:
Server/sync app stopped (log errors): payments queue and post once the server app is reinstalled or restarted (Luvic Advanced Dentistry, Sep 2026).
Duplicate or soft-deleted patient profile: plan linked to a patient with no EHR ID, so payments do not post. Dev relinks plans to the correct EHR patient; past missed payments are not auto-posted (practice posts manually or sends the list).
Patient ID not generated / not synced: patient sync interval can be shortened (90 to 50 minutes) with the client's approval, weighing EHR load.
Duplicate posting in Dentrix: backend usually shows a single Adit entry; check whether staff added a manual entry; dev needs unattended access plus EHR login to verify.
Wrong provider (Dolphin, Equinox): fixed by a server app update that reads the patient's selected provider.
Balance not updating without an extra manual step (some EHRs, Eaglesoft 20.0): sync app update.
Payment posting latency (Eaglesoft), daysheet totals missing Adit payments, refunds causing balance discrepancies (Gill Orthodontics, Dolphin).
Process: confirm EHR and version, get unattended access (UA) to the server, raise an Asana task in Adit Pay Team with patient, amount, date and screenshots, keep the client updated. Never paste EHR or server passwords into tickets or Asana.

13. From the Adit Pay team documents (Support Guide, SOP, Stripe document, Payment Case Investigation, Payment Issues sheet, module training doc, Integration matrix, Offboarding SOP)

Pricing and hardware

Processing: card not present 2.9% + $0.30; card present 2.25% + $0.20 (SOP and setup screens agree).
EHR sync interval varies by EHR: standard 30 seconds, maximum up to 5 minutes. Docking station $49; S700 case $19; S700 hub (Ethernet) $39. Return within 30 days of order only (Stripe accepts no returns after 30 days); 1-year warranty.
Shop now in Preferences > Terminals: order can be cancelled within 30 minutes; delivery 4 to 5 business days.
Register a terminal: Preferences > Terminals > Add terminal; on the reader swipe left to right > Settings > Wi-Fi > Generate pairing code; enter the code and a label (for example "Check-in"); allow 30 minutes for the reader to update.

Terminal troubleshooting

Browser web app: follow KB "Troubleshooting Terminal Connection Issues".
Desktop app: ping the terminal's IP from the computer. If it replies (same network), set the network adapter DNS to 1.1.1.1 (preferred) and 8.8.8.8 (alternate) under IPv4 properties, then test; tell the practice's IT so they can fix the network DNS. If it does not reply, the terminal is on a different network: schedule a call with the practice and its IT to put both on the same Wi-Fi.
Power cycle and connectivity check first; escalate if unresolved.

Posting to the EHR (Support Guide SOP, Feb 2026)

Posting settings: Settings > Locations > (location) > EHR > Edit Settings. Communication Log (notes: calls, texts, payment notes) and Patient Ledger (payments, refunds, discounts). Ledger posting needs Adit Pay enabled under "Select which activities will appear in the patient ledger". Payments post within 5 to 10 minutes.
Unpaid requests never post; insurance payments (Process Insurance Payment) never post.
On-prem EHR checklist: patient name, amount, date/time; server access; confirm missing in ledger; Adit user selected in EHR settings and posting enabled; not an insurance payment; wait 5 minutes (escalate after 10 to 15). Then Asana task "Practice Name (as in Admin App) - Payment Posting Issue - EHR Name" with issue, patient, date/time, type, EHR, server app version, screenshots; share in the EHR Payment Issues Google Chat group; state live access and whether unattended access was given or denied.
Cloud EHR: check the working station sync status; posting enabled; not insurance; wait 15 minutes; task with extension version; share in the integration group.
Every posting task adds the account's OB/CSM and the OB and CSM managers.
Duplicate posting: each Adit entry carries a unique payment ID (UQID) in the description; same UQID twice = duplicate; different UQIDs = two real payments. Adit entries are made by the Adit user with payment mode AditPay; an entry edited by another user means someone at the practice changed it.
"Only ledger" / "only log": setting value 1 = log only, 2 = ledger only, 3 = both.
Dev path: no posting response in the AditEventListener or PaymentLogFile logs means the web side did not send it (Web team). Response present but not posted = EHR team root cause.
Duplicates seen recently in the Dentrix ledger and Open Dental log; fixed by updating the server app.

Disputes are handled by the practice (evidence or accept); the team guides using the KB article.

Onboarding and access

Owner-level access is required to set up or change Adit Pay settings. Giving someone owner access needs the practice owner's verbal or written approval and whether it is temporary or permanent; the TL/manager changes the access.
Sign-up is always done by the client; the team never collects SSN or personal details by email or call and never completes sign-up for them.
Required: individual (US and Canada): legal name, owner full SSN, driver's license image, bank details; company (US): EIN, legal name, owner full SSN, bank details.
Explain processing fees before sign-up, every time. Do not call escalated clients for Adit Pay demos until de-escalated, and wait if they have an open ticket that could escalate. Book on the Adit Pay Training calendar.
Change of ownership: Preferences > Business Details (Identity: EIN, legal name, website; Management & ownership: new owner name, email for the 1099-K, text-enabled mobile, home address, full SSN entered by the client only; add the new bank as Checking without deleting the old one; confirm payout schedule; update payout and payment notification emails). Can trigger verification review or payout hold.
1099-K corrections are made in the Stripe dashboard (Tax forms > Correct > File) after the client confirms EIN, payee name and address in writing or on a call.

Payouts

Daily, weekly or monthly; daily is available to everyone and enabled from the app. Funds reach the bank in 1 to 2 business days. Canada has a 7-day rolling period before daily payouts start. Verify the change in Stripe and attach a screenshot to the ticket.
In Transit to Bank = already sent; Future Payout = recently collected, goes in a future payout (refunds are taken from it); Total Balance = both; Total Lifetime Payout = all deposits to date.

EHR migration

Connecting a new EHR to the same Adit location wipes the old synced data permanently: get written client approval first.
To keep old data, create a new location (Tech OB) and move the phone number to it, otherwise payment requests will not work.
Old unpaid requests: cancel and resend from the new location; old payment plans stay on the old location and their payments must be posted manually; recreate reminders and Payment Request page settings.

Offboarding (churn)

Reach the client about 7 days before offboarding: cancel unpaid links, mark partial payments paid, set plans and subscriptions inactive or completed, download history, payout reports and plans. Data is kept only 30 to 45 days after deletion.
Adit never closes or deletes Stripe accounts: pause payments, wait for final payouts to settle, then pause payouts.

14. Verification code not received for payout or ownership changes (MFA OTP agent SOP, draft until MFA goes live)

For T1 CS and the Adit Pay team. Use it when a practice cannot finish a change to its payout bank account or its Management & Ownership details because the verification code is not arriving (T1 CS: a call, a chat, or a ticket from "Contact Adit Support" in the app; the Adit Pay team handles calls and tickets only, never chat).

How the verification code works

Editing the payout bank or the ownership details under Adit Pay > Preferences > Business Details texts a 6-digit code to the phone number listed under Management & Ownership. That is the owner's number, not the business phone and not the practice's outgoing number. The code is sent from an Adit number, so it never appears in the practice's Engage inbox.
The code expires after 10 minutes. The user gets 5 attempts before a new code is needed, and can resend at most once every 60 seconds. After a payout bank change the owner also gets a "Your Payout Banking Information Was Changed" email.

Step 1: Basic troubleshooting (solves most cases)

1. Check the number: the last two digits shown in the dialog ("ending in ••XX") must belong to the phone the owner has with them now.
2. Wait, then resend: wait a minute and use Resend code. Only the newest code works; ignore earlier codes.
3. Check the phone: filtered or spam messages, and that messages from unknown senders are not blocked.
4. Start over if stuck: if the code expired or the attempts are used up, close the dialog and start the change again for a fresh code.
5. Hand over to the owner: if the person asking is not the owner, explain the code only goes to the owner's phone; the owner must be present to complete the change.
If the code arrives and the change goes through, close the ticket as resolved.

Step 2: When the code cannot be received (verify first, change nothing until done)

Covers a landline in the field, an owner who no longer has that number, or a code that never arrives after Step 1.
1. Confirm who you are dealing with: work only with the person named under Management & Ownership. Anyone else: ask them to have the owner contact us; never take instructions on the owner's behalf.
2. Call back on a number we already have: from CRM or the account record, never one the caller gives during the conversation. If no other number is on file, go to the next step and wait for the email reply.
3. Email the owner: send a verification email to the owner email on the account, saying what change was requested and asking them to reply to confirm. Wait for the reply.
4. Check the details match: the owner's name must match Management & Ownership and the reply must come from the email on file.

Step 3: Make the change (after the owner is verified)

1. Update only the phone: change the Management & Ownership phone to the owner's current mobile number and confirm it is a mobile, not a landline. (To be confirmed with Dev: whether agents make this change in the admin app or through the Adit Pay team.)
2. Let the owner finish: the owner makes the bank or ownership change themselves; a new code goes to the updated number. Stay on the call or chat until they confirm it worked.
3. Confirm the bank email: for payout bank changes, check the owner received the "Payout Banking Information Was Changed" email.

Never do these

Never ask for a code, read one out, or accept one from anyone.
Never change the payout bank or ownership details for the practice; the owner always completes the change.
Never update the Management & Ownership phone without finishing all of Step 2.
Never use the business phone, or a number the caller has just given, to verify someone.
Never share the owner's full phone number or email with the caller; refer to them only by the last digits.

Escalate to the supervisor and stop

Escalate and make no change if: the caller cannot be verified or the details do not match; someone other than the owner keeps pushing for the change; the owner says they did not request the change or reports a bank change they did not make; the owner cannot be reached by phone or email after two attempts over 24 hours.
An owner reporting an unauthorised bank change is urgent: escalate straight away and do not try to fix it yourself.

Logging

On the ticket record: who you verified; how (the source of the callback number and the email reply); what you changed, with the old and new last four digits of the phone number; when the owner confirmed the change. Tag the ticket "Adit Pay MFA" (tag name to be confirmed).

Suggested reply to the practice

Hi [Name], for security, changes to your payout account and ownership details need a verification code sent to the owner's mobile number on file. Since the code cannot be received on that number, we will first verify the owner and then update the number for you. We have sent an email to the owner's email address on file. Once [Owner Name] replies to confirm, we will update the number and they can complete the change with a new code.
