Adit app (app.adit.com) walkthrough notes, Nov 16 2026 (test location "Memorial")

Navigation map (left sidebar, top to bottom)

Adit logo: Dashboard (tabs Schedule, My Tasks, Adit Insider). Schedule shows operatory columns (Operatory 1-4), Day/Week/Month, Today, date picker.
Practice Analytics: Morning Huddle, Follow Ups, Patient Lists, Dashboards (expand), Schedule (expand), Reports (expand), Preferences (expand)
Engage (texting): Messages, Auto-Reminders (expand), Mass Texting, ASAP Lists, Reports (expand), Canned Messages, Preferences (expand). On open, a 10DLC prompt: "Please register for 10DLC to enable text messaging ... To ensure compliance with FCC regulations and to avoid additional federal violation fines and charges related to 10DLC, it is recommended to register your business information." Button Register Now, link "Why and How to Register Business for 10DLC".
Tasks: My Tasks, Projects
Patient Forms: Submissions, Forms Manager, Insights, Preferences
Online Scheduling: Appointments, Scheduling, Providers, Services, Insurances, Black List, Booking Links, Webforms, Preferences
Adit Pay: Payments, Payment Plans, Subscriptions, Disputes, Payouts, Insights, Payment Reminders, Preferences (expand)
RCM (shield icon): opens /rcm/setup/get-started (Insurance verification, claims etc.)
Call Tracking: Calls, Voicemails, Fax, Leads, Insights, Legacy Insights, Numbers, Call Intelligence
CRM: Patients (Patients List, Birthday, Overdue Patients, Collection), Leads, Contacts, Unrecognized, Segments
Treatment Plans: Tx Plans, Templates, Treatments, Preferences
Financing: Transactions, Applications, Preferences (CareCredit)
Email Campaigns: Campaigns, Automation, Mailing Lists, Templates, Preferences
Reports: Reports (Google Ads etc.)
TeleMed: Virtual Operatories ("No Patients in Session Right Now. Please Click the Link Below to Generate a Link." button Start Session)
Top bar: location switcher (pin + location name), global Search, softphone (phone icon; first load asks "Would you like to enable softphone?" toggle + Save + "Don't show this again"), + (Create Request launcher), notifications bell, internal chat, help (?), settings gear (/setting), avatar.

+ Create Request launcher (top bar plus, also "Create Payment Request")

Icons row: Review Request, Appointment Request, Form Request, Appointment Reminder, Recall Reminder, Payment Request; second row: Text Message, Send Fax, Financing Request, Create Task.
Payment Request form: Location, Patient First Name, Patient Last Name, Cell Phone* (required), Email, amount, "Include Convenience fee" checkbox (info: "Convenience Fees allows your office to offset the cost of accepting card payments by automatically passing Adit's fees to your customers. These convenience fees would only apply to credit and debit cards."), shows Convenience Fee and Final Amount with CNP (card not present) values, Next.

Adit Pay > Payments

Buttons: Create Payment Request, Process Insurance Payment, search "Search by Name, Email, ID", date filter, Filters, Table Settings, more (...).
Tabs: All, Paid, Unpaid, Refunded, Voided, Expired, Merged.
Columns: Patient Name, Requested Date, Guardian, Patient ID, Service Amount, Paid Date, Conv. Fee, Processing Fees, Plan Type, Plan Name, Method, Paid Amount, Refund Date, Status, Actions.
Statuses seen: Paid, Refunded, Partially Refunded, Expired. Unpaid row actions: Cancel Request (x), Merge Request, Resend Payment Request.
Example: $102.00 request showed Conv. Fee $3.36 (card-not-present 2.9% + $0.30 grossed up).
Process Insurance Payment form: Location, Patient Name, amount, Next. Note on form: "Please note, this payment will not post in the patient's ledger".
Payment detail (click patient name): left list of requests; right: Request ID (copy), Amount + status, Patient Name, Requested Amount, Guardian, Request Date, Phone, Request Type (e.g. Offline Payment), Email; transactions table: Type (Paid/Refund), Payment Method (Check, card...), Amount, Refunded Amount, Processing Fees, Discounts, Net, Transaction Date, Remarks (e.g. "Requested by Customer"), Actions; Timeline (created by user, Payment Succeeded via Offline Payment with check, Refund Processed by user); Payment Details summary (Amount, Discount, Net, Refund, Status).

Adit Pay > Payment Plans (/adit-pay/payment-plan)

Buttons: Create Plan, search "Search by Name, Plan, ID", Table Settings, more (...). Tabs with counts: All, Draft, Sent, Active, Payment Issue, Inactive, Completed, Archived.
Columns: Plan Name, Patient Name, Patient ID, Plan Amount, Frequency, Actions (copy link icon for sent plans, edit pencil, archive). Orange info icon next to some plan names. In-app tip: "How to Create a Payment Plan".
Create Plan wizard (3 steps): 1 Select Patient (Patient Name*, Payment Plan Name* max 60 chars, Description max 150 chars) > 2 Plan Details (Total Amount*, Frequency* = Daily / Weekly / Bi-Weekly / Monthly / Yearly, Start Date*, Deposit (Optional), # of payments* and Recurring Payment* linked by a swap toggle so you enter one and the other calculates; Schedule table: Instl. #, Pay Date, Recurring Amount, Remaining Balance; deposit is charged on the activation day) > 3 Payment Details ("How would you like to set up payment?": Manually Add Payment Method, or Request Payment Method from Patient; Payment Options on File with Add; plan summary) > Activate. Buttons Cancel / Back / Next.
Example: $100 plan, $50 deposit, 5 monthly payments of $10.

Adit Pay > Subscriptions (/adit-pay/subscription)

Same layout as Payment Plans: Create Plan, search, Table Settings; tabs All, Draft, Sent, Active, Payment Issue, Inactive, Completed, Archived. In-app tip "How to Create a Subscription".

Adit Pay > Disputes (/adit-pay/disputes)

Search by Patient Name; tabs Needs Response, In Review, All, Won, Lost. Tip "How to Manage Disputes".

Adit Pay > Payouts (/adit-pay/payouts)

Buttons: Pay Out To Bank, Search by Amount, date range (default last ~30 days), Filters, Table Settings. Tabs Payouts and Balance.
Cards: In Transit to Bank, Future Payout, Total Balance, Total Lifetime Payout (each has an info icon).
Pay Out To Bank dialog ("Your Bank Account"): From Adit Balance, $ available, amount, Send to (bank name ****last4), Delivery Method (Standard, Instant), Confirm Payout checkbox "Pay out $X USD available from Adit balance to BANK ****1234", Cancel / Pay Out. (Manual payout of the available balance.)
Balance tab: balance transactions ("No balance data found" when empty). Tip "How to Review Payouts".

Adit Pay > Insights (/adit-pay/insights)

Date range filter. KPI cards with % change vs previous month: Amount Processed, Conv. Fees, Processing Fees, Net Processed, Total Unpaid Balance (Open Reqs count), Refunds (Refund Count).
Gauges: Paid in Office, Paid Online, Paid via Card, Paid via ACH, Paid via Cash, Paid via Check; Paid via One-Time Payments / Payment Plans / Subscriptions; Disputes.
Charts by day: Paid (Card, ACH, Cash, Check), Requested vs Paid, Refunds.

Adit Pay > Payment Reminders (/adit-pay/payment-reminder)

List of reminders: name, "Text and Email to Patients with request due", "N Days After at time", "*Send both text and email", message preview, edit, delete, on/off toggle. +Add.
Add Payment Reminder: Create From Scratch (Create) or suggested templates: Payment Reminder 3 Days After (2:00 PM), 14 Days After (10:00 AM), 30 Days After (10:00 AM), each "Hello $PatientFirstName$, this is $OrganizationName$. Our records show an outstanding balance of $PaymentAmount$ on your account. Please pay securely here: $PaymentLink$. Thank you!"
Editor fields: Reminder Name, Send Via (Text always on, Email optional), When to Send After Request Date (number + Days/Weeks), Time of Day slider, Spanish Auto-Reminders? toggle, Edit Text Message (character counter) with variables Patient First Name, Patient Preferred Name, Patient Last Name, Location Name, Organization Name, Payment Amount, Payment Link, DBA Name. Note "*Messages take up to a few minutes to send." Save.

Adit Pay > Preferences > Payment Request (/adit-pay/preferences/{loc}/payment-request)

Payment Request Logo (Choose file; recommend > 300x300 px, jpg or png).
Payment Request Text and Payment Confirmation Text editors (each with Save and variable chips; confirmation adds Transaction ID). Live SMS previews of the request and the receipt.
Pass Through Adit Fees to Patient? (Convenience Fees): "Pass Through Adit fees for One-Time Payments?" Yes/No. Note: "Adit will charge a convenience fee of 2.25% + $0.20 for card present transactions and 2.9% + $0.30 for card not present transactions." "This does not apply to recurring transactions such as payment plans or subscriptions as per Visa/Mastercard/Merchant rules." Link: How Are Convenience Fees Different From Surcharges, and Why Is This NOT a Surcharge?
Payment and Subscription Plans: "Allow Bank/ACH Payments" Yes/No. Note: "Adit charges a transaction fee of 0.5% + $1.75 per ACH transaction." Link: What Is ACH, and How Does It Work in Adit Pay?
Privacy Policy and Terms On/Off.
Payment Plan Request Text and Subscription Plan Request Text editors (default: "Hello $PatientFirstName$, this is <practice>. Please use this secure link to activate your payment plan by adding your card on file: $PaymentLink$ Thank You!").
Payment Page Settings: Enable Direct Payments Yes/No with Payment Page URL (p.adit.com/xxxx, Copy) so patients can pay without a request; Enable Financing Options Yes/No; Theme Color (hex) with PAY NOW preview; Partial Payments Yes/No; Expiration Time for a Payment Request Link: 1, 2, 3, 30, 60, 90, 120 Days (default seen 3 Days).

Adit Pay > Preferences > Business Details (/adit-pay/preferences/{loc}/payment-gateway)

Header: Business Legal Name, Status (Completed), Payments (Enabled), Payouts (Weekly), Total Balance.
Tabs: Identity (Business Type e.g. Individual/Company, Business Legal Name, Phone, Business Address, Doing Business As Name "*This name will show on the patient's statement or receipt.", Industry e.g. Dentists or Orthodontists, Website, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Save; then Management And Ownership: owner name, Date of Birth, SSN Provided, Identity Document Verified (front and back), email, phone, address).
Bank Accounts: Add Card, Add Account; table Bank/Card (Default badge), Account/Card Holder Name, Account/Card Number (masked ****1234 / routing), Currency, Primary, Actions.
Payout Schedule: "Set a schedule to automatically receive payouts, or send manual payouts via API or Dashboard" options: Automatic every [Day/Week/Month] on [weekday or date], or Manual ("You'll no longer be able to see which transactions are bundled in a payout").
Email Notifications: Payout Email Notification (emails, comma separated; Payout Notifications Yes/No) and Payment Details Email Notification (emails; Yes/No).

Adit Pay > Preferences > Terminals (/adit-pay/preferences/{loc}/terminal)

Buttons: Shop Now, Orders, Add Terminal. Table: Terminal image, Label, Serial Number (e.g. WSC5...), Status (Online/Offline), Actions (edit pencil). Tip "How to Purchase a Terminal".
Register Terminal dialog: "Adit supports the S700 and BBPOS WisePOS E card readers." Registration code, Terminal label, link How to Register a Terminal, Cancel/Add.
Shop Now (test account view): BBPOS WisePOS E $187.00 and BBPOS WisePOS E Dock $49.00 with quantity steppers; "Stripe Terminal hardware can only be shipped to locations in the continental U.S. and Hawaii." Payment types Magstripe, Chip (EMV), Contactless (EMV + NFC), Apple Pay, Google Pay; connectivity Ethernet, WiFi. NOTE: the team docs say WisePOS E sales stopped Nov 21, 2025 and S700/S710 are sold now; the test location still lists WisePOS E. Confirm with Sandy which the live shop shows.
Orders: cards with item image, qty, Status (Draft / placed), Order Placed date, Total, Order # (thor_...), View Draft.

Engage (texting) (/engage/...)

Submenu: Messages; Auto-Reminders (Birthday Wishes, Scheduled Confirmations, Appointment Reminders, Recall Reminders, Payment Reminders, Missed Call Text, Form Reminders, Auto-Assign Forms); Mass Texting; ASAP Lists; Reports (Auto Recall, Appointment Insights); Canned Messages; Preferences (Messaging = Quick Text, Opt in/Out Settings).
10DLC prompt appears on Engage when the practice has not registered: register business info for 10DLC (FCC compliance, avoids carrier fines); button Register Now.

Messages (inbox)

Left panel: + New; Shortcut (Home, Mentions, Search, Tags); Patients (unread count, toggle + filter); External SMS (unknown numbers shown as #number); Internal Direct (staff 1:1 chat); Internal Group. Middle: conversation list with Home / Unread tabs and Filters (Locations, Patients Active/Inactive, Task Status, Task Due Date, Message Date, Internal Message Date).
Conversation: header with patient name and number, search, tags; message bubbles show "Sent by <user>" or "Sent by Adit" for automated; red ! on failed message. Composer: + (quick actions: Create Request, reminders etc.), paperclip (attach), # (canned messages), emoji, "Type a message...", Schedule Send (arrow next to send), Send.
Patient side panel (Patient 360): name, ID, location, gender, status; action icons (call, text, appointment, email, +, ...); Last Visit, Next Visit; tabs Activity, Patient info, Medical; cards: Pending Forms + Assign Form, Hygiene status, EHR Balance Due, Adit Pay Balance, Remaining Benefits, Unsched Production, Unsched Family Members; Family File; Open Task (View All, Add); Open Appt Requests (Add); Patient Notes; Timeline of every event (Payment Request Sent, Payment Received via Offline Payment, Payment Plan Sent, Subscription Sent, Treatment Plan Sent, Outgoing Text Correspondence).

Auto-Reminders

Birthday Wishes: one message "Text at 10:30 AM on Birthday", edit + on/off.
Scheduled Confirmations: sent "immediately after an appointment is booked" via Text or Text and Voice; can attach Assigned Forms; +Add, edit, delete, toggle. Variables include $AppointmentDate$, $AppointmentTime$, $DirectionURL$.
Appointment Reminders: General Settings: Auto Confirmations Yes/No (if patient replies C, "C", C., Confirm, Confirmed, I'll be there, I will be there, Adit confirms the appointment in the EHR based on Status Mapping); Auto-Confirm Response Yes/No; Mark As Read Yes/No. Reminders list (e.g. "Text to Unconfirmed Patients 1 day(s) Before at 09:00 AM").
Add Appointment Reminder: Create From Scratch or templates (1 Week / 3 Days / 1 Day Before for Unconfirmed; 1 Week / 1 Day Before for Confirmed; Text, Email and Voice). Editor: Reminder Name*, Send Via* (Text, Email, Voice), Send Reminder To* (patient status: Confirmed, Unconfirmed, Attempted to Contact, In Office, Broken), Advanced Filters (+ Appt Type, + Provider, + Operatory), When to Send (Before/After Appt, Hours/Days/Weeks), Time of Day, Family Reminders?, message with variables (Patient First/Preferred/Last Name, Location Name, Organization Name, Appointment Date, Appointment Time, 15 Min Early Appt Time, Direction URL, Patient Form URL), Preview, Spanish Auto-Reminders?, Save. "*Messages take up to a few minutes to send."
Recall Reminders: "Recall Types to Use" toggles pulled from the EHR (e.g. Prophy, Child Prophy, Perio, 4BW, Pano, 2BW); reminders "Text / 1 Week(s) Before Due Date / 09:00 AM" or After Due Date; Text or Text and Voice. Note: Recall Types are taken into account when automated notifications are sent.
Payment Reminders: same as Adit Pay > Payment Reminders.
Missed Call Text: rules "Text when call is missed in all hours / after business hours / during business hours" with Selected Numbers; templates All Hours, After Business Hours, During Business Hours.
Form Reminders (Incomplete Form Reminders): Text and/or Email N day(s)/hour(s) Before the appointment with $PatientFormURL$.
Auto-Assign Forms: table Assignment Name, When to Send (e.g. Text and Email 1 Day Before Appt), Forms Assigned with frequency (Only Once, At Every Qualified Appt, Every 6 Months, Every 12 Months), Text Message Preview, Actions.

Mass Texting (/engage/textcampaign)

Add Campaign; search; date range; tabs All, Draft, Scheduled, In Progress, Sent, Cancelled. Columns Campaign Name, Location, Audience, Last Modified, Status, Sent/Scheduled Date & Time, Action (edit/delete for draft/scheduled, view for sent).
Create Campaign: Location, Campaign Name (100 chars), Begin. Campaign detail: Edit Campaign, Add Recipients, Cancel / Schedule / Send Now; Edit Message (320 chars) with variables First Name, Last Name, Preferred Name, Location Name, Organization Name, Pozative Link, Custom Appt Request, Patient Form Request, Last Appt Time/Date, Next Appt Time/Date; Preview. Note: if the message has a dynamic placeholder, each patient gets a message (families sharing a phone number may get multiple); remove dynamic fields to send one per number. Add Recipients: Select a Segment (Patient dropdown, segment list), Add Segment, Add Patient List; Back / Save.

ASAP Lists (/engage/asap)

Patients who want an earlier appointment: Patient Name, Mobile, Schedule Time, Provider, Length, Source, Note, Actions (edit, delete), Communications (call, text, email). Filter, search.

Canned Messages

"To load a pre-made response, use any of the shortcuts below while typing your message." Each has text and #shortcuts; edit, copy, delete; +Add; search shortcuts.

Reports

Auto Recall (/engage/reports/recall-report): tabs All, Unscheduled, Overdue and Unscheduled, Scheduled, Completed; month picker; search by name or recall type; Recall Reminders charts.
Appointment Insights (/engage/reports/appointment-log): date picker; Time, Patient Name, Operatory, Status, Confirmed Status (e.g. Appointment confirmed), Appt Log (info icon shows the reminder/confirmation log).

Preferences

Messaging = Quick Text: Appointment Date Format (e.g. MM-DD-YYYY), Appt Reminder quick text and Recall Reminder quick text with variables.
Opt in/Out Settings: Opt In Confirmation and Opt Out Confirmation texts ($OptOutURL$, "Text STOP at any time to unsubscribe"); Automatically Send Opt Out Confirmation toggle with "Send Confirmation When Patient Updates Communication Preferences" and "...When Practice Updates..."; Opt Out Preferences Updated text. Tip "How to Opt Out a Patient".

Tasks (/task/my-task, /task/projects)

My Tasks: Add Task, Add Section, Search Tasks, Filters, Table Settings. Grouped sections Recently assigned, Due today, Due later (+ Add section). Columns #, Task Name, Due date, Patient Name, Collaborators, Created By, Projects. Inline "Add task..." rows. Tips: Understanding Tasks, Creating Tasks in Adit, Understanding Task Permissions.
Projects: Add Project, Search Project; Project Name with color icon and Members avatars (e.g. Hygiene Recare, Incomplete Form, Observation Followup, Unconfirmed Appointment, Unscheduled appts, Unscheduled Broken Appointment). Tip: How to Manage Projects.
Create Task is also in the + launcher.

Patient Forms (/patient-form/...)

Submenu: Submissions, Forms Manager, Insights, Preferences (Forms, Auto-Assign).
Submissions: Create Form Request, search, date range, Table Settings, more. Tabs All, Requested, Submitted, Imported, Archived. Columns Patient Name, Form Name, Location, Status (Approve button on submitted forms), Date Requested, Date Submitted, Method (Text, Email), Actions (download, archive). Orange info icon next to some form names.
Create Form Request (launcher Form Request): Location, First Name*, Last Name*, Cell phone, Email, delivery (Send Both Text & Email / text / email), "*Phone OR Email is required", Next (then pick forms).
Forms Manager: Create Form, search, Filters; tabs Active, Inactive, Library. Columns Form Name, Fields mapped to EHR (e.g. 19 Fields, None), Location, Active? toggle, Auto import?, Automation (Edit Settings), Folder Selected (e.g. Patient Information, Medical History, Insurance), Download Format (Compressed Layout, Table Layout, Large Print Layout), Pre-filled fields?, Actions (edit, download, view, copy link). Tip "How to Build a Form".
Library: Adit templates with Preview and Use Template (Adult/Child Health History, Spanish versions, many Consent forms: Alveoplasty, Bone Graft, Bridge, Crown, Composite Fillings, Dentures, Extraction, Fluoride Varnish - Pediatric, etc.).
Form Builder (edit-form): Back, View Form, Copy Form Link, Save, Save & Exit. Left palette: Basic Fields (Single Line (Text), Add Title/Content, Phone, Email, Date, Time, Percent, Currency, URL, Dropdown, Multiple Choice, Checkboxes, Short Answer, Paragraph, Add Image, Upload, Signature); Custom Fields (Allergies, Problems, Medications); Other (Add Section, Add Page, Import Questions). Top settings: Location, Show Logo? (display practice logo), Form Active (make form active), Multi Sign? ("allow additional signatures from the practice before approving/importing the form"), Auto Import?, e-sign certified? ("Request patient to provide name, email, and phone when they go to sign"), Folder (EHR folder where the form imports), Form Name, Download (PDF layout: compressed, table, or print-friendly), Category (organize forms). Right panel: Form Name (75 chars) and Description. Green cloud icon on a field = synced to an EHR field (tooltip e.g. "Syncs with First Name"). Builder tour: 1 select location, 2 click title to name form, 3 right panel holds form properties, 4 drag fields from left panel, 5 update Field Settings and map PMS fields, 6 drag and drop fields or rows to reorder.
Insights: date range; Submissions chart; Conversion Data (Invite Sent, # of Patients Who Completed The Form); Device Access (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile, Other); Patient Form Log (Patient Name, Email, Phone, Time Sent, Clicked Link?).
Preferences > Forms: General Information (Practice Name, Form Logo, Reply To Email for patient replies to form-request emails, Receptionist Email that receives copies of completed forms); Mobile Device Settings (Device's Splash Screen 768x1024+, Devices Connected list with Disconnect = the Adit forms tablet/kiosk apps, Theme Color); Allow Self Sign ("After a patient signs the form, if a staff signature is required, populate the form again on the mobile device for the staff to sign?"); Default Text Settings (Form Request Template, Resend Submission Template, 2nd Sign Request Template, each with a p.adit.com link); Form Settings (Form Categories Edit; Email a Copy of Submitted Form to Patient Yes/No; Thank You Page redirect; Signers Allowed = team members who can sign for the office).
Preferences > Auto-Assign: same as Engage > Auto-Assign Forms.

Submenu: Appointments, Scheduling, Providers, Services, Insurances, Black List, Booking Links, Webforms, Preferences.
