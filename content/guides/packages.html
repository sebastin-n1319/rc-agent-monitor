Package structure and package change process (Nov 2026)

Internal reference for T1 CS and the Adit Pay team. Source: "Adit: New Package Change Process & Package Structure in CRM" (Nov 16 2026) and the New Package Structure and Change Process walkthrough. Never quote prices, discounts, rack rates or approvers to a practice.

1. The short version

Every customer's package lives in the CRM in two places that must agree: the Deal says what the customer has and what they pay a month, and the Billing record lists each product with its own price.
The one rule: the deal's Monthly Price equals the sum of the Sold Prices of its Active products on the Billing record.
The CRM is the source of truth. Zoho Books bills from it, and Aria checks every live deal against both.
Why it changed: it was hard to tell from the CRM which products a customer is on, hard to see how the monthly price splits across products, and package changes across standard and add-on products were causing errors.

2. On the Deal: what the customer has (read Overall Package first)

Overall Package (CRM field Package_Text): every product the customer has, add-ons included, joined with "+". Example: Advanced SEO + PMP + Core + Voice + Analytics + AI Front Desk.
Marketing Package (Marketing_Package): Advanced SEO, or Advanced SEO + PMP (PMP was called PBG and needs Advanced SEO).
Tech Packages (Tech_Packages): Core, Voice, Analytics, plus at most one RCM level (Soft Verifications, Soft & Manual Verifications, or Full Billing).
AI Add-Ons (AI_Add_Ons): AI Front Desk or AI Front Desk AH (never both), Call Intelligence, AI Webchat.
Monthly Price: what the customer pays a month for all products together; equals the Billing record's Active total.
The old Package field is now called Primary Package. It holds only the base bundle, never add-ons, and is kept for older deals. It does not show everything a customer has; always read Overall Package first.

3. Old package names and their new equivalent

Tech Bundle = Core + Voice.
Analytics Bundle = Core + Voice + Analytics.
Core + Analytics = Core + Analytics.
PBG = PMP.

4. What each package includes (which modules a practice should see)

Every package switches on the same five basics: CRM, Adit Pay, Financing, Tasks and Internal Chat. On top of those:
Adit Core (primary or add-on): SMS and Mass Texting, Auto Reminders, Patient Forms, Tx Plans, Online Scheduling, Pozative, Automations, Email Campaigns, TeleMed.
Adit Voice (primary or add-on): SMS and Mass Texting, Call Tracking.
Advanced Analytics (primary) and Practice Analytics (add-on): Practice Analytics.
Advanced SEO (primary or add-on) and PMP (add-on): Call Tracking, Online Scheduling, Reports.
Soft Verifications, Soft + Manual Verifications, Full Billing RCM (add-ons): that RCM level.
AI Front Desk (add-on): AI Front Desk 24/7, SMS and Mass Texting, Auto Reminders, Call Tracking, Online Scheduling.
AI Front Desk Afterhours (add-on): AI Front Desk After Hours, Call Tracking, Online Scheduling.
AI Webchat (add-on): AI Webchat, SMS and Mass Texting, Online Scheduling.
Call Intelligence (add-on): Call Intelligence, Call Tracking.
AI Recare (add-on): AI Recare, SMS and Mass Texting, Call Tracking, Online Scheduling.
AI Care Coordinator (add-on): AI Care Coordinator, SMS and Mass Texting, Call Tracking, Online Scheduling.
AI Scribe (add-on): AI Scribe, Tx Plans, Online Scheduling.

When a customer has several packages

They get every module any of their packages includes; a shared module (such as Call Tracking in Voice and AI Front Desk) is simply on.
Adit Voice + AI Front Desk: the five basics, SMS and Mass Texting, Auto Reminders, Call Tracking, Online Scheduling, AI Front Desk 24/7. They do NOT get Patient Forms, Tx Plans, Pozative, Automations, Email Campaigns, TeleMed or Practice Analytics, because none of their packages includes them.
Adit Core + Adit Voice + Practice Analytics: everything in Core and Voice plus Practice Analytics.
Adit Core + Adit Voice + Soft Verifications + Call Intelligence: everything in Core and Voice, plus Soft Verifications and Call Intelligence.
Advanced SEO + PMP (marketing only): the five basics, Call Tracking, Online Scheduling, Reports.
So when a practice asks why they cannot see a feature, check Overall Package against this list first. If their packages do not include the module, it is a package question for the account's CSM, not a bug.

Packages that need another package first

AI Front Desk (24/7 and After Hours) and Call Intelligence need Adit Voice.
RCM (Soft Verifications, Soft + Manual Verifications, Full Billing RCM) and Practice Analytics need Adit Core. Exception: an Analytics-only customer, whose primary package is Advanced Analytics.
PMP needs Advanced SEO.

5. On the Billing record: each product and its price

Every deal has one Billing record (in the deal's related list). Its Package Details table has one row per product: Product; Primary / Add-On; Sold Price (what the customer pays a month for it after any discount, the number that counts); Rack Rate (the list price, kept even when the Sold Price is lower or zero); Discount; Payment Cycle (Monthly or Annually; the Sold Price is still the monthly amount); Start Date / End Date; Recurring Invoice Id (the Books profile that bills it); Status (Active = has it now; Inactive = removed, kept for history and not counted).
Upgrade: a new Active row; Monthly Price goes up by its Sold Price; the product is added to Overall Package and its type field.
Downgrade: the row is set to Inactive with an End Date, never deleted; Monthly Price goes down; the product is removed from the package fields.
Price change on the same products: the Sold Price changes, Rack Rate stays.
Free customer: every Sold Price is zero, package fields are still filled in.
Billed annually: Sold Price is still the monthly amount, Payment Cycle says Annually, Books invoices 12 times it once a year.
Several locations on one Books invoice: each location's deal keeps its own rows, price and products.

6. Requesting a package change (CSM and sales process)

Every upgrade, downgrade or price change is raised in the Package Change portal: in the CRM, Request Package Change in the left menu. Package changes are no longer created directly in the CRM. The portal writes the Package Change and Package Details records, so Monthly Price and the Billing record stay in step.
Steps: New request, find the deal (one deal per location); change the stack (Add, pencil to change a price, bin to remove; lines in another open request are locked); Review and submit; give each line its effective date, payment cycle and reason; Submit request, which returns a PCB reference.
Approval is needed only when a line is priced below its rack rate; nothing else triggers it. Lines at rack rate go to the CRM straight away; only the discounted lines wait. Two approvals in order: the team that owns that kind of package, then Finance. From the walkthrough: Getting Started or Closed On deals go to Fahad then Finance; Onboarding to Tanya; Marketing to Josh; CSM to Kushal; AI pricing to Anthony; RCM to Francis. The Package Change record (PKG) is created after the required approvals.
My requests lists every request and its status (Waiting on approval, Waiting on Finance, Approved, Rejected by approver, Rejected by Finance). Approvals lists requests waiting on you; a remark is required.
Questions about the process: the portal's help section, or Sebastin, Hayden or Anuj.

What this means for T1 CS and the Adit Pay team

Neither T1 CS nor the Adit Pay team raises package changes. An upgrade, downgrade, cancellation or pricing request goes to the account's CSM (or the escalation manager), with an internal note of what the practice asked for.
Never quote prices, discounts, rack rates, approvals or approvers to the practice, and never promise a package change or a date.
A missing module is first checked against Overall Package and the module list above before it is treated as a bug.

7. Auditing: how Aria checks it

Aria, Reports, Audits has two self-updating reports. Audit: CRM Monthly Price <> Package Details lists live deals whose Monthly Price is not the sum of Active Sold Prices; the fix is in the CRM; refreshes about every 10 minutes. Audit: Books <> CRM Amount Diff lists live deals where Books bills a different monthly amount than the CRM; the CRM is right and each row says what to change in Books; refreshes after every Books sync. Both have Open and Resolved tabs; Resolve needs a reason, and a row returns to Open if its amounts change.
