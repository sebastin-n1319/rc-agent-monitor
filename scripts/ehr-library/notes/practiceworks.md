## PracticeWorks (Carestream Dental family, on-premise)
- On-premise dental practice management software from the Carestream Dental family (same family as SoftDent and OrthoTrac). Public help: https://help.carestreamdental.com/
- Main areas: Appointment Book (scheduling, block bookings for hours, vacations, production), Recall Management (recall contacts, cards, letters, schedule), Accounts Receivable, Contact Experts (work lists for pending items, confirmations, recalls, delinquent accounts, unscheduled treatment), Documents (letters), Statements, Treatment Plans, Clipboards (patient relationships), Insurance (claims, estimates), Reports. Source: https://help.carestreamdental.com/rh/web/server/PracticeWorks/projects_responsive/OH_DE210/PW_TrainingTopics_Checklist_Form.pdf
