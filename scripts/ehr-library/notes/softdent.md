## SoftDent (CS SoftDent, Carestream Dental, on-premise)
- On-premise dental practice management software from the Carestream Dental family (same family as PracticeWorks and OrthoTrac). Public help: https://help.carestreamdental.com/
- Morning reports: Reports > 3. Confirmation List (confirmation status for a date); Reports > Recall/Appt > Short Call List (patients willing to come earlier, tickler file patients); Reports > 2. Missed Appointments (lists yesterday's missed appointments and MOVES them from the Daily Appointments screen to the tickler file, and posted appointments to history). So a missed appointment disappears from the schedule once this report runs; integrations then see it as moved, not as on the schedule. Source: https://help.carestreamdental.com/rh/web/server/SoftDent/projects_responsive/OH_DE1010/Job_Aids/Daily_Reports_Morning_JA.pdf
