## OrthoTrac (Carestream Dental family, orthodontic, on-premise)
- Orthodontic practice management software from the Carestream Dental family. Public help: https://help.carestreamdental.com/rh/web/server/OrthoTrac/projects_responsive/OH_OR215/SysOps.htm
- System Options that change how names look: Automatic Capitalization (formats common words and abbreviations in upper and lower case), Show First Name As (first or common name in the chart, financial documents and forms), Combine Middle & Last Name. If a practice says names change case after syncing, check Automatic Capitalization in System Options. Source: same page.
- Recalls: scheduled only from the patient chart (Patient chart > Functions > Schedule Appointment > Recalls standard > Add Recall), or created automatically when the No-Show report runs. Recall appointments do NOT appear on the appointment book; they work as a tickler file. Reports > Schedule > Recall List lists patients with recalls in a month. Source: https://help.carestreamdental.com/rh/web/server/OrthoTrac/projects_responsive/OH_OR215/SchRecal.htm
