## OfficeMate (Eyefinity, on-premise; also used with ExamWRITER)
- On-premise optometry practice management software from Eyefinity (VSP). Public help: https://help.eyefinity.com/omew/OfficeMate/OfficeMateOverview.htm (screens and steps with pictures).
- Patient demographics: Patient Demographic window > Demographic tab (or Patient Center > Demographics). Name in the yellow boxes under Record Contact Information (P button records a previous name); phone with a Texting OK check box; email; Communication Pref. drop-down; Active check box (clear it for inactive patients); provider; Preferred Language drop-down (languages added in Customization > Preferred Languages). No automatic name capitalization is described. Copy Last Name to Patient copies the guarantor's last name. Source: https://help.eyefinity.com/omew/OfficeMate/Recording_Demo_Info.htm
- Scheduler setup: Tools > Scheduler Preferences; Tools > Office Hours (closed days, open from/to per location); Tools > Services (appointment types, durations in Customization); Tools > Providers And Resources > resource > Hours (Normal Hours or date range; Fill Availability, Add/Delete Availability); Hours > Resource Template Setup > Add Template (service templates); File > New Schedule Exception (available or not available blocks, recurring up to two years); Tools > Appointment Icons. Resources must be Available and Active; Allows Overbooks and Maximum Overbooks per period; Minute Increments per resource. If online scheduling shows no slots, check resource hours, templates and exceptions here. Source: https://help.eyefinity.com/omew/OfficeMate/Setting_Up_the_Appointme.htm
