## Dolphin Management (Dolphin Imaging, Patterson, orthodontic)
- Orthodontic practice management on Microsoft .NET with a SQL Server database; on-premise or hosted on the Dolphin Cloud Subscription (no office server). Scheduler with 2 to 60 minute increments, chairs, locations and day templates, a Sooner if Possible waitlist; patient records with treatment card, tooth chart, document organizer and relationship manager; ledger with contracts, payment plans and family statements; questionnaires and online new-patient forms. Has its own reminders (SMS, email, phone, preferred method per patient) and two-way SMS. Source: https://www.dolphinimaging.com/Areas/Product/Documents/Management/ManagementBrochure.pdf
- Adit's field-mapping notes for Dolphin: middle name, address 2, marital status, secondary provider, allergies, problems, medications, driver's license, emergency contact and employer do not sync; insurance fields write only for new patients.
