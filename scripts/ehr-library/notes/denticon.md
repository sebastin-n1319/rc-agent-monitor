## Denticon (Planet DDS, cloud)
- Cloud dental practice management software from Planet DDS. Public support portal: https://support.planetdds.com/hc/en-us (Appointments, Scheduler, Ledger-Payments and Adjustments sections).
- Online scheduling availability comes from Denticon watermarks: each provider needs watermarks for every day, time and operatory that should be bookable online. Use full-day watermarks; an online booking cannot span two watermarks, so fragmented blocks hide slots. Also needed: provider Active = Yes and Visible in AppointNow = Yes with Works At, operatories, schedules, holidays and watermarks set (Setup > Providers > Provider Setup); office schedules and holidays (Setup > Offices > Office Setup); production type Active and AppointNow Visible (Setup > Misc Setups > Scheduler Production Type). Appointment length must fit the schedule increments. Holidays and lunch breaks block online booking. Source: https://educate.dentalintel.com/en/articles/13717565-set-up-denticon-for-dental-intelligence-online-scheduling
- Adit rulebook: Adit cannot update Denticon availabilities; users change watermarks directly in Denticon.
