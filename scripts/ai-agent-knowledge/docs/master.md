# Intro to Adit AI Agent  

## Intro to Adit AI Agent

  

Adit AI Agent is a full suite of AI solutions that can automate, enhance and streamline the operations of the practice. The AI Agent suite comprises 3 different agents which are independent of each other and can be deployed on its own to solve for a specific use case or together for maximum benefits.

  

The three agents are - 

  

AI Front Desk - Capable of handling all incoming communication to practice (calls and texts) 

AI Care Coordinator - Capable of planning and handling all relevant outgoing communication (calls and texts)

AI Web Chat - Capable of engaging and converting visitors to the practice’s website via an embedded chat widget

  

Every communication handled by the agent is fully documented, transcribed and analysed for easy consumption for the practice. Each complete communication handled by the agent is called an Interaction in Adit AI module. An Interaction can mean a full completed call or a thread of texts which constitutes a single completed conversation from a patient or a thread of web chats which constitutes a complete conversation. 

  

Every interaction will provide the following information - 

  - Summary
  - Transcript
  - Interaction Type
  - Outcome
  - Customer Sentiment
  - Reason for Visit
  - Reason for Not Booking
  - Revenue Potential
  - Patient Name
  - Patient Type
  - Follow Up Tasks

  
  
  

How it Works

  

As part of the getting started process, practices will provide their preference with regards to the agent identity (name, voice etc), agent behaviour (handle all types of calls or only handle scheduling and emergency and don’t handle rescheduling etc) agent knowledge (practice details, business hours, direction reach, parking availability etc), booking preferences (accepted insurances, allowed services, provider-service mapping, availability rules, booking buffer etc), task handling, call handover preferences etc. 

  

On finishing getting started, the agent gets instantly created with the preferences of the practice. This is a dedicated agent created just for that practice and it only handles the calls and texts of that practice. Practice will set up the call forwarding criteria in call flow (only send after-hour calls to the agent or send all calls to the agent etc) sms forwarding criteria in AI agent preferences (engage only after-hour SMS, engage all SMS etc)

  

Based on the preferences of the practice, respective calls and texts are redirected to the agent. The agent engages based on its context and handles the calls and texts completely or partially based on the agent behaviour settings. 

  

Preference can be updated anytime by the practice and the agent will get instantly updated based on the updated preferences.

  
  

Capabilities

  

AI Front Desk - AI Front Desk is capable of fully handling all sorts of scheduling calls (emergency, single-patient, multi-patient etc), rescheduling calls, insurance acceptance confirmations and appointment confirmation calls. The agent can partially handle the calls related to billing, product order tracking (braces, specs, lens etc), callbacks from patients and can create a follow up task for the practice or do a warm transfer as required. The agent is also capable of gate-keeping marketing and other soliciting calls from going to the practice and wasting their time. The same capabilities are applicable for texting as well.

  

AI Front Desk cannot do outbound calls and hence won’t be able to handle outbound calls for payment collection, recall due, outbound appointment confirmation etc.

  

AI Care Coordinator - Full capabilities of this agent will be finalized close to the release date, but this agent will be able to proactively engage patients with broken and unscheduled appointments, recall due patients, patients unscheduled for a long time, payment overdue, appointment confirmation etc. 

  

AI Web Chat - Full capabilities of this agent will be finalized close to the release date, but this agent will be able to answer any practice or booking related questions the web site visitors have along with trying to proactively outbound engage website visitors to try and convert them into patients.

  
  

Underlying Models

  

A variety of providers are used for STT (Speech to Text), LLM (Large Language Model) and TTS (Text to Speech) based on the context and use case with Deepgram for STT, GPT for LLM and Elevenlabs for TTS being the predominant providers

Adit AI Agent can interact in almost all major languages and handle multiple accents. For Non-English interactions, the transcript and threads will be shown in the original language, but the interaction summary and analysis will be in English

# Switch Factors  

# **AI Front Desk - Switch Factors**

  

1.  **\*\*The "Zero Missed Call" Standard\*\***

  

*How many potential new patients are you losing simply because your lines are busy or the office is closed? If a new patient calls and goes to voicemail, how likely are they to leave a message versus hanging up and calling the next practice on Google?*

  

With the AI Front Desk, your practice achieves a 100% answer rate instantly. Whether it is 2 PM during a lunch rush or 2 AM on a Sunday, every single caller is greeted immediately. The AI captures the intent, answers questions, or schedules the appointment on the spot, ensuring you never lose a patient to a competitor simply because no one picked up the phone.. 

  

1.  **\*\*After-Hours Revenue Capture\*\***

  

*Your doors close at 5 PM, but your patients' lives don’t. How many scheduling opportunities are trapped in your voicemail box the next morning? How much time does your staff spend playing "phone tag" trying to call those voicemails back the next day?*

  

The AI Front Desk turns your after-hours voicemail into an active booking engine. Instead of leaving a message, patients can schedule, reschedule, or confirm appointments directly with the AI. You wake up to a filled calendar rather than a list of callbacks, allowing your practice to generate revenue even when the lights are off.

  

1.  **\*\*Eliminating "Hold Time" Frustration\*\***

  

*When your front desk is overwhelmed with in-office patients, do they put callers on hold? How often do those callers hang up after waiting too long?*

  

The AI acts as an infinite overflow valve. When your front desk is tied up, the AI picks up the slack instantly. It handles the routine requests—scheduling hygiene, confirming times, answering FAQs—so your patients never experience a busy signal or hold music. If the issue requires a human touch, the AI can perform a warm transfer once your staff is free, or create a prioritized task for a callback.

  

1.  **\*\*Staff Productivity & Burnout Prevention\*\***

  

*Is your highly trained front desk staff bogged down by repetitive, low-value calls like "Are you open on Fridays?" or "I need to reschedule my cleaning"? Does this distract them from checking in patients or presenting treatment plans?*

  

By offloading the repetitive scheduling and confirmation tasks to the AI, you free your staff to operate at the "top of their license." They can focus on high-value interactions—like treatment presentation and in-person patient hospitality—while the AI handles the administrative noise. This reduces burnout and improves the in-office experience.

  
  

1.  **\*\*Accuracy of Message Taking\*\***

  

*How often do handwritten notes or hurried messages get lost or misinterpreted? Have you ever had a patient claim they called to cancel, but there is no record of it?*

  

The AI Front Desk eliminates human error in message taking. Every interaction is transcribed, categorized, and summarized automatically. If the AI cannot handle a specific complex request, it notes down the exact reason for the call and creates a specific task for your practice to follow up. This creates an unshakeable audit trail and ensures that "I forgot to tell the doctor" never happens again

  
  

1.  **\*\*The "Warm Hand-off" Experience\*\***

  

*Are you worried that an AI will feel robotic or disconnect the patient from your practice's personal touch?*

  

Our solution bridges the gap between automation and personal care. The AI is intelligent enough to recognize when a caller is distressed or has a complex clinical question. In these scenarios, it doesn't just block the caller; it initiates a "warm transfer," passing the caller to your staff along with a summary of the conversation so far. This ensures the patient feels heard, and your staff picks up the conversation exactly where the AI left off.

  
  

1.  **\*\*Operational Continuity (Sick Days & Staffing Shortages)\*\***

  

*What happens to your phone lines when your receptionist calls in sick or goes on vacation? Do you have to scramble to find coverage or let service levels drop?*

  

The AI Front Desk provides instant operational redundancy. It is the employee that never gets sick, never takes a vacation, and never has a bad day. During staffing shortages, you can dial up the AI's involvement to handle a larger volume of calls, ensuring your practice runs smoothly regardless of who is physically at the desk.

  

1.  **\*\*The Self-Improving Front Desk\*\***

  

*How do you ensure your AI doesn't make the same mistakes your humans might?*

  

While your staff’s performance is being monitored only sporadically for the calls coming to your practice(unless you have a Call Intelligence solution to continuously track and guide on performance improvement), the AI agent is being continuously monitored across 100s of practices and 1000s of calls daily by a sophisticated set of algorithms, other AI agents and a set of AI domain experts making daily improvements. While it is really good out-of-the-box, it just keeps getting better everyday.

## Why do we have overages for AI Agents?

  

1.  We use enterprise-grade, state-of-the-art models for each of the components that goes into building the agent — the same technology powering the most advanced AI systems in the world. These aren't basic, commodity services; they're premium models that deliver:

<!-- end list -->

  - Natural, human-like conversations
  - High accuracy in understanding patient needs
  - Fast, reliable responses every time

           

    Most competitors will be using lower tier models which saves cost but impact performance

  

1.  We waited for the technology to evolve so we could perfect it. We could have gone to the market much earlier with a product that cost much lower, but we made a conscious decision not to do that since we did not want to compromise on the quality. We know all the permutations and combinations we can do in order to reduce cost, but decided against that since it affected quality every single time. The product we have now is the result of more than 1.5 yrs of R\&D of a dedicated team focussed on just making the agent the best sounding, best performing product. 

  

1.  AI is resource intensive so if there is lack of consistent availability of processing resources, performance quality of the agent can fluctuate. In order to avoid that we maintain dedicated computing resources (not shared with other customers during peak times) for each agent (each customer). Most providers will not reserve computing resources as it is expensive

  

1.  We can keep a higher entry price for all customers and offer unlimited minutes, but once again 90% of practices will just end up paying extra for something they don’t need and will not use. So we chose to keep the entry price low so most customers can benefit and those with very high usage can still take advantage of a superior product at a modest overage

  
  

**The Bottom Line**:

  

The credits we provide for the plans we have covers typical practice usage generously. 90% of the practices will never cross the overage limit.

  

But if a practice receives exceptionally high call volumes, we need to cover the real costs of:

  - Premium AI model usage
  - Dedicated infrastructure to ensure your patients always get instant, reliable service

  

We keep our overage rates transparent and fair—you only pay for what you actually use beyond your plan, and every dollar goes toward maintaining the premium experience your patients expect.

If your usage is heavy and if it hits overages, it would mean that the agent has booked that many appointments for you and has handled a heavy volume of your patients.

# Getting Started \> Configure Agent  

## Getting Started \> Configure Agent

  

Configure Agent allows you to set up the basic identities for the agent like its name, voice and capabilities

  

  

**Agent Name** - This will be the name the agent uses when it is answering calls

  

**Practice Name** - This will be how the agent will communicate the name of the practice with the caller. The reason why it is kept as a setting here is because many times the practice name is updated as just the location/city name in location settings in Adit. Using the information directly from there might end up having the agent communicate the practice name as Houston instead of ABC Practice

  

**Agent Voice** - This will allow the practice to listen to a selection of voices to go with what they like. Agent Voice is not just about the voice but covers the entire dimension of communication like tone, timbre, cadence, rhythm, pitch, pacing, volume and accent. 

  

  
  

**Agent Capabilities** - This will allow the practice to decide what all things they want the agent to handle end to end. 

  

1.  Scheduling - Set to ‘Yes’ means the agent will collect all relevant information, look up relevant slots and will always try to book the appointment. There could be some scenarios where the agent is not able to book because slots are not available on the preferred day etc., but the agent’s instruction is to book an appointment if possible. Set to ‘No’ means the agent’s instruction is not to book an appointment by itself. It will collect relevant information from the caller and will create a task for the practice to follow up
2.  Rescheduling - Set to ‘Yes’ means the agent will always try to reschedule the appointment provided the appointment falls outside the rescheduling window. There could be some scenarios where the agent is not able to reschedule because slots are not available on the preferred day etc., but the agent’s instruction is to reschedule the appointment if possible. Set to ‘No’ means the agent’s instruction is not to reschedule an appointment by itself. It will collect relevant information from the caller and will create a task for the practice to follow up
3.  Cancellation - Set to ‘Yes’ means the agent will always try to reschedule the appointment for a cancellation call provided the appointment falls outside the rescheduling window and if not possible then cancel that appointment if it falls within the cancellation window. The agent’s instruction is to try and reschedule the appointment if possible or else cancel it. Set to ‘No’ means the agent’s instruction is not to cancel an appointment by itself. It will collect relevant information from the caller and will create a task for the practice to follow up

  

For all the other types of calls, the agent will collect relevant information from the caller by default and do a warm transfer to the practice or create a task as per the practice preferences

# Getting Started \> Booking Settings  

## Getting Started \> Booking Settings

  

Booking Settings is arguably the most important preference page in Ai Agent. This page allows practice to configure what services the agent should book, what payment options are accepted, minimum notice period for rescheduling and cancellations etc. 

  

  

**Payment Options** - In this section, practice can mention if they allow Medicaid, Medicare, Care Credit, Credit Cards, FSA and In-house financing. If they accept in-house financing, the practice will have the option to expand on the details as well so the agent can communicate the same clearly to patients who call and inquire about the same. There is one more free-form field in this section for practice to mention any other payment options they accept as well

  
  

  

**Accepted Insurances** - This section holds the insurance information picked from online scheduling. This cannot be directly edited from Ai Agent preference. If the edit button is clicked, it will open up Insurance settings in Online Scheduling. 

  

As of now the agent does not have the capability to verify if the insurance mentioned by the patient is part of the accepted list. Because of that there is no option for the agent to reject a patient from booking an appointment based on their insurance provider. The agent just collects the insurance info and proceeds to book as of now. If the caller wants clarity on if their insurance is accepted, then the agent will communicate that someone from the practice will call them back and will proceed to create a task for the practice to follow up. It will still proceed to book the appointment unless the caller is insistent that they don’t want to book unless they get a confirmation on their insurance acceptance.

  

The functionality to check insurance providers and to communicate if that is accepted is being built out and should be available by the end of March 2026.

  
  

  

**Services Offered** - This is the main section that needs to be carefully updated for the agent to function properly. The agent by default will not be able to book for all the services set up in the OS settings. More context has to be provided on this to the agent. It is being done this way for two reasons - 

  

1.  Practices will not want to book some types of appointments for new patients or for existing patients as well just based on a phone call. For eg. a person who is calling to do a root canal will not be set up as a root canal service. It is usually set up as a New Patient Exam where the doctor can do an initial exam and recommend next steps. In this scenario it is very important for the agent to understand if the practice has New Patient Exam and Root Canal as services and a new person calls for Root Canal, which service should be booked

  

1.  In many cases, practices will set up service names in such a way it won’t be obvious what it means. Providing just the names of those services will not give enough clarity to the agent regarding what service needs to be booked

  

The way this section works is a combination of OS (Online Scheduling) setting and Ai Agent setting. If the practice wants to add a new service then, they have to do it in OS. The edit button next to the services offered opens up that page in the OS. 

  

Under Services for New Patients, all the OS services set up for new patients will be available. Once a service is selected, it has to be defined to that clarity to the agent so that there is no confusion. The explanation can be manually typed or the ai button to the right of the explanation text box can be clicked to auto-generate an explanation. Services offered can’t be saved without the explanation. 

  

Only the services selected and explained here are available for the agent. Appointments will be booked only based on these services. Hence, it is imperative that this list is populated with all the services patients can book by just calling their office.

  
  

  

**Availability Settings** - This is the availability formula set up in online scheduling. This decides how the agent looks for availability. This cannot be edited directly from the Ai Agent setting and will have been updated in Online Scheduling if required. The edit button will redirect to the corresponding OS page

  

**Max Booking Window** - This decides how far out the agent can book an appointment. For example if this is set as 7 days and some caller is looking for an appointment 2 weeks from now, the agent will not book an appointment.

  

**Cancellation Window** - This decides the minimum notice period required for canceling an appointment. If this is set a blank, the agent will cancel the appointment regardless of when the appointment is scheduled for. If this is updated for, let's say 48 hours, and a patient calls to cancel an appointment for tomorrow, the agent will not cancel it directly. It will communicate about the cancellation policy and will confirm that someone from the practice will call the patient back. Then the agent will create a task for the practice to follow up. 

  

As a best practice, we don’t recommend keeping this window longer than 12 to 24 hours. If a practice wants to set this up for a longer window during onboarding it would be good to check with them how would a human handle if someone is calling to cancel an appointment for today due to a personal emergency and wouldn’t they want the agent also to do the same. The reason we suggest this is because if there is a delay from the practice to follow up on the cancellation task created by the agent, this can result in poor coordination and communication. The patient will not have clarity on if the appointment is cancelled or not and the practice might end up wondering why the patient did not show 

  

**Rescheduling Window** - This decides the minimum notice period required for rescheduling an appointment. If this is set a blank, the agent will cancel the appointment regardless of when the appointment is scheduled for. If this is updated for, let's say 24 hours, and a patient calls to reschedule an appointment for today, the agent will not reschedule it directly. It will communicate that someone from the practice will call the patient back. Then the agent will create a task for the practice to follow up. 

  

As a best practice, we don’t recommend keeping this window longer than 12 to 24 hours.

  
  

  

**Appointment Schedule Text Confirmation** - Enabling this means the agent will communicate to the patient that they will be getting an appointment confirmation text at the end of appointment booking. This is just a communication the agent is doing. The text will go out from Engage as part of Scheduled Confirmation. 

  

**Price Sheet** - Practice can use this section to give the agent knowledge about their treatment costs. If this is kept as blank and someone asks the agent about the cost of a particular treatment, the agent will communicate that it will depend on the prognosis provided by the doctor and the patient’s insurance coverage for that particular treatment option. If price is provided, then the agent will communicate the same if the caller inquires about the same.

  

**Age Limitation** - This section can be used by the practice to define the age range of patients accepted. For example if it is pediatric only dentistry and set up the max age to be 18, the agent will not book appointments for anyone older than that

  

**Walk-ins** - This is just additional information that can be provided to the agent which it can use if a caller asks the question - “Do you accept walk-ins?” 

  
  

# Agent Architecture & Flow  

## Agent Architecture & Process Flow

  

This section covers how the agentic flow is designed based on the type of conversation it is handling. Whenever a call is sent to the agent, the agent will already have following information provided the practice is using our phones - Phone Number and Caller Id (If available) if a New Patient; and for existing patients Phone Number, First Name, Last Name, DOB, Family File, Upcoming appointments for self and family

  

**New Patient Scheduling** - When the caller mentions that they are looking to book an appointment, the agent will ask if the appointment is for someone who has visited the practice before or for a new patient. The reason why we ask this question even though we get the caller id information is because sometimes existing patients can call from other numbers and new patients (like the spouse of a patient) can call from existing patient numbers.  If the caller answers they are a new patient, then this flow is triggered. The agent will collect the required information - First Name, Last Name, DOB and Insurance provider - and will then proceed to create a new patient record in Adit. At this point the application will check if we already have a patient record with the same First Name, Last Name, DOB and Phone Number. If so, no duplicate patient record is created and the patient record id of the existing patient is sent to the agent. If no duplicate is found, then the new patient record is created and the patient record id is sent to the agent. 

  

Once the agent gets the patient record, it once again double checks the appointment history to verify if they are actually a new patient or not. This is important as this defines which services are the agent allowed to book for the caller. If verified as a new patient, then the agent will proceed to ask for the reason for visit and the preferred date and time. The reason for visit mentioned by the patient is converted into appropriate service type based on the allowed options and the service explanations. For eg. If a new patient calls to check if they do invisalign and invisalign is not part of the approved services to be booked for new patients, the agent will proceed to book the generic service the practice has mentioned as a catch all

  

Based on the service and date - it will map the provider / operatory or availability set by the practice and ask if they prefer morning or afternoon slots and then the agent will call OS API which will return 2 available slots. Based on the returned slots, the agent communicates the options and ask if any of those options are acceptable to the patient then it will book the appointment. If not acceptable the agent will call OS APIs for alternate days and will present other options to the patient. If none of the options are acceptable (for eg the earliest slots available are only after 5 days and it is an emergency situation for the patient), then the agent will do a warm transfer to the practice if the call is within business hours (based on practice settings) or will create a task for the practice and communicates with the patient that someone from practice will call them back ASAP. At any point in time, a maximum of 4 APIs calls are done and if it is not able to do booking / register patient / fetch patient details then it will create a task irrespective.

  

Mandatory Preference Requirements : 

  - Configure Agent \> Agent Capabilities \> Scheduling should be turned on

  
  

**Existing Patient Scheduling** - When the caller mentions that they are an existing patient, then this flow is triggered. As part of verifying the identity of the patient, the agent will ask for the date of birth, first name and last name. If the date of birth plus phone number plus First Name and Last Name matches the information received along with the call it will proceed with the reason for visit. 

  

We ask for the name just to be HIPAA compliant because you need to request for multiple identifying information to verify the authenticity of the caller before revealing any information like their appointment details. If the DOB does not match the record the agent has, then it will confirm if they might have provided another phone number rather than the one they are calling from. With the updated phone number DOB and First and Last Name data, it will fetch the patient record.  

  

Once the correct patient record is identified, the agent will proceed with the reason for visit. The reason for visit mentioned by the patient is converted into appropriate service type based on the allowed options for existing patients and the service explanations

  

Based on the service and date - it will map the provider / operatory or availability set by the practice and ask if they prefer morning or afternoon slots and then the agent will call OS API which will return 2 available slots. Based on the returned slots, the agent communicates a few options and if any of those options are acceptable to the patient then it will book the appointment. If not acceptable the agent will call OS APIs for alternate days and will present other options to the patient. If none of the options are acceptable (for eg the earliest slots available are only after 5 days and it is an emergency situation for the patient), then the agent will do a warm transfer to the practice if the call is within business hours (based on practice settings) or will create a task for the practice and communicates with the patient that someone from practice will call them back ASAP. At any point in time, a maximum of 4 APIs calls are done and if it is not able to do booking / register patient / fetch patient details then it will create a task irrespective.

  

Mandatory Preference Requirements : 

  - Configure Agent \> Agent Capabilities \> Scheduling should be turned on

  
  

**Rescheduling** - When the caller mentions that they are looking to reschedule an appointment, the agent will check if the Registered number is the number they are calling from and then ask for the DOB and name to verify the caller and to fetch/confirm the patient record. Once the patient record is confirmed, upcoming appointments against the patient are fetched. The agent will then communicate the upcoming appointment dates and check which appointment they are planning to reschedule.Once the appointment to reschedule is identified the agent proceeds with asking for a preferred date and time to reschedule. Once the patient selects one of the options provided, the agent will proceed to reschedule the appointment.

  

If the appointment the patient wants to reschedule falls within the Rescheduling Window (Min Notice Period), then the agent will communicate that someone from practice will call them back to help with this and will create a task for the practice.

  

Rescheduling is not supported for Eaglesoft EHR

  

For Rescheduling - we check Service + Operatory + Provider and then suggest availability, if Appt was booked via EHR and then either Service / Operatory / Provider is not listed in Adit APP then no slots will be fetched. 

  

Mandatory Preference Requirements : 

  - Configure Agent \> Agent Capabilities \> Rescheduling should be turned on

  
  

**Cancellation** - When the caller mentions that they are looking to cancel an appointment, the agent will ask for the DOB and name to verify the caller and to fetch/confirm the patient record. Once the patient record is confirmed, upcoming appointments against the patient are fetched. The agent will then communicate the upcoming appointment dates and check which appointment they are planning to cancel. Once the appointment to cancel is identified the agent will check if they would like to reschedule instead of cancellation. If the patient agrees to reschedule, then the agent will initiate the rescheduling flow. If they insist on cancelling, the agent will ask if they would like to share the reason for cancellation without being pushy. If they share the reason, that will be available to the practice under Reason for Not Booking in Call Analysis. Once the reason is shared, the agent will then proceed to cancel the appointment.

  

If the appointment the patient wants to cancel falls within the Cancellation Window (Min Notice Period), then the agent will communicate that someone from practice will call them back to help with this and will create a task for the practice. 

  

Cancellation is not supported for Eaglesoft EHR

  

Mandatory Preference Requirements : 

  - Configure Agent \> Agent Capabilities \> Cancellation should be turned on

  

**Confirmation** - When the caller calls in to check the details of their upcoming appointment, the agent will ask for DOB and name to verify the caller and to fetch the correct patient record and upcoming appointment details. The agent will then communicate the details of the upcoming appointment.

  

**Other Patient Call Types** - When a patient calls for any other reason other than the cases mentioned above, the agent will communicate that someone from the practice will call back to help them and will create a task with the relevant details for the practice to follow up with the patient

  
  
  
  
  
  

# Possible Error Scenarios  

## Possible Issues / Tickets

  

This section covers some issues the agent might run into and an explanation for why that happens and how to troubleshoot it. Before we get into that, just quickly re-summarising the technology behind the product. It has 3 core parts - 

  

STT (Speech to Text) - This converts what the caller says into text transcript the agent can understand

LLM (Large Language Model) - Based of the transcript generated by the STT, the LLM will generate a response based on the prompts provided (Prompts define how the agent should behave for every scenarios it encounters)

TTS (Text To Speech) - This converts the text response generated by the LLM into audible speech

  

In addition to this, the agent will also use a lot of APIs to handle scheduling related things

  

For an agent to do a perfect call, all the 4 things - STT, LLM, TTS & APIs - should work well. Issue happens when one of these or multiple of these falters. Among these, APIs are the only thing we can control to be close to 100% accurate all the time. It is the inherent non-deterministic nature of STTs and LLMs which makes it difficult to control the accuracy of what the agent says and does sometimes. While TTS is also non-deterministic, it does not usually result in the agent saying the wrong thing, but can cause sudden fluctuations in volume and tone when it misbehaves. 

  

**Possible Issues**

  

1.  Agent is not Scheduling/Rescheduling/Cancelling appointments by itself and just keeps creating tasks - This could be because the Agent Capabilities in preferences have been set to No for Scheduling/Rescheduling/Cancellation. If this is set to “Yes” and the agent still does handle scheduling related things on its own then it needs to be looked into by the Ai Support Team

  

1.  Appointment booked for the incorrect service - Like we have covered before, AI will only have access to the services set up in the Ai Agent preferences. The first check is to see if all the relevant services are set up correctly. If all required services are mentioned, then the next check should be to see if the explanation is provided correctly and makes sense as to how that particular practice book and appointment for that type of service. If all these settings are correct, then it has to be sent to the Ai Support team to troubleshoot

  

1.  Communicating no slots are available when slots are actually available or vice-versa - This could happen because of 2 reasons. The most common reason would be an API failure. If the transcript clearly captures the date correctly and if the practice’s OS shows availability on those days for that service and the agent was not able to fetch the slots, then it is clearly an API issue and needs to be troubleshooted by the Ai Team. Another reason for this could be a transcription error where the AI transcribes the date as an incorrect one compared to the one the caller asked for. If that is the case, then it can be identified by quickly checking the transcript and listening to the call

  

1.  Triggering existing patient flow for new patients and vice versa - This can happen if there is a transcription error in STT. STT models we use are state-of-the art which could mostly mean the issue is arising from call quality or the accent/pronunciation of the caller where most human recipients might also get confused. One example error scenario here is that “I’m a new patient” when spoken fast and with a particular accent can sometimes get transcribed as “I’m your patient” which can effectively trigger the existing patient flow. To tackle this we frame the questions the agent asks in such a way that the answers are not easy to get confused with regardless of call quality or pronunciation. But in rare cases, this might still happen. To identify if the issue is because of this, you can quickly check the transcript of the call in the Adit App to understand if the agent got confused because of the transcription issue. Also you can listen to that part of the call to understand if it was actually confusing. If the audio was clear or the transcription did not have any errors, then this has to be troubleshooted by the Ai Team

  

1.  Answering for a different question than what the caller asks/says - This is also usually a case of transcription error in STT, but once again to be clear - more often than not it will be due to call quality or pronunciation issues. We do denoising (a process of removing unwanted noise and background speech) and optimize the STT for accuracy to reduce instances like these as much as possible, but it can still happen. Once again, to identify if the issue is because of this, you can quickly check the transcript of the call in the Adit App to understand if the agent got confused because of the transcription issue. Also you can listen to that part of the call to understand if it was actually confusing. If the audio was clear or the transcription did not have any errors, then this has to be troubleshooted by the Ai Team

  

1.  Switching to another language even when the caller does not - This is another instance of STT transcription issue. Since the STT is trained on more than 30 languages, the unclear audio of a supposed English word transcribed incorrectly can mean a proper word in another language the agent speaks. This triggers the auto-language switch functionality of the agent and immediately in the middle of the conversation it will start speaking in another language even though the caller did not. The agent will immediately switch back to English / or the original language of the conversation as soon as the caller replies back in English / original language. But it can still sound very jarring for the caller and they will feel like the agent just switched language for no reason. Once again, to identify if the issue is because of this, you can quickly check the transcript of the call in the Adit App to understand if the agent got confused because of the transcription issue. Also you can listen to that part of the call to understand if it was actually confusing. If the audio was clear or the transcription did not have any errors, then this has to be troubleshooted by the Ai Team

  

1.  Not able to verify existing patients - This could happen because of 2 reasons. One reason is again a transcription error when the AI collects the DOB or Phone Number. In this scenario the agent will try to fetch the patient record with the incorrect DOB or Phone Number. The second reason is that the patient is not able to correctly recollect the phone number or DOB which was provided earlier resulting in the agent not being able to pull up the correct record. If it is the first issue. once again transcript + call recording recording will be able to identify it. If it is the second issue, we can go to the location and search for that patient record just using the name and compare the DOB/ Phone Number present in the record compared to the info given by the caller.

  

1.  Adit App shows as Existing Patient even when the caller clearly says New Patient and vice versa - 

  

1.  Booked appointment for unaccepted Insurance - As of now, the functionality is not available to check if the insurance provided by the user is part of the accepted list or not, so the agents default behaviour is book the appointment if the patient is a PPO insurance holder. If the caller specifically asks if their insurance is accepted, the agent will communicate that mostly all PPOs are accepted but we will have someone from the practice double check and call you back and will create a task for the practice. The functionality to check if a particular insurance is accepted or not is being built out should be available by the mid of March 2026.

  
  
  
  
  

# Interaction Type  

## Interaction Type

  

Interaction Type will be updated for all calls

  

  - Scheduling - If the patient is calling the practice looking to book an appointment 
  - Recheduling - If the patient or the practice is calling to cancel an appointment or to reschedule an appointment
  - Confirmation - If the patient is calling to confirm or clarify the time of the appointment or if the practice is calling the patient to confirm their participation
  - Emergency - If the patient is looking to book an appointment on an emergency basis due to severe pain, accident or for some other unforeseen reason
  - Recare - If the practice is reaching out to the patient to get them to book an appointment because they missed their appointment or if some of their treatment is due or just trying to bring them back because it has been sometime since their last visit
  - Clinical - If the patient or the practice is calling to check on general care, treatment care, treatment recovery, instructions, prescriptions or medicines
  - Billing - If the conversation is between the patient (or guardian) and the practice regarding outstanding payments, billing clarifications or payment plans and benefits
  - Insurance - If the conversation is between the patient (or guardian) and the practice regarding insurance acceptance, insurance rejection or claim follow ups
  - Verifications: Calls made by the practice to an insurance company or provider on behalf of a patient to check eligibility, claims, benefits, or status
  - Product Order - If the call is related to tracking the order or confirming the arrival of products like crowns, braces, veneers, specs, lenses etc
  - Vendor - If the call is between the practice and a vendor other than the insurance provider regarding the orders placed by the practice to the vendor or regarding diagnostics done with the vendor
  - Others - If the completed call does not fall into any of the above categories, then it will get categorised as others
  - Incomplete - If the call gets terminated very early without a proper conversation between the practice and the patient, then it will get marked as “Incomplete”
  - Missed - If it is a missed call

  
  
  
  

# Outcome  

## Outcome

  

All calls other than Others, Incomplete and Missed Calls will get categorised with this

  

  - Booked - If the call type is Rescheduling, Scheduling, Emergency or Recare and an appointment has been booked. An appointment should be considered Booked if the practice schedules it and will follow up only if there is an issue, even if insurance verification is pending. 
  - Cancelled - If the call type is Rescheduling and the appointment has been cancelled without rescheduling
  - Not Booked - If the call type is Scheduling, Emergency or Recare and an appointment has not been booked.Only mark as Not Booked if the patient actively leaves without an appointment or if no appointment was scheduled
  - Resolved - If the call type is Confirmation, Clinical, Billing, Insurance, Verifications, Vendor, Product Order or Others and call has been fully resolved with no further action required for now
  - Not Resolved - If the call type is Confirmation, Clinical, Billing, Insurance, Verifications, Vendor, Product Order or Others and call has not been fully resolved and will require further action

## Reason for Not Booking

  

Reason for Not Booking will be updated only for Scheduling, Emergency, Rescheduling and Recare Calls if the call outcome is Not Booked or Cancelled.

  - Medicaid Not Accepted - Patient has Medicare and practice do not accept it
  - Medicare Not Accepted - Patient has Medicaid and practice do not accept it
  - Insurance Not Accepted - Practice does not accept the insurance the patient has. This does not include those scenarios where patient has Medicare or Medicaid
  - Personal Emergency - When a rescheduling call goes cancelled due to personal emergency
  - Cash Patients Not Accepted - Practice do not accept cash patients for those cases where patients are willing to pay cash because their insurance is not accepted or they don’t have insurance
  - Language Barrier - Patient looking for specific language speaking staff to book an appointment. For eg. a Spanish only speaking patient checking if someone in the practice can speak Spanish
  - Not Interested Right Now - If the call type is Recare and the patient declines to book at this time or indicates they are not ready to schedule, and does not provide a specific callback time
  - Call Back Later - If the call type is Recare and the patient mentioned the practice to call back later at a specific time or time period to get them scheduled. Only mark as **Call Back Later** if the patient explicitly mentions a specific time, day, or period for the practice to call back to schedule. Any vague statements like ‘maybe later’ or ‘not now’ should **not** be considered Call Back Later
  - Outstanding Balance - If an appointment is not booked due to an outstanding balance or collections
  - Others - If a scheduling/emergency call goes Not Booked and reasons do not fall within any of the above
