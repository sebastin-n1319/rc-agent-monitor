# **What Is Statement IQ?**

Statement IQ is a tool I built to make our statement analysis process faster and more consistent. You upload any statement \- PDF, photo, or screenshot \- and it pulls out the merchant name, processor, volume, and fees automatically. It then compares those numbers against our flat-rate pricing and gives you the exact monthly and annual savings. No manual math, no spreadsheets. The savings number is ready before you even finish the conversation.

# **Quick Start \- Analyze a Statement**

| 1 | Get the statement Ask the merchant for last month’s processing statement. PDF works best, but a phone photo or screenshot is fine too. |
| :---: | :---- |
| **2** | **Upload it** Drag it onto the upload area or click to browse. Supported: PDF, PNG, JPG, WEBP, XLSX, CSV. Max 5 MB. |
| **3** | **Reduce size if needed** If the file is over 5 MB, a ⚡ Reduce Size button appears. Click it \- it compresses in-browser, no tool needed. |
| **4** | **Run the analysis** Click Analyze Statement. It reads the document and pulls out the merchant name, processor, volume, fees, and period \- usually 10-30 seconds. |
| **5** | **Check the extracted values** The numbers will appear in a band below the upload area. Quickly scan the fees and volume \- these drive the savings number, so they need to be right. |
| **6** | **Set the transaction split** Adjust the slider for the in-person vs. online ratio. Default is 90/10. Change it if you know the merchant’s setup. |
| **7** | **Calculate savings** Click Calculate Savings. The monthly and annual savings show up immediately, along with a summary you can use in your pitch. |
| **8** | **Push to Zoho** Enter the AP ID and click Save. The statement and savings note go straight into the AP record in Zoho. |

# **Zoho CRM Integration**

When you push a statement, the tool writes directly into the **Adit Pay (AP) module** in Zoho CRM. Three things happen in one click: a note is added, key fields are updated, and the statement file is attached to the record.

## **Module  →  Adit Pay (AP)**

Enter the AP ID from the record URL or the CRM search. The tool locates the record automatically and writes all data to it.

# **Tips for Best Results**

* **Double-check the extracted fees and volume** before hitting Calculate \- if those numbers are off, the savings will be off too.

* **Push to CRM while you’re still on the call** \- don’t let statements pile up to do later.

* **Use the Print one-pager** as a leave-behind after the call. Seeing their own numbers on a clean document lands better than a verbal number.

* **Hit ⚡ Reduce Size** on anything over 2 MB before uploading \- keeps the CRM attachment clean.

* **Check the Opportunity Signal first** before your pitch approach: Strong → lead with the dollar savings; Challenging → lead with the platform.

* **Add a note to every record** \- even just a line about where the conversation is. It helps when you come back to it.
