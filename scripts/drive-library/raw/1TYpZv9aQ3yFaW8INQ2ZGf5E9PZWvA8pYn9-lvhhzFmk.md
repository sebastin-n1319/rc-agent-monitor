**Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Ticket Handling Between T1 and T2**

### **1\. Purpose**

#### This SOP outlines the responsibilities and procedures for handling tickets between the Tier 1 (T1) and Tier 2 (T2) support teams to ensure efficient resolution, proper communication, and client satisfaction.

### **2\. Scope**

#### This SOP applies to all T1 and T2 support agents responsible for ticket resolution and customer interactions.

### **3\. Responsibilities**

* #### **T1 Team:** Initial triage, investigation, and customer communication.

* #### **T2 Team:** Advanced troubleshooting and resolution of tickets. 

### **4\. Process Guidelines (T1 to T2)**

#### **4.1 Ticket Closure Responsibility**

* #### Any ticket assigned to T2 from T1 will be the responsibility of T2 to investigate, resolve, and close.

* #### T2 must ensure that all troubleshooting steps are documented before closing the ticket.

#### **4.2 Client Satisfaction (CSAT) Collection**

* #### Upon resolving and closing a ticket, T2 is responsible for obtaining a Client Satisfaction (CSAT) rating from the client.

* #### T2 should send a follow-up email to the client requesting feedback and addressing any additional concerns.

#### **4.3 Handling Improperly Investigated Tickets**

* #### If T1 assigns a ticket to T2 without proper investigation, or having lack of basic understanding of the product, T2 will document the issue on the ticket and notify the following individuals by tagging them:

  * #### Karan

  * #### Ronnie

  * #### Sabariranjan

* #### These individuals will review the matter and provide feedback to the concerned T1 agent to ensure proper handling in the future.

####  **4.4 Client Education Cases**

* #### If T2 identifies that the issue is due to a lack of client understanding rather than a technical issue, they will return the ticket to T1.

* #### T1 will be responsible for educating the client on the relevant topic and ensuring the client has the necessary knowledge to prevent recurring issues.

* In this case, T1 will be responsible for closing the ticket after the client's education is completed.

### **5\. Process Guidelines (CSM to T2)**

#### **5.1 CSM to T2 Ticket Handling**

* Any tickets assigned to T2 from Customer Success Managers (CSMs) will be handled exclusively by T2.  
* T2 will communicate directly with the client regarding the resolution and provide all necessary updates.  
* Once the resolution is communicated, T2 will close the ticket and get CSAT from the client.  
* For CSM tickets, any client education required will be managed by the T2 agent.

### **6\. Process Guidelines (OB/Mark to T2)**

#### **6.1 Ticket Closure Responsibility**

* #### Any OB/Mark Tickets where a client is escalated or needs urgent resolution will directly go to T2.

* Any other tickets go to T1 first and If T1 will not be able to resolve the issue they will add the required details to the ticket and pass it to the T2.

* #### After requiring details added in the ticket and ticket being assigned to T2  from T1 or OB/Mark, will be the responsibility of T2 to investigate, resolve, and close.

* #### T2 must ensure that all troubleshooting steps are documented before closing the ticket.

* Once the resolution is determined, T2 will communicate directly with the client regarding the resolution and provide all necessary updates..

#### **6.2 Handling Improperly Investigated Tickets**

* #### Any tickets transferred from OB/Mark to T1 must include a basic preliminary investigation conducted by OB/Mark. This ensures that the issue is well-defined and that T1/T2 receives sufficient context to proceed with troubleshooting.

* #### T1/T2 is responsible for working on the resolution of the issue but will not engage in direct communication with the client to gather additional details. OB/Mark should provide all necessary background information before transferring the ticket.

* #### If Mark/OB assigns a ticket to T2 without proper investigation, or having a lack of basic understanding of the product, T2 will document the issue on the ticket and notify the team leads by tagging them in the ticket.

* #### These individuals will review the matter and provide feedback to the concerned Mark/OB Agent to ensure proper handling in the future. 

#### **6.3 Client Education Cases**

* If any ticket comes to T1 and T2 finds that there is a client education part then T2 will return the ticket to T1 so T1 can educate the client.  
* In cases where client education is necessary, OB/Mark will handle the communication and explanation to the client. They must ensure that the client understands the resolution and any preventative measures necessary to avoid similar issues in the future.

#### **6.4 Client Satisfaction (CSAT) Collection**

* #### Upon resolving and closing a ticket, T2 is responsible for obtaining a Client Satisfaction (CSAT) rating from the client.

* #### T2 should send a follow-up email to the client requesting feedback and addressing any additional concerns.

### **5\. Compliance & Accountability**

* All agents must adhere to this SOP to maintain efficiency and improve client experience.  
* Any repeated failure to follow these procedures will be escalated to the management team for review and corrective action.

### **6\. Review & Updates**

* This SOP will be reviewed periodically to ensure alignment with best practices and operational requirements.  
* Any modifications will be communicated to all team members in advance.
