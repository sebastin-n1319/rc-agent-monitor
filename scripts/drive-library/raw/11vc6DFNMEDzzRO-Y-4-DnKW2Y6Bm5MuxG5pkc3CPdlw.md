**Troubleshooting steps for users who say “I am not receiving my OTP”**

1\. Confirm with the user that the email inbox they are checking is the correct email associated with the User.   
You can view their email by going to **Settings \-\> Admin \-\> Users & Access \-\> Select User**,  


2\. Have user search for “[**no-reply at adit.com**](mailto:no-reply at adit.com)“ in their email inbox to see if it shows anywhere.   


3\. Have user search for “**OTP**” in the inbox to see if it shows anywhere  


4.Have user check “**Spam**”, “**Junk**”, and “**All** **Mail**” sections in their Email Inbox.  


4\. If they are still unable to find it,  have the user click on “**Forgot Password**” and see if they get a reset pw link.  


5\. If they are still not getting anything, have the user reach out to the owner of their account and request them to change the email address on file. Here’s where the owner can do that  
Go into **Settings \-\> Admin \-\> Users & Access \-\> Select User** \-\> **Change Email**

6\. After changing the email, have user try signing in again, the OTP will trigger to the new email on file.

**Troubleshooting steps for users who say “I don’t know my my email on file”**

1\. Go into **Settings \-\> Admin \-\> Users & Access \-\> Select User**,   
Confirm what their username is, and check their email that’s associated with their username,  
 

2\. Make sure we verify their identity thoroughly before sharing their email information.
