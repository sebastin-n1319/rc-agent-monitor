## **Guide to Insurance Verification Packages**

Adit offers three types of Insurance Verification services designed to meet different practice needs—from fully automated verification to expert-reviewed, high-accuracy verification.

---

### **1\. Basic Verification (EDI Only)**

**Verification Type:**  
Fully automated EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) verification.

**Pricing:**  
\$99/month includes **300 free EDI verifications**  
Additional transactions are charged at **\$0.06 per verification**

**CDT Code Coverage:**  
Supports up to **40 CDT codes**

**Process:**  
Adit integration pulls eligibility information directly through EDI in real-time.

**Failed Verifications:**

* No cost for failed verifications  
* Does **not include manual expert follow-up**

**EHR Integration:**  
Verified PDFs and breakdown notes are **automatically posted into your EHR**

---

### **2\. Advanced Verification (Standard)**

**Verification Type:**  
Combination of automated EDI verification and manual expert validation.

**Pricing:**  
\$99/month \+ **\$1.00 per advanced verification**

**CDT Code Coverage:**  
Supports up to **20 CDT codes**

**Process:**  
Adit pulls eligibility via EDI, which is then **reviewed and finalized by our expert RCM team** for improved accuracy.

**Failed Appointments:**

* If EDI verification fails, the **expert team manually verifies the appointment**  
* Ensures higher success rates

**Additional Flexibility:**

* Any individual patient appointment can be **upgraded to Advanced verification**

**EHR Integration:**  
Verified PDFs and notes are **automatically posted into your EHR**

---

### **3\. Advanced Verification (Extensive)**

**Verification Type:**  
EDI \+ Manual expert verification designed for complex or high-volume needs.

**Pricing:**  
\$99/month \+ **\$2.25 per advanced verification**

**CDT Code Coverage:**  
Supports **20 to 40 CDT codes**

**Process:**  
Eligibility is pulled via EDI and **fully validated by the expert RCM team**, making it ideal for complex plans such as Medicaid and HMOs.

**Failed Appointments:**

* All failed verifications are **handled by the expert team at no additional cost**

**Additional Flexibility:**

* Suitable for appointments requiring **extended code coverage and deeper verification**

**EHR Integration:**  
Verified PDFs and notes are **automatically posted into your EHR**
