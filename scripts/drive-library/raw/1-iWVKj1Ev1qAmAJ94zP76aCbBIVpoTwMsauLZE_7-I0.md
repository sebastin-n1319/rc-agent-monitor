# Staff Training Process

## Purpose

The goal of this training is to teach the staff how to use the Adit platform in their day-to-day work. Remember that you are only training on the Core platform: Engage, Pozative, Patient Forms, and Adit Voice and eFax (if applicable).

If your client plans on using Online Scheduling and you have already met with them to fully configure it, you can include it as part of the Core Platform and train the staff on it at this stage. If they are not sure about using OS yet, it is better to save that conversation for the Adoption phase.

## Before Training

Ensure that the Core platform has been fully configured and you know how the practice will use certain features, such as if they will send Review Requests manually instead of automatically. You will train the staff on the correct way to use the Adit platform; it is up to the POC, Office Manager, or practice admins who are implementing Adit for the practice to ensure the staff follows all of their internal processes.

Discuss with your POC at the practice ahead of time how their team prefers to ask questions:

1. Interrupt as each question comes to mind?  
2. Wait until you pause for questions at the end of each section?  
3. Hold all questions until the end?  
4. Send all questions to the POC, who will forward them to you via email so you can answer in writing?

Each OB has their own preference, but in general we find that the options 2 or 4 are best for larger groups (6 or more people).

## Staff Training Call

The Staff Training call should take around 60 minutes, though it can be longer if the staff has a lot of questions.  Anyone who will be using the Adit platform or communicating with patients \- including using Adit phones (if applicable) \- should be included in this training session. If they cannot make it to the live training, that’s ok\! We’ll send the recording after the call so they can share it with anyone who missed it, or use it to train new members of their team in the future.

***You should not cover any settings or preferences in the staff training; 90% of staff will never change their settings and showing them how is just overloading them with more information than they need.***

If applicable, pause after each section and check in with the staff to see if they have any questions. 

#### Training Agenda

Be sure to cover the following:

* Navigation  
  * How to switch between modules    
  * Notifications  
  * How to access the help widget   
  * How to search for a patient   
  * Patient Card   
  * Quick Text  
* Phones  
  * Call pop and softphone  
  * How to make an outbound call  
  * How to check voicemails  
  * How to view call history  
  * Call Override  
  * How to hold, park, and transfer on hardphones  
* eFax  
  * How to send an eFax  
  * How they will receive incoming faxes  
  * How to see fax history  
* Engage  
  * How to search for an existing conversation  
  * How to start a new conversation  
  * How to send a text message  
  * How to tell when they have received a new text  
  * How to send a Mass Text  
* Pozative  
  * How to interpret the data the dashboard  
  * How to manually send a review request  
  * How to view and respond to reviews  
* Patient Forms  
  * How to request a patient fill out a form  
  * How to see requested and submitted forms, tabs in Forms page  
  * How to re-send forms back to patients (i.e. to complete a skipped question)  
  * How to view, approve, and archive forms  
  * How to see if a form has been imported to the PMS

If you have configured OS for the practice, you should also include:

* Online Scheduling  
  * How to see an appointment request  
  * How appointments write into their EHR (if applicable)  
  * How to manually send an appointment request to a patient

If you have configured Treatment plan templates for the practice, you should also include:

* Treatment Plans  
  * How to interpret the data on the Tx Plans page  
  * How to create a treatment plan for a patient  
* Email Campaigns  
  * How to create an email campaign  
  * How to duplicate or delete an existing campaign  
  * How to interpret the data of the campaign report

## After Training

#### Send Follow Up Email

Send a follow up email to the POC and thank them for their time. Provide the recording of the training session and link them to any resources that you think they may find particularly useful, such as a KB article for a feature their staff was excited about.

#### Update CRM

Update the **Staff Training Complete** field on the TO-record in CRM.
