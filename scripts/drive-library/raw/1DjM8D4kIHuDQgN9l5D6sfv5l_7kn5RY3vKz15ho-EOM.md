# 🎯 Adit Pay – Sales Enablement Playbook

**Audience:** Adit Pay Team  
**Product:** Adit Pay (Stripe-powered)  
**Goal:** Increase Card Terminal Sale & Adit Pay Transaction Volume  
**Primary Challenge:** Clients already using bank POS terminals

## 1️⃣ Sales Mindset (Most Important)

### ❌ What NOT to Sell

* Card terminal

* Better Merchant Discount Rate

* Hardware replacement

### ✅ What TO Sell

**Outcome-based value**: \> Faster collections, fewer missed payments, smoother patient experience — directly inside Adit.

**Sales Rule:**  
If you say *terminal*, *machine*, or *POS* — immediately reframe to **collections, speed, or experience**.

## 2️⃣ Discovery Questions (Use As-Is)

Start with questions, not features.

1. How do you currently collect payments from patients?

2. Do patients ever leave without completing payment?

3. Do you collect any payments before the appointment?

4. How do you handle unpaid or pending balances?

👉 Let the client describe the gaps in their own words.

## 3️⃣ Gap Framing (Mirror the Pain)

Use this exact framing:

“So today, payments mostly happen only when the patient is physically at the front desk, and anything outside that becomes manual. That’s very common.”

This builds trust and sets up Adit Pay naturally.

## 4️⃣ Core Pitch (30-Second Script)

“Your bank terminal works well at the front desk.  
Adit Pay works **everywhere else** — online scheduling, treatment plans, invoices, and follow-ups.  
It helps practices get paid **faster and more consistently**, not just accept cards.”

🚫 Never position Adit Pay as a replacement.  
✅ Position it as an expansion.

## 5️⃣ Objection Handling (Must Memorize)

### Objection: “We already have a card machine”

**Response:**  
\> “Absolutely  and you should keep using it. Most of our clients do. Adit Pay is used for online payments, advance collections, and payment link.  Areas where bank terminals can’t help.”

### Objection: “Our bank Merchant Discount Rate is lower”

**Response:**  
\> “That’s great for swipe transactions. Adit Pay focuses on increasing collections and reducing missed payments. Most clinics recover more revenue than the MDR difference within the first month.”

### Objection: “Front desk already handles payments”

**Response:**  
\> “Exactly Adit Pay actually reduces their workload. Amounts are auto-filled, payments auto-recorded, and reconciliation happens automatically.”

### Objection: “Patients prefer cash or card”

**Response:**  
\> “That’s true at the counter. But for online scheduling and treatment approvals, patients prefer quick digital links. That’s where Adit Pay significantly improves payment completion.”

## 6️⃣ Killer Differentiator (Use This Line)

**“Bank POS helps you accept payments.**  
**Adit Pay helps you not miss payments.”**

## 7️⃣ Use-Case Selling (Sell Scenarios, Not Features)

### Online Scheduling

Patients confirm appointments by paying online → fewer no-shows.

### Treatment Plans

Approval \+ payment in one flow → no delays.

### Follow-ups

Payment links via SMS/email → no awkward calls.

## 8️⃣ High-Converting Call to Action

❌ Weak CTA:  
“Do you want to try Adit Pay?”

✅ Strong CTA:  
\> “Let’s enable Adit Pay for your next 10 invoices and see how many get paid faster.”

## 9️⃣ Sales KPIs to Track

* % of clients enabled with Adit Pay

* % of invoices paid digitally

* Average days to payment

* Payment volume per clinic

📌 Reward **adoption and usage**, not just activation.

## 🔑 Sales One-Line Summary

**“We don’t sell card terminals.**  
**We help practices collect money faster, easier, and more reliably — inside Adit.”**
