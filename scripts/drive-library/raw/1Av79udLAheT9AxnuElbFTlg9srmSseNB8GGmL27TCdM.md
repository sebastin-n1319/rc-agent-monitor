# **Adit Call Intelligence Upgrade Sales Playbook**

## **Introduction to Call Intelligence**

Adit's Call Intelligence is an AI-powered upgrade to our existing Call Tracking module, designed specifically for dental, optometry, and multi-location practices (including DSOs) to transform how they handle inbound and outbound calls. Built on advanced AI technology, it goes beyond basic call logging by providing automated summaries, transcripts, sentiment analysis, performance scorecards, task creation, and actionable insights. This helps practices capture missed revenue, improve staff performance, enhance patient experiences, and make data-driven decisions without manually listening to every call.

Key benefits include:

* **Revenue Recovery**: Identifies unbooked scheduling opportunities, missed calls, and high-value leads (Invisalign or implants) for follow-up.  
* **Operational Efficiency**: Automates tasks, notifications for upset patients or lost opportunities, and integrates summaries directly into patient profiles in the EHR.  
* **Performance Insights**: Scores staff on calls, tracks conversions, and provides dashboards for sentiment, call types, and marketing ROI.  
* **Seamless Integration**: Upgrades the Call Tracking UI with AI features; available per location, with phase 2 enhancements (automatic lead-to-patient conversion) rolling out soon.

Priced as an upgrade, it's ideal for existing Adit customers already using Call Tracking or phone services. Phase 1 focuses on core AI analysis and reporting; phase 2 adds advanced automation like multi-location dashboards and lead conversion.

This playbook equips Upgrade SDRs and AEs with a step-by-step guide to pitch and sell Call Intelligence, drawing from internal training insights and proven switch factors reasons practices upgrade or switch providers.

Focus on value over features tie everything to pain points like lost revenue, staff inefficiencies, and lack of visibility. Use the 11 switch factors (Linked Below)  as your "winning points" to build urgency. Sell to Office Managers, Doctors, or DSO leaders; avoid front desk staff initially, as they may resist performance tracking.

### **2\. Discovery: Uncover Pain with Targeted Questions**

Discovery is 60% of the sale use open-ended questions based on switch factors to build pain and position Call Intelligence as the fix. Aim for 10-15 minutes; listen more than talk.

**Opening Rapport**: "I noticed you're using Adit Call Tracking great foundation. Today, let's talk about upgrading to AI-powered insights to capture more revenue and streamline your front desk."

**Core Discovery Questions** (Group by theme; ask 4-6 per call, probing deeper):

**Revenue & Opportunities (\#1, \#2, \#4, \#8)**:

"How many scheduling calls don't result in bookings each month? How do you follow up?"

"What happens with missed calls? Do you track which ones get called back and converted?"

"How do you spot high-value opportunities like Invisalign or crowns that slip away, and why weren't they booked?"

"After a call, how do you ensure action items (insurance verifications) don't fall through?"

**Patient Experience & Notifications (\#3, \#5, \#6)**:

"How do you know if a patient was unhappy during a call before it escalates to a bad review?"

"Is your front desk delivering consistent experiences across new/existing patients or call types (billing vs. scheduling)?"

"How do you keep patient notes updated after every interaction without manual work?"

**Staff Performance & Training (\#7)**:

"How often do you review staff calls for improvements? If you retrain, how do you measure progress?"

**Data & ROI (\#9, \#10, \#11)**:

"Which marketing channels convert best? If one fails, is it lead gen or front desk conversion and why?"

"How do you make decisions on staffing, services, or insurances based on call data?"

"For your locations, how do you compare front desk performance side-by-side?"

**Probing Tips**: Follow up with "Tell me more" or "What impact does that have on revenue?" Quantify pain: "If you miss 50 calls/month, that's potentially \$10,000  in lost appointments."

**Transition to Pitch**: "Based on what you've shared about (pain point), Call Intelligence directly addresses that by (tease value). Let me show you how."

**3\. Pitching: Build Value with Stories and Demos**

Keep pitches 15-20 minutes; use storytelling from training ("Practices miss 50+ calls a month during business hours, imagine recovering that revenue"). Focus on 3-5 switch factors per pitch, tailored to discovery. Structure: Problem → Solution → Benefit → Proof.

**Pitch Framework**:

**Hook with Pain**: Start with a stat: "Most practices lose 60% of potential revenue from unbooked calls and inconsistent follow-ups."

**Overview Value Prop**: "Call Intelligence uses AI to analyze every call in real-time, providing summaries, sentiments, tasks, and insights so you never miss revenue, upset patients, or training opportunities."

**Demo Walkthrough** (Use app/Figma):

**Call Detail Page**: "Here's a call, AI summarizes it, detects sentiment (green=happy, red=unhappy), scores staff ( rapport, empathy), and creates tasks like 'Follow up on billing issue.'

**Leads Dashboard**: "Unbooked calls become leads here, sorted by revenue potential. Phase 2 auto-converts to patients in your EHR."

**Insights & Reporting**: "See new patient bookings, missed call recovery (click to view unreturned calls), conversion rates (inbound vs. outbound), and marketing sources."

**Notifications**: "Get instant emails for upset patients or lost high-value ops with summary and transcript snippets."

**Integration**: "Summaries push to patient notes in EHR for quick reference, no manual updates." (Phase 2\)

**Tailored Stories**:

* For OMs: "Imagine Friday at 5 PM, check missed calls dashboard, see unreturned ones, assign tasks instantly."

* For Doctors: "A patient calls for a \$3K implant but doesn't book, AI flags it, shows reason (insurance), and creates a follow-up task."

* For DSOs: "Compare locations: Location A converts 70% of scheduling calls, Location B only 50%, drill down to staff scores and sentiments."  
  **Quantify ROI**: "Practices recover 10-20% more appointments; reduce bad reviews by catching unhappy calls early; improve staff conversion by 15% via scoring."

### **4\. Objection Handling: Turn Doubts into Closes**

Common objections from training insights respond with empathy, restate value, and ask for commitment.

**"Too Expensive/We Don't Need It"**: "Upgrades add cost, but consider the ROI. If you recover just 5 appointments/month at \$200 each, it pays for itself. Plus, no manual call reviews saves hours. Shall we run a quick calculation based on your call volume?"

**"Our Staff Already Handles This"**: "That's great they're proactive, but even top teams miss visibility. AI scores every call and tracks conversions without listening, empowering better training. Have you measured outbound scheduling success lately?"

**"Not Ready for AI/Privacy Concerns"**: "AI is secure and compliant; we use voiceprints for accuracy, not storage. It starts with simple summaries and tasks building to full insights. Many practices start with one location to test."

**"What About Phase 2 Features?"**: "Core features like summaries and notifications are live now; phase 2 (auto-lead conversion) launches Soon, enhancing what you get today. We can onboard with phase 1 and upgrade seamlessly."

**"We Have Something Similar"**: "RingCentral summaries voicemails, but Call Intelligence analyzes full calls, creates EHR-integrated tasks, and provides staff scoring, addressing switch factors like upset notifications that others miss."
