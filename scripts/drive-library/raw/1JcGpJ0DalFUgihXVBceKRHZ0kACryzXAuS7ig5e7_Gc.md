**Update regarding porting process Getting Started to Onboarding**

1\. The sales team is helping the porting team to get the porting details and documents from the client.

2\. After all the GT steps are done and the IT details record is marked as Valid by porting, the deal gets moved from Getting Started to Onboarding.

3.Deals are marked as invalid if the porting details or documents are incomplete or incorrect.

4\. An automation task is sent to Ruchi after the deal is marked as invalid.

5\. For invalid deals Ruchi helps the porting team to get the correct details and documents from clients.

6.We have maintained a porting rule book which has the contact details and other details of Adit competitor companies. Porting details and document required sheet

7\. If there are any additional numbers apart from landline and fax numbers in client current phone provider company CSR or invoice which is not updated in IT details record that is supposed to be confirmed by the OB of the deal before the config call date is updated in tech OB record.

Note: We have observed multiple issues while obtaining porting details and required documents from the current phone provider. These issues often include delays in sharing information or providing incomplete details in the CSR or invoice. Similar problems sometimes continue even after the porting process has started, where porting requests may be rejected by the current phone provider despite submitting correct details and documentation.

Therefore, we request you to kindly set the right expectation with the client that until the number is successfully ported, any delays or issues encountered during the process may occur due to the current phone provider or their carrier.

Below are the main reasons for porting getting rejected.  
·  The current phone provider company or carrier company didn’t get approval from client (owner of the number) to release the number when they received the port out request which we have raised.

·  The current phone provider company does this intentionally so that they can make the process complicated and convince the client to stay with them.
