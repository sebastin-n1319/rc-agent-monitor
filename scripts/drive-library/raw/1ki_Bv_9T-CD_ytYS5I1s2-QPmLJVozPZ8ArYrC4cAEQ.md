## **OB Training: Communication, Expectation Setting, and Client Updates**

Effective communication is the foundation of successful client management and long-term retention. It is also the **number one complaint** we receive from clients—making it one of the most critical skills for an Onboarding Specialist to master.

### **The Role of an Onboarding Specialist**

An Onboarding Specialist is responsible for:

* Understanding the client’s needs and goals

* Coordinating onboarding activities to meet those needs

* Communicating clearly so the client feels both informed and understood

* Advocating for the client within Adit

* Representing Adit professionally in every client interaction

**Understand Your Client**

Our success—individually, as a team, and as a company—depends on how well we meet our clients’ needs.

The most important part of your role is truly understanding your client. This process begins during the kickoff call and should continue throughout every interaction. You should consistently seek to understand:

* What are the client’s goals and expectations?

* What motivated them to choose Adit?

* What challenges or pain points are they experiencing?

* How can Adit’s platform help address those needs?

Equally important is understanding **how** your client prefers to communicate. Adjust your communication style to match theirs—it is not the client’s responsibility to adapt to you. For example:

* Do they prefer detailed phone calls or brief emails?

* How strictly do they adhere to their schedule?

* How responsive are they to follow-ups?

Once you understand your client’s needs, goals, and communication style, you can more effectively coordinate the many moving parts of their onboarding experience.

## **Communicate Effectively**

Strong communication is a skill that requires constant practice. Always aim to:

* Speak slowly and clearly

* Actively listen and restate key points to confirm understanding

* Ask open-ended questions that encourage clients to share more information

Clear communication builds trust and prevents misunderstandings before they arise.

## **Set and Manage Expectations**

Set expectations with your client at **every opportunity**. Clients should always know:

* When they will hear from you next

* What the next communication will cover

* When to expect updates from other teams, especially if an issue is in progress

Setting expectations is only effective if you follow through. If you commit to a call on Wednesday, you must call on Wednesday. If you promise an email the next morning, send it in the morning. Do not substitute one form of communication for another, and avoid reaching out at the end of the day when clients are typically busiest.

Reliability is a key driver of client confidence.

**Share Frequent and Meaningful Updates**

When another team (e.g., T2 Core Tech) is involved in resolving a client issue, it is your responsibility to:

* Review ticket updates daily

* Check notes and current status

* Proactively relay relevant information to the client

Do not wait until an issue is fully resolved to provide an update, and do not rely on other teams to communicate directly with the client.

Even when there is no major change, letting the client know that work is ongoing reassures them that their issue is being actively addressed and that they remain a priority.

## **Advocate for Your Client**

You are both:

* The voice of Adit to the client, and

* The voice of the client within Adit

While part of your role is to encourage adoption of Adit’s products and features, this must be done thoughtfully. Rather than pushing products, demonstrate their value by aligning recommendations with the client’s specific goals and business needs. Help clients understand both the benefits of using the platform and the potential cost of not doing so.

Above all, you are the client’s advocate. By fully understanding their needs, you can collaborate effectively with internal teams to resolve issues and deliver solutions that truly support their practice.

## **Own the Onboarding Process**

You are responsible for tracking and managing all aspects of the client’s onboarding journey, including any offshoots such as technical issues or support tickets. This includes:

* Coordinating issue resolution with internal teams

* Asking questions to ensure tasks are completed correctly and on time

* Reviewing relevant CRM records (e.g., Porting status, Adoption Score)

By proactively monitoring both the onboarding and support experience, you can often identify potential dissatisfaction before the client voices it. This allows you to address concerns earlier, communicate more effectively, and prevent escalation.

## **Final Takeaway**

By mastering communication, expectation setting, and client advocacy, you build trust that extends beyond onboarding. These skills create confident, satisfied clients who not only succeed with Adit but become enthusiastic advocates for our platform within their communities.
