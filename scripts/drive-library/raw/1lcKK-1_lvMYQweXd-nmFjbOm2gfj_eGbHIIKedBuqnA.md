1. # **Objective** {#objective}

This SOP provides the VoIP Asset Management Team with a standardized process for reviewing, validating, ordering, tracking documenting, and completing pending phone and equipment orders.  
The process applies to:

* Clients in Onboarding (OB)  
* Additional phone and equipment orders   
* CSM-related additional equipment orders Orders communicated by Billing  
* Orders assigned through Asana by the Tech OBs or Tech CSMs

2. # **Scope** {#scope}

This SOP covers the process from the initial notification of a pending phone or equipment requirement through:

1. Order identification  
2. Order verification  
3. Equipment selection  
4. Order placement through Teledynamics & NTS Direct  
5. Finance notification  
6. Customer communication  
7. Shipment tracking  
8. Final documentation and record updates

## **Step 2: Access the Deal** {#step-2:-access-the-deal}

Open the applicable Deal associated with the client.  
**Step 3: Review Supporting Documentation**  
Navigate to the attachments available under Agreements. Review the relevant attachment to identify and verify:

* Phone model  
* Quantity  
* Equipment requirements

 

Equipment to be ordered must match the information documented in the applicable attachment

## **Step 4: Access the Teledynamics/NTS Direct Portal** {#step-4:-access-the-teledynamics/nts-direct-portal}

Log in to the Teledynamics/NTS Direct Portal using the credentials provided by the Manager.

**Step 5: Search and Add Equipment**

* Search for the requested phone or equipment model.   
* Add the required equipment to the cart.

Before proceeding to checkout, verify that:

* The correct model has been selected   
* The correct quantity has been added  
* All requested accessories have been included where applicable.

## **Step 6: Proceed to Checkout** {#step-6:-proceed-to-checkout}

* Click the **Checkout** button.

## **Step 7: Configure Shipping Information** {#step-7:-configure-shipping-information}

Under **Ship To**, select:

##### **“Drop Ship Order to my Customer”**

* Select the option to enter the customer's shipping address manually.  
* Enter the shipping address as required for the applicable customer order.  
* The shipping information should be carefully reviewed before placing the order.

## **Step 9: Select Shipping Method** {#step-9:-select-shipping-method}

* Under **Ship Method**, select:

**“UPS Ground Only” “FedEx Ground Only”**

## **Step 10: Verify Billing** {#step-10:-verify-billing}

* Under **Billing**, ensure that the designated billing card/ Use Card on file ending in  
  **8515** is selected.

## **Step 11: Capture Order Evidence** {#step-11:-capture-order-evidence}

Before placing the order:  
Review the complete Order Summary Capture a screenshot of the Order Summary

## **Step 12: Place the Order** {#step-12:-place-the-order}

Place the order after verifying:  
Equipment model, Quantity, Customer shipping information, Return address, Shipping method & Billing selection.

## **Step 13: Create Finance Task** {#step-13:-create-finance-task}

* Create an Asana task for the orders and send or assign it to the Finance Team (Oshin) as established by workflow.


## **Step 14: Update the Tech OB Record** {#step-14:-update-the-tech-ob-record}

In the Tech OB record, under the **Phone Install** section:

* Mark the phones as **Ordered**  
* Update the **Phone Ordered Date**

8. # **Zoho Ticket Handling for Additional Equipment Orders** {#zoho-ticket-handling-for-additional-equipment-orders}

Tickets are created only for:

##### **Additional Phone Equipment Orders assigned to the Imp-VoIP Assets in Zoho Desk.**

The following actions must be completed:  
**Day 1 – Order Placement Communication**  
On the first day of placing the order:

* Send an email to the client confirming the order. The communication should include:  
  Equipment model Quantity ordered

**Day 2 – Tracking Communication**  
On the following day:

* Obtain the tracking number from Teledynamics / NTS Direct once it has been generated.  
* Send the tracking details to the client via email.

**System Updates**  
Update the applicable systems as required:

* Add the required lines in the Adit App.  
* Add the applicable phones in GDMS.  
* Update the applicable CRM records.

**Installation Follow-Up (For VoIP Techs)**  
Assign the ticket to VoIP Tech for the phone installation in the VoIP Tech Group

**Ticket Closure**

* After completing the required actions:  
* Close the Zoho Desk ticket.

9. # **Zoho Desk Ticket Classification Requirements** {#zoho-desk-ticket-classification-requirements}

For applicable additional phone equipment orders, ensure the following ticket details are configured correctly.

| Field | Required Value |
| :---- | :---- |
| Subject Line | Additional Phone Equipment Order \- \<Deal Name\> |
| Classification | Implementation |
| Adit App Module | Voice |
| Implementation Group | Phone Orders/Returns |
| Phone Order/Return Category | Additional Phone Order |

If an Asana task was also assigned for the order, mark the Asana task as complete only after all required actions have been completed and documented.

1. ## **Incorrect Shipping Information** {#incorrect-shipping-information}

If incorrect shipping information is shared or entered: Immediately involve the Reporting Manager or Team Lead. The objective is to determine whether the order can be:

* Rerouted, or Cancelled  
* The escalation must occur as quickly as possible to minimize the risk of the order being shipped to the incorrect location

Before completing the daily order process, confirm the following.

#### **Order Verification**

Correct Deal reviewed

Supporting attachment or Order Form reviewed Correct equipment model verified

Correct quantity verified

#### **Teledynamics Order**

Correct equipment added to cart

Ship To set to customer drop shipment Customer address entered correctly

Teledynamics Address retained as Return Address UPS Ground Only selected

Billing card ending in 8515 verified Order Summary screenshot captured Order successfully placed

#### **Internal Documentation**

Asana task created

Task organized under the applicable daily Order parent task Finance Team notified

Tech OB Phone Install status updated, where applicable Phone Ordered Date updated, where applicable

#### **Additional Equipment Orders**

Client informed of model and quantity

Tracking information obtained and shared when generated Adit App updated

GDMS updated CRM updated

Tentative installation follow-up event created Zoho Desk ticket classified correctly

Ticket closed after all required actions
