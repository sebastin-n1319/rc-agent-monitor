# FOR INTERNAL USE ONLY

## Overview

This article outlines the process for handling client requests to order additional VoIP phones. Since there is no self-service option within Adit for ordering phones, all requests must be manually processed through a support ticket and routed to the appropriate queue.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Confirm the Order Request**  
   * Verify the client’s request.  
   * Confirm the device model(s) they would like to order.  
   * Confirm quantity and any additional relevant details.

2. **Create a Support Ticket**  
   * Open a new ticket in the ticketing system.  
     * Clearly document all relevant details in the ticket comments, including:  
       * Client name and location  
       * Device model requested  
       * Quantity  
       * Any special instructions provided by the client  
       * Tag **@Jay Adesara**   
           
3. **Route the Ticket**  
   * Assign and forward the ticket to the **VoIP Assets Management** queue.  
       
   * Ensure all required information is included before routing to avoid delays.

4. **Notify the Client (if needed)**  
   * Confirm with the client that their request has been submitted to the appropriate team.  
   * Provide any expected next steps if applicable.

# CLIENT-FACING INSTRUCTIONS

## How Clients Can Order Additional Phones

At this time, clients cannot order phones directly through the Adit app. To request additional phones, they may use one of the following methods:

* **Email:** Send the request to **support at adit.com**  
* **Submit a Ticket:** Create a request through our Adit ticketing system  
    
* **Call Adit Support Team:** Contact us at **281-468-1445**

Once the request is received, our team will review the details and coordinate the order.
