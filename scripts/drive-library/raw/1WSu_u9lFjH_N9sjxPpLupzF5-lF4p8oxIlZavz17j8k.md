# **Zoho Desk: How to Authorize the Adit Client Bugs Tab** 

Follow the steps below to authorize the Adit Client Bugs Tab extension in Zoho Desk.   
Once authorized, you will be able to view and manage client bug information directly from any Zoho Desk ticket.

## **Step 1: Open a Zoho Desk Ticket** 

- Navigate to a Zoho Desk ticket.   
- Click the three lines button in the top-right area of the ticket.   
- From the dropdown, select CLIENT BUG INFO.



## **Step 2: Click Authorize in the Client Bug Info Tab**

The Client Bug Info tab will open and display a message: "Your authorization is required to continue." Click the red Authorize button to proceed.



## **Step 3: Authorize the Zoho Desk Connection**

- You will be taken to the Adit Client Bugs Tab extension page.   
- A popup will appear indicating that Authorization is Required.   
- Under the Connections tab, click the Authorize link next to Zoho Desk.



## **Step 4: Connect to Zoho Desk**

- A dialog box will appear for the Zoho Desk service connection.   
- Click the Connect button.   
- This will redirect you to the Zoho Desk login page to grant permission for the connection.



## **Step 5: Accept Permissions**

- A Zoho permissions page will appear listing the data that DRE IDC would like to access.   
- Check the checkbox next to "I allow DRE IDC to access the above data from my Zoho account," then click the Accept button.



## **Step 6: Authorization Successful**

You will see an "Authorized Successfully" confirmation message.



## **Step 7: Return to the Client Bug Info Tab** 

Navigate back to your Zoho Desk ticket and click on the CLIENT BUG INFO tab. If the client bug status hasn’t been entered yet, the tab will display "No client bug details yet"



## **Step 8: Client Bug Info Tab — With Data**

If the client bug status has already been set, the tab will display the full section.
