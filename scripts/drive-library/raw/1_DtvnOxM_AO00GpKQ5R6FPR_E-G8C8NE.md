1. What is Adit Insider?
Adit Insider is a rewards-based engagement app that allows users to:

     ●​    Participate in simple activities
     ●​    Share their experience with Adit
     ●​    Refer other practices
     ●​    Earn Insider Points

These points can then be redeemed for rewards such as gift cards, products, and more.


2. Why Should Our Clients Care?
Adit Insider turns everyday actions into rewards.

     ●​    Many actions take just a few minutes
     ●​    You earn points for activities they already do
     ●​    Points can be redeemed for real rewards
     ●​    The more they participate, the more they earn


3. Quick Start
     1.​ Open the Adit app
     2.​ Go to the Adit Insider tab
     3.​ Click any of the orange buttons
     4.​ Scan the QR code to download the app
     5.​ Note the 4-digit verification code
     6.​ Register in the app using your phone number and code


4. Login & Registration
Step 1: Login

     ●​ Enter your phone number
     ●​ Receive and enter the OTP

Step 2: Registration

Fill in:

     ●​ Username (same as Adit app)
    ●​ First & Last Name
    ●​ Personal Email
    ●​ 4-digit verification code (from Adit app)

Once completed, you’ll land on the Home Screen.


5. Home Screen (Dashboard)
The Home Screen gives you a quick overview of:

    ●​ Your points balance
    ●​ Rewards you can redeem
    ●​ Actions available to earn points

You can navigate the app using the bottom navigation tabs.


6. Navigation Overview
Bottom Tabs:

    ●​   Home – Overview of points, rewards, and actions
    ●​   Refer a Friend – Submit referrals
    ●​   Actions – Earn points
    ●​   Submissions – Track your activity
    ●​   Redeem – Browse and claim rewards

7. Refer a Friend
You can earn points by referring practices to Adit.

How it works:

    1.​ Enter:
            ○​ Practice Name
            ○​ Email
            ○​ Phone Number
            ○​ Relationship
    2.​ Click Submit

You can click Know More for reward details.
8. Actions (Earn Points)
This section shows all available activities.

Example: Leaving a Review

    1.​ Click on an action (e.g., G2 Review)
    2.​ Read instructions
    3.​ Click Submit Review
    4.​ Complete the action externally
    5.​ Upload a screenshot as proof
    6.​ Submit

You’ll see:

      “Your submission is under review”




9. Submissions (Track Progress)
Track all your completed actions.

Status Types:

    ●​ In Review – Under verification
    ●​ Approved – Points awarded
    ●​ Rejected – Needs correction

If Rejected:

    ●​ You can contest the submission
    ●​ Upload additional proof
    ●​ Create a support ticket
