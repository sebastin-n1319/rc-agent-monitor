**Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) – Adit Pay Team**

**Department:** **Adit Pay**

* Handling Support Tickets \- We take inbound calls from clients or call them back if required in order to address/resolve their queries.

* Demos/Trainings \- I handle Adit Pay/Financing Demo and Trainings whenever required and ensure that the process is smooth and hasslefree for the clients.

* Terminal Registration \- We register the terminal once it is delivered to the client and also provide them a short walkthrough on how to process in office payments in addition to training.

* Assisting Tech OB/Tech CSM \- Sometimes we are requested to assist the Tech OBs or Tech CSMs if the client is on call with them and has Adit Pay queries or if they are facing any issues in regards to Adit Pay.


* Creating Asana Task \- I create Asana tasks whenever required and assign it to the respective team for quick and effective resolution of client issues. Some examples of creating tasks are payment posting issues, Client bugs and Terminal/base Station Order.

* Terminal Issues/Troubleshooting \- We also receive calls quite frequently about terminal connectivity issues where the clients face issues to process in office payments.  
  We assist them in troubleshooting and once the issue is identified we resolve that and ensure that the client is updated about it.  
    
    
* Google Chat \- Respond to messages from other groups/individuals and ensure that they are tagged to the concerned person or team.

* Assigning tickets to the Adit Pay team including me and sharing EOD report on Adit Pay/Agent group.
