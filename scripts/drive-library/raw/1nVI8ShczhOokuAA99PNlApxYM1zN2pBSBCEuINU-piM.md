**What is AI Front Desk?**

AI Front Desk is your practice's smartest receptionist, one who never takes a day off, never puts anyone on hold, and never misses a call. It's an add-on module that works around the clock to handle the routine stuff: scheduling, rescheduling, cancellations, and answering those same questions your team gets asked a hundred times a week.

When a caller has something more complex: billing questions, clinical details, insurance specifics. The AI doesn't try to wing it. During business hours, it does a warm handoff to your staff with a quick summary of the conversation. After hours? It creates a prioritized task so nothing falls through the cracks.

It plugs right into what customers already have: Phones, Online Scheduling, and their EHR. That means it pulls real-time availability, knows which insurances they accept, understands their services, and can even share directions all without anyone having to re-enter a single piece of data.

**Why Practices Love It**

**Here's what you'll hear from practices once they've been using it:**

* **"We're finally capturing those after-hours calls."** Every voicemail used to be a gamble, would they call back or just go somewhere else? Now those calls turn into booked appointments.  
* **"No more hold music."** Callers hate waiting. The AI picks up instantly, so they're never left listening to elevator music wondering if anyone's coming back.  
* **"It actually sounds like a person."** The voice is natural, not robotic. Patients often don't even realize they're talking to AI until we tell them.  
* **"My staff can breathe again."** Scheduling calls are repetitive. Freeing the front desk from those means they can focus on patients who are actually standing in front of them.  
* **"I can see exactly what's happening."** Every call gets a transcript, a summary, and a sentiment score. The dashboard shows ROI in black and white, calls answered, appointments booked, new vs. existing patients.

**1\. Before the Call**

**A little homework goes a long way. Before you pick up the phone, take five minutes to understand their situation.**

* High call volume (check their Call Intelligence data)

* Staffing crunches during peak hours

If you can pull a specific number "I noticed 36 missed calls across your locations in the last 15 days” you've got their attention.

Try their cell first, then the office line. If they don't answer, leave a voicemail that's friendly and to the point: "Hey, this is \[Your Name\] from Adit about our scheduled AI Front Desk review. Give me a call back or shoot me a text if you're having trouble joining, I'll send you a text reminder too." Then actually text them.

You don't need to hit every point. Read the room and focus on what matters to them:

**2\. Discovery: The Most Important Part**

**Start with empathy. If there are multiple people on the call, acknowledge everyone: "Glad you could join us Sarah, always good to have more perspectives."**

**Then dig in with questions that get them talking about their pain:**

* "On a scale of 1 to 10, how happy are you with how after-hours calls are handled right now? What happens to those calls after 5 PM?"  
* "How many missed calls are you seeing in your VoIP logs each week? What do you think that's costing you in new patients?"  
* "When your front desk says 'please hold,' how often do callers just hang up?"  
* "What repetitive tasks eat up your team's time that could be spent on patient care?"  
* "Have you ever lost a patient because the phone was busy or nobody picked up?"  
* "If you could capture every call 24/7 without hiring another person, what would that mean for your practice?”  
* "What happens when your front desk is out sick or on vacation? How do you handle the call volume?"  
* "How often does your team get the same questions over and over—insurance acceptance, directions, parking? How much time does that eat up?"  
* "When a new patient calls and you're already on another line, what's your backup plan? Are you confident they'll call back?"

Listen for the triggers: high missed call numbers, people hanging up on hold, voicemails that never get returned, staff feeling overwhelmed. When you hear it, connect the dots: "Based on what you're telling me about those missed calls and voicemails, AI Front Desk would capture all of that. Let me show you exactly how."

**4\. The Demo**

**The demo is everything. This isn't like showing off other modules, you’re letting them experience something that feels almost human. The best approach: walk them through a structured flow, then hand them the number to try it themselves.**

**How the Agent Actually Works**

Before you demo, understand the architecture so you can answer questions confidently. The agent operates entirely on Advanced Online Scheduling APIs. When a call comes in, it pulls real-time availability, insurance acceptance, services, provider info everything flows from what the practice already has configured. No duplicate data entry.

**The call routing is flexible:**

* **After-hours only:** Call goes to agent outside business hours, staff handles everything during the day  
* **Overflow:** If the practice doesn't answer within 20-30 seconds, call rolls to the agent, this catches those "almost missed" calls  
* **Full-time:** Agent handles everything, warm transfers when needed  
* Most practices start with after-hours \+ overflow. That means 90% of their after-hours calls, which are mostly new patients get handled automatically.

**The Live Call Demo**

**Demo number: 832-590-3388 (confirm current number before your call)**

**Have the Client Call it and put it on speaker. Walk through this flow, it mirrors exactly what a new patient would experience:**

1. **Listen to the greeting.** The agent introduces itself with a custom name (practices pick this) and the practice name. Notice how natural it sounds, we’ve tested dozens of voices and selected the four that perform best.  
2. **Say you're a new patient wanting to schedule.** The agent will ask for your first and last name, then ask you to spell them (just like a real receptionist would). Then it asks for date of birth and confirms the phone number you're calling from.  
3. **Go through insurance.** If you say you have BlueCross, the agent checks the practice's accepted insurances. If you say Medicaid and they don't accept it, the agent will politely explain that, and if they have in-house financing or cash pay options configured, it'll mention those.  
4. **Ask for an appointment.** Give a preference like "Tuesday afternoon." The agent pulls real-time availability from Online Scheduling and offers specific slots. If nothing works, it offers alternatives.  
5. **Confirm the booking.** The agent confirms the details and if the practice has it enabled, mentions they'll receive a text confirmation. The appointment gets written directly to the EHR.  
6. **Now throw a curveball.** Ask something the agent won't know: "What school did the doctor go to?" or "Is Dr. Smith board certified?" Watch what happens, the agent doesn't make something up. It says it will have someone from the practice follow up, and creates a task. This is huge.

**What to Show in the Module**

**After the call, share your screen and walk through what just happened on the backend. This is where you prove the value isn't just a cool phone call, it’s actionable data.**

**Home Page (Interactions Feed):**

Each call shows a one-line summary of what happened. Click into the call you just made. They'll see the full transcript, a 3-6 line summary the AI generated, sentiment categorization, and the outcome (booked, created task, transferred, etc.). Point out how fast this processes, within 1-2 minutes of the call ending, everything is populated.

**Tasks:**

Show the task created from your curveball question. Each task includes the patient context, what they asked, and a link to the call recording. Tasks can be assigned round-robin across staff, to a specific person, or by task type (billing questions to one person, scheduling follow-ups to another). It's like having an assistant who takes perfect notes.

**Insights Dashboard:**

Walk through the metrics: total calls handled, after-hours vs. business hours breakdown, scheduling calls booked vs. transferred vs. task-created, and reasons appointments weren't booked. For practices worried about ROI, this page is gold. They can see exactly what the agent is doing for them.

**Preferences (The Customization Page):**

**Show them how much control they have:**

* **Agent name and voice:** They pick a name (Amy, Sarah, whatever fits their brand) and choose from four tested voices  
* **Custom greeting:** They can script exactly how the agent introduces itself  
* **AI disclosure toggle:** Some practices want to disclose it's AI, some don't. We give them the option (and note we don't recommend disclosure, people hang up more when they know)  
* **Practice info:** Parking, directions, anything patients commonly ask  
* **Payment options:** Medicare, Medicaid, CareCredit, FSA, in-house financing, whatever they accept gets added to the agent's knowledge  
* **Services:** Which appointment types the agent can book (keep this focused, 3-5 services max for best performance)  
* **Cancellation/reschedule windows:** If they require 48 hours notice, the agent enforces that  
* **Age restrictions:** If they don't see kids under 14, the agent will politely decline those bookings

**What the Agent Won't Do (And Why That's Good)**

**Be ready to explain what's intentionally limited:**

* **It doesn't guess.** If someone asks a question outside its knowledge base, it creates a task instead of making something up. No hallucinations, no liability.  
* **It filters sales calls.** The agent recognizes solicitation calls and gatekeeps them, doesn’t transfer, just creates a "vendor" task if the practice wants to review later.  
* **Services are kept focused.** We recommend 3-5 service types max. More services \= more context for the AI to process \= slower, less accurate responses. Keep it tight.  
* **Warm transfers respect business hours.** If it's after hours and someone asks for a human, the agent explains no one is available and offers to create a callback task.

**After the demo, connect what they just experienced to their specific pain points: "Remember those 36 missed calls we talked about? Every single one of those would have been answered, qualified, and either booked or turned into a follow-up task. How much revenue do you think that represents?"**
