**Introduction to Adit voice**

Adit Voice is an **All-In-One VOIP Phone System** designed specifically for dental offices to centralize patient communication. It is user-friendly and easy to install, operating using your internet connection. This system is intended to help dental practices move beyond traditional landlines, offering more tools for the front desk for a fraction of the cost, and saying goodbye to missed calls.

### **Key Features and Functionality**

**Centralized Communication Management:**

* Adit Voice allows practices to **manage all calls, text messages, and faxes in one place**.  
* It functions using the existing internet connection and does not require changing the current phone number.  
* Through the Engage Integration, practices can use **one phone number for texting, reminders, and voice**.

**Integration with Practice Management Software:**

* Adit Voice **integrates with dental practice management software** to centralize all patient data.  
* When a patient calls, a **pop-up window appears** on the receptionist's computer screen immediately.  
* This pop-up shows relevant patient information, including the **Patient’s Name, Phone Number, Last & Next Appointment Date, and Balance Due**.  
* Integrating with Adit's Notify Desktop App allows staff to immediately **see the Caller ID** of the inbound caller.

**Advanced Call Handling and Routing:**

* The system allows practices to build **call flows that automatically route calls**.  
* Calls can be routed based on criteria such as the **time of day, call source, or other unique criteria** that work for the practice.  
* This functionality provides enhanced control over how, when, and where calls are routed, moving beyond the capabilities of regular dental office phones, which often only offer caller ID and tone-based routing.

### **Data and Operational Insights**

Adit Voice integrates with Adit's existing Call Tracking feature to collect valuable data and operational insights. This integration **records and stores all phone calls, faxes, and voicemails in one place**.

**Key Insights Provided:**

* Identification of **at what times of the day the practice is missing calls**.  
* Tracking the **number of rings it takes before a staff member picks up the call**.  
* A comparison of staff members who are picking up and making outbound calls.  
* Data on **how often staff members are answering or following up on missed calls**.  
* Insights into **why leads are not converting**.  
* Identification of **which team member brings in more new patients**.

### **AI Technology and Value Optimization**

Adit Voice goes beyond a typical dental VoIP phone system by incorporating **AI technology**.

* AI technology is used to **qualify all inbound and outbound calls**.  
* It provides a dashboard that shows which calls carry the **highest potential value** based on the type of procedure needed.  
* This AI feature ensures that team members know how to **prioritize calls and maximize revenue**.  
* The system helps practices tap into the potential patient pool represented by the 78% of all inbound inquiries to dental offices that typically do not book an appointment.

### **Platform Context**

Adit Voice is one component of the larger **Adit platform**. It is categorized under the "Centralize Communications" module, which also includes Phones & TeleMed, Emails & eFax, Texting & Reminders, and Call Tracking. The overall Adit platform is described as the **All in One AI-Powered Platform**. Promotions are frequently offered, such as **Free phones & VoIP for life** when signing up with Adit.

**What is Adit Voice?**

Adit Voice is a cloud-based VoIP communication platform designed specifically for healthcare practices.  
It unifies phone calls, texts, emails, voicemails, and faxes into one centralized system, giving staff a streamlined and efficient way to manage all patient communications. Integrated directly with your practice management and EHR system, Adit Voice helps teams deliver faster service, reduce missed opportunities, and create a smoother patient experience.  
From AI-powered call scoring to voicemail-to-text transcription and advanced analytics, Adit Voice equips healthcare teams with tools that improve efficiency, boost conversions, and enhance overall operational performance.

**How Does Adit Voice Work?**

Adit Voice works by combining cloud-based VoIP technology, real-time system integrations, and automation tools to simplify communication across your entire practice:

1\. **Cloud-Based Phone System:**

2\. **Unified Communication Hub**

3\. **Real-Time EHR & PMS Integration**

\-Name  
\-Upcoming appointments  
\-Account balance  
\-Past communication history

4\. **Automated Reminders & Messaging**

5\. **Smart Call Routing**  
Custom call flows ensure every call reaches the right person or department based on:  
\-Time of day  
\-Caller type  
\-Custom rules set by the practice

This helps keep wait times low and patient satisfaction high.

6\. **Advanced Analytics & AI Insights**  
Adit Voice tracks call volumes, missed calls, wait times, staffing performance, and more.  
AI evaluates calls, identifies high-value leads, and highlights missed opportunities—helping practices grow and convert more patients.

7\. **Always-On Reliability**  
Even during internet issues or outages, calls can automatically forward to mobile devices, ensuring communication never stops.

# **Port your number(s) to Adit**

Before we get started, please keep in mind that only the Owner-level and Manager-level users are able to port in numbers.  
**Port in your existing Number.**

Typically, when you sign up for Adit there is a section in your first Welcome Questionnaire that asks you for information about any numbers you would like to port. If for some reason your practice still needs to port in a number, please follow the below instructions. These instructions apply to landline numbers, fax numbers  and tracking numbers. 

To Port in your existing number:

1. Click on the **Settings** gear icon at the top right corner of your Adit platform.

2. Select **Phone System** Under the **Voice menu.**.  
   

3. Select the Location  
     
4. Click the “Port-In” button    
     
5. Select the location you are wanting to port the number for and click Continue  
   

6. Fill out the form and click submit. Our team will follow up with you on further instructions and updates on the status of your port. This can typically take 1 to 2 weeks depending on your existing provider.  
   

**How to setup Multiple voicemail boxes**

This guide outlines the **step-by-step process** for setting up and **managing multiple voicemail boxes in the Adit App.**

**Step 1: Log into the Adit App**

* Go to the Adit App and log in using your credentials.

**Step 2: Access Settings**  
From the dashboard, click on the gear icon to open the Settings menu.



**Step 3: Navigate to Number List**

* Under Voice, click on Voicemail Boxes.  
  

**Step 4: Enable Multiple Voicemail Boxes**

* Important: The toggle to enable multiple voicemail boxes can only be activated by the Adit Support Team. The Default Voicemail Box is already present in the VM box.  
* Once enabled :You will see Add Voicemail box option



**(Note: default voicemail settings are limited and shared across all users by default).**

**Step 5: Create New Voicemail Boxes**  
Once multiple voicemail boxes are enabled:

1. Click **“Create New Voicemail Box”**.  
2. Enter a **name** for the voicemail box (e.g., Front Desk).  
3. From the drop-down, assign users who should access this voicemail.  
4. Optionally, enable email and/or text alerts for message notifications.  
5. Click Save.



**You can repeat this process to create voicemail boxes for different teams, roles, or departments.**

**Step 6: Assign Voicemail Boxes in the Call Flow**  
After creating a voicemail box:

1. Go back to **Settings \> Voice \> Voicemail Boxes\> Click on your location name.**  
2. Hover over the landline number and click the pencil icon to edit the call flow.  
3. In the call flow editor, navigate to the part where the voicemail should occur.  
4. Select the appropriate Voicemail Box from the options available under the select voicemail box and through the drop down option you assign the voicemail.  
5. Save the changes.  
* 



**Step 8: Assign Voicemail Boxes Under the Line Section**  
To complete the voicemail setup, it is important to assign the voicemail box to the appropriate **extension/line**:

1. Once **location** is selected, go to the **Lines/ Prefixes** under **Voice** section.  
2. Locate the line that is assigned to the user who should receive the voicemail.  
3. You will see a **Voicemail Box** option for each extension.  
4. From the **drop-down list**, select the voicemail box you previously created (e.g., Front Desk).  
5. Save the changes.  
   





**This will automatically link the voicemail box to that specific extension.**

**Note:**

* **Default Voicemail Box**: This is automatically used unless a custom voicemail box is assigned. It cannot be customized.  
* **User Access**: Only assigned users will have access to messages left in the voicemail box they are assigned to.  
* **Alerts**: Ensure alerts are enabled if you want to be notified of new voicemails via email or SMS.  
* Make sure the same voicemail box is assigned both in: The **Call Flow**, and the **Line settings** of the assigned user/extension.


This ensures that messages are properly routed to the intended user or group.

**For assistance enabling multiple voicemail boxes or if you don’t see the option, please contact the Adit Support Team.**

# **A Guide to Managing Users and Their Access**

The Adit platform makes it easy for you to invite users, manage their permissions, and control access points across multiple applications and locations. 

# **You will need to understand the 4 key roles a user can be added as in the Adit platform.** 

1. **Owner:** Generally the owner of the practice. Has the ability to edit all settings, edit banking information, export data, etc.  
2. **Manager:** Generally the office manager. Has the ability to edit most settings.  
3. **Staff:** Generally the front-desk, associates, and those who would engage with patients.  
4. **Read-Only:** Generally a consultant. Has the ability to view all information but not edit or engage

Please go to this link to understand all of the adit role permissions: [**Understanding Adit's User Role Permissions**](https://app.adit.com/setting/admin/user/rolepermissions?userId=)

If a user is added to the adit app with just access to internal chat, then by default they will be given a read-only user role. 

# **Where to manage user permissions**

1. Click on the Settings gear icon in the top right corner  
     
2. Select Users  
     
3. Clicking on “**Location Access**” will allow you to see all the users and which locations they have access to.  
     
4. Clicking on “**Application Access**” will let you see which users have access to which modules.  
     
5. Please note that all users will have the same level role and same app-level permissions across all locations that they will have access to. 

# **How to add a new user**

1. Click the Add User button   
     
2. Fill in the user’s information  
     
3. Select the applications/modules you would like to provide access to this user  
     
4. Select locations you would like to provide access to this user  
     
5. You can choose to create a password or send the user an email where they can create their own password.  
    

# **How to edit an existing user**

1. Click on the User you want to edit  
2. Use the dropdown menu to edit the role  
     
3. Click on the necessary toggles to change location and module access  
   

***Call Tracking – Feature Overview Document***               

**1\. Introduction**

Call Tracking is an essential feature within the AddIt VoIP system that helps users monitor, analyze, and manage all call-related activities. It provides complete visibility into incoming and outgoing calls, voicemails, faxes, and reporting tools. 

                                

This section is accessible from the Call Tracking icon located on the left blue panel at the bottom of the AddIt app.

**2\. Components of Call Tracking**

Call Tracking consists of the following sections:

* **Call Logs**  
* **Unique Callers**  
* **Voicemails**  
* **Insights**  
* **Faxes**  
* **Leads**  
* **Export Call CSV**  
* **Call Intelligence** 

Each section provides specific details and tools to help users manage communication efficiently.

**3\. Call Logs**

The **Call Logs** section gives a detailed breakdown of each call received or made by your organization. It includes caller information, call duration, timestamps, and actionable tools to review or filter call data.



**1\. Call Volume Graph (Call Volume Breakdown)**

At the top of the Call Logs page, you will see a **Call Volume Breakdown graph**. This graph visually represents:

	•	The total number of calls

	•	Call frequency over selected dates

	•	Trends in call activity

This helps users quickly understand call traffic for the time range selected.

**2\. Call Information Displayed in Call Logs**

Each call entry in the log will show the following details:

**a. Caller Information**

Displays the **caller’s name and phone number**.

If the caller is saved in the system, their name will appear. Otherwise, the number only will be shown.

**b. Location**

Shows the **location/office** where the call was received.

This is useful for multi-location practices or businesses.

**c. Date & Time**

Displays the exact **date and time** when the call occurred.

**d. Patient Type**

Shows the classification of the caller based on their relationship to the business.

(*This is visible for practices using patient categorization.*)

**e. Call Duration**

The total time the call lasted—useful for tracking call handling length and agent performance.

**f. Actions Tab**

Each call log has an **Actions** button that includes:

	•	**Play Recording** – Listen to the recorded call, if call recording is enabled.

	•	**Profile Card** – Displays detailed caller information such as past interactions, notes, and call history.

	•	**Timeline** – Shows a chronological sequence of all interactions with that caller, including calls, messages, voicemails, etc.

These tools help in understanding customer communication history.

**3\. Filters Available in Call Logs**

Filtering allows you to narrow down call data based on specific criteria.

**A. Quick Filters**

You can quickly filter calls by:

	•	**New Patients**

	•	**Existing Patients**

	•	**Scheduling Calls**

	•	**Booked Calls**

	•	**Not Booked Calls**

	•	**Not Resolved Calls**

	•	**Missed Calls**

These filters help you categorize calls and analyze specific call types.

**B. Date & Time Filters**

You can filter call logs based on the date range. Common preset filters include:

* **Today**  
* **Last 7 Days**  
* **Last 30 Days**  
* **Last Month**  
* **Last Year**

You can also select a **custom date range** to search for calls during a specific period.

**C. Add Filter (Advanced Filters)**

Under the **Add Filter** option, you can filter based on:

	•	**Number** (Search using a specific phone number)

	•	**Source**

	•	**Tags**

	•	**Extension**

	•	**Duration** (Search long or short calls)

	•	**Answer Status** (Answered, Missed, Busy, No Answer)

	•	**First-Time Calls**

	•	**Tracking Number**

	•	**Location**

These filters help refine call logs for deeper analysis.

**4\. Table Settings**

The **Table Settings** option allows users to customize which columns they want to display in the Call Logs table. You can toggle visibility for:

	•	**Location**

	•	**Time**

	•	**Source**

	•	**Patient Type**

	•	**Duration**

	•	**Tracking Number**

	•	**Extension Number**

This helps personalize the view based on user preference.

**5\. Export Options (CSV & PDF)**

The Call Logs section also provides export options for reporting or documentation.

**A. Export CSV**

You have two CSV export choices:

	1\.	**Export CSV – Current View**

	•	Exports only the columns visible in your current table settings.

	2\.	**Export CSV – All Columns**

	•	Exports all available call log data regardless of your on-screen view.

**B. Download PDF**

You can export the complete call log data into a **PDF format** for easier sharing or offline reference.

**C. Save View**

Allows you to **save custom table settings \+ filters** so you can quickly access the same configuration later without re-selecting filters.

**4\. Voicemails**

The Voicemail section helps users manage all voicemail messages received on the account.



Voicemails Table will have the following columns. Table can be customized to show based on practice preference

* Caller \- User id of the caller  
* Tracking Number \- The original number that received the voicemail  
* Time \- Time stamp of the voicemail  
* Location \- Location of the organization which received the voicemail  
* Source \- Based on the source from the tracking number that received the voicemail  
* Duration \- Total duration of the voicemail  
* Voicemail Box \- The voicemail box which received the voicemail  
* Voicemails will also have an archive functionality when archived will push the voicemail to the Archived tab. To unarchive click on the unarchive option and it will push the voicemail back to the inbox

**Voicemail Transcription**



* The system automatically converts voicemail audio into **text format**.  
* Benefits include:  
* Quickly reviewing message content without listening to the recording.  
* Improving documentation and faster response times.  
* Accessibility for users who cannot play audio in certain environments.

**Call Summary** is a short, automatically generated overview of the voicemail message.

When the system transcribes a voicemail, it also creates a quick summary so that you can understand the **main purpose of the caller’s message** without listening to the entire voicemail or reading the full transcription.



**What it means:**

* It highlights the **key points** from the voicemail.  
* Gives you a **brief idea** of why the caller contacted you.  
* Helps you **quickly decide** if the voicemail is urgent or what action is needed.  
* Saves time by summarizing the most important details.

**5\. Faxes**

The Fax section stores and manages all incoming and outgoing fax communications.



Details available in Fax Section:

* Location / Fax Number: Which fax number received or sent the fax.  
* Date & Time: When the fax was received or delivered.  
* Sender Information: Shows the number from which the fax was sent.  
* Pages: Number of pages in the fax.  
* Status: Indicates whether the fax was received, delivered, failed, etc.  
* Open Fax File: You can open and view the fax document directly.  
* Archive / Unarchive: Helps in organizing fax records.

**6\. Insights**

The **Insights** section in the Call Tracking module provides a complete analytical overview of your organization’s call performance. It visually displays call trends, call volume, caller behavior, patient types, and call outcomes. This data helps businesses evaluate communication efficiency, identify patterns, and optimize call handling processes.

**1\. Overall Call Summary**

The top section of Insights displays a summarized breakdown of all call activities for the selected time period. These metrics help you quickly understand total call volume and caller behavior.



**A. Total Number of Calls**

This represents the **overall count of all calls** received and made by your practice or business during the selected date range.

It includes:

	•	Incoming calls

	•	Outgoing calls

	•	Answered, missed, and busy calls

This number gives you an overall picture of call traffic.

**B. Incoming Calls**

This metric shows the **total number of calls your business received** from customers or patients.

Incoming calls include:

	•	Answered calls

	•	Missed calls

	•	Calls missed during business hours

Monitoring incoming calls helps evaluate how well your team handles customer inquiries.

**C. Outgoing Calls**

This displays the **total number of calls made by your team** to customers, patients, or leads.

Outgoing calls may include:

	•	Follow-ups

	•	Appointment confirmations

	•	Reminders

	•	Call backs

Tracking outgoing calls helps measure outreach and follow-up productivity.

**D. Unique Callers**

Unique callers represent the **total number of individual people** who contacted your business during the selected timeframe.

Even if a person calls multiple times, they are counted **once** in this section.

This helps identify:

	•	How many different customers reached out

	•	First-time caller trends

	•	Repeat engagement levels

**E. Unique Recipients**

This shows the **number of unique people your team dialed out to**.

It includes:

	•	New outbound contacts

	•	Follow-ups to different individuals

	•	Callbacks to patients or leads

This metric helps understand outbound communication reach.

**2\. Incoming Calls Breakdown**

The Insights section includes a dedicated breakdown of incoming calls to show call outcomes.

**A. Total Incoming Calls**

This is the total number of inbound calls received from all customers during the selected date period.

**B. Incoming Calls Answered**

Shows how many inbound calls were successfully answered by your team.

This helps measure staff responsiveness and call handling efficiency.

**C. Incoming Calls Missed**

This displays the total number of calls that **rang but were not answered**.

Tracking missed calls helps identify staffing gaps, peak call times, and areas requiring improvement.

**D. Incoming Calls Missed During Business Hours**

This is a critical metric.

It shows how many calls were missed **during your official working hours**.

These missed calls often indicate:

	•	Missed opportunities

	•	Understaffing during peak hours

	•	Customer dissatisfaction risks

This helps businesses take corrective action in real time.

***How to Create and Use a Duplicate Call Flow in Adit***

**Overview**

A duplicate call flow is useful when:

* A client wants to create a backup of their existing call flow  
* The main call flow was accidentally deleted  
* The client wants to create a test version before applying changes to the main number

This article explains how to create, copy, and apply a duplicate call flow to a landline number or tracking number.

**1\. How to Access the Call Flow Section**

To view and manage call flows:

* Log in to your **Adit app**.  
* Click on the **Settings (gear) icon**.  
* Go to **Call Flow Templates** under the **Voice** section.  
* Select the **Location Name** from the Drop menu on the top Left Corner   
    
* Once selected, it will expand all available sections.  
* Click on **Call Flow**.  
* This is where duplicate call flows, backup flows, or test flows can be created and managed.

**2\. How to Create a Duplicate Call Flow**

If a client wants to prepare a backup or a test version:

* In the **Call Flow** section, click on **Create Call Flow**.  
* Enter a name for the new call flow for example:  
* *Test Call Flow*  
* *Main Flow Copy*  
* *Backup Flow*  
* Build or replicate the call flow as needed.  
* Save the call flow once all changes are completed.

This duplicate call flow can be used to restore the main flow if the original is ever deleted.

**3\. How to Copy an Existing Call Flow to the Main Line Number**

* If the **main call flow was deleted**, you can restore it using the duplicate call flow:  
* Open the **duplicate call flow** from the Call Flow section.  
* Scroll to the bottom of the pageant  click the **Copy Flow** button.  
* A list of numbers will appear.  
* Select the **main landline number** to which you want the call flow applied.  
* Confirm and save the changes.  
* This will copy the entire call flow setup to the main number.

**4\. How to Apply the Duplicate Call Flow to a Tracking Number**

If a client wants a tracking number to use the newly created duplicate call flow:

* Go to **Settings**.  
* Under **Voice ,**  Click on **Tracking numbers**  
* In the **Action** column, click the **Pencil (edit) icon**.  
* Under **Route To**, select **Call Flow**.  
* A dropdown will appear choose the **call flow** you created.  
* Scroll down and click **Save** at the bottom.

This updates the tracking number so it now uses the selected duplicate call flow.

**5\. When Should You Use a Duplicate Call Flow?**

A duplicate or backup call flow is recommended when:

* Clients frequently change their call flow and want a safe version to revert to the original call flow was deleted and needs to be restored  
* Testing is required without affecting live calls  
* Separate call patterns for tracking numbers need to match the main line’s structure

**Troubleshooting Quick Reference**

**Phone won't power on:**

·       Check power adapter connection to phone and outlet

·       If using PoE, verify the switch/router is PoE-compliant and powered

·       Try a different electrical outlet

**No network connection:**

·       Check ethernet cable is fully inserted into Internet port

·       Verify cable is connected to a powered switch or router

·       If using WiFi, check WiFi is enabled and network is visible

·       Verify DHCP server is active on your network

**WiFi won't connect:**

·       Verify WiFi is enabled in Menu \> Settings \> Basic Settings \> WiFi

·       Check network name and password are correct

·       Move closer to WiFi router if signal is weak

·       Try forgetting the network and reconnecting

**Poor call quality:**

·       Move closer to WiFi router if using wireless

·       Switch to wired Ethernet connection if experiencing issues

·       Check for background noise and use mute when not speaking

·       Ensure adequate bandwidth on network

·       Enable quality of service (QoS) settings on your router

**Bluetooth device won't pair:**

·       Ensure device is in pairing mode

·       Bring device closer to the phone

·       Clear previous pairing attempts and try again

·       Check device is compatible with Bluetooth 4.2

**Calls won't transfer:**

·       Verify target extension is valid and in service

·       Try attended transfer instead of blind transfer

·       Check Call Forward settings aren't interfering

**Voicemail not working:**

·       Confirm voicemail service is provisioned on your account

·       Verify Message key is functional

·       Check password is correct (default is extension number)

**USB recording not working:**

·       Verify USB drive is properly inserted

·       Check recording feature is enabled in settings

·       Ensure USB drive has sufficient space

·       Try a different USB device to rule out device compatibility

**Troubleshooting Quick Reference**

**Phone won't power on:**

·       Check power adapter connection to phone and outlet

·       If using PoE, verify the switch/router is PoE-compliant and powered

·       Try a different electrical outlet

**No network connection:**

·       Check ethernet cable is fully inserted into Internet port

·       Verify cable is connected to a powered switch or router

·       If using WiFi, check WiFi is enabled and network is visible

·       Verify DHCP server is active on your network

**WiFi won't connect:**

·       Verify WiFi is enabled in Menu \> Settings \> Basic Settings \> WiFi

·       Check network name and password are correct

·       Move closer to WiFi router if signal is weak

·       Try forgetting the network and reconnecting

**Poor call quality:**

·       Move closer to WiFi router if using wireless

·       Switch to wired Ethernet connection if experiencing issues

·       Check for background noise and use mute when not speaking

·       Ensure adequate bandwidth on network

·       Enable quality of service (QoS) settings on your router

**Bluetooth device won't pair:**

·       Ensure device is in pairing mode

·       Bring device closer to the phone

·       Clear previous pairing attempts and try again

·       Check device is compatible with Bluetooth 4.2

**Calls won't transfer:**

·       Verify target extension is valid and in service

·       Try attended transfer instead of blind transfer

·       Check Call Forward settings aren't interfering

**Voicemail not working:**

·       Confirm voicemail service is provisioned on your account

·       Verify Message key is functional

·       Check password is correct (default is extension number)

**USB recording not working:**

·       Verify USB drive is properly inserted

·       Check recording feature is enabled in settings

·       Try a different USB device to rule out device compatibility

# **Updated Schedule Call Flow** 

| Category: | Call Routing / Schedule Configuration | Applies To: | Offices with lunch closures |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |

 

## **Overview**

This article explains the updated call schedule structure for offices that close during lunch. The new three-branch setup replaces the previous two-branch method, making schedules easier to build, read, and troubleshoot.  
 

## **What Changed**

| Previous Setup (2 Branches) | New Setup (3 Branches) |
| ----- | ----- |
| Branch A — Morning \+ Afternoon hours Branch B — Lunch hours (voicemail) *Each weekday is entered twice in Branch A* | Branch A — Before lunch Branch B — Lunch hours (voicemail) Branch C — After lunch *One entry per weekday per branch* |

 

## **Why it changed:** 

The reason for this update:

With the previous setup, the schedule had opening and closing hours configured within the same branch. This meant that in Branch A, we had to create duplicate schedules for each day (for example, two Mondays and two Tuesdays).

For example:

* The office opened at 7:00 AM and closed at 12:00 PM for lunch in Branch A.  
* Branch B was then used only for the lunch hours from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.  
* After lunch, the office reopened from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM in Branch A again.

With this setup, calls were routed back to Branch A after lunch hours, which created a second leg in the call flow. This caused issues with call routing, and at times, the phones did not ring properly, leading to the issues reported by the client.

With the new setup, we have eliminated the need to route calls back into the same branch after lunch hours. Instead, the new Branch C is being used for the reopening schedule, which provides a cleaner call flow and helps maintain proper ringing functionality.

* After entering the forwarding number, make sure to click "OK" or "Apply" to save your changes.  
* Double-check that the settings have been applied correctly.

# **VoIP Audio Quality Issues: Common Causes, Investigations and Fixes**

The key for solving all audio quality issues is to try to identify a pattern 

- Time of call, carrier, phone instrument, provider, call duration, geographic location etc.  
- Same issue is affecting one client (at a particular time/different times)  
- Same client facing multiple issues  
- Multiple clients facing same issue  
  .


1. ### Call Drop

   Investigation: (Try to identify a pattern)

- Does the call drop soon after the call connects \- like within the first 30 seconds or so?  
- Do the calls drop after the call is placed on mute/hold by either or both sides?  
- Calls drop at random time on the call while speaking \- but mostly during peak hours of the day  
    
    
  Calls drop after 30 seconds:  
    
- Most likely ACK message is not received to complete the SIP Invite  
- This may happen from time-to-time because UDP is “best-effort” protocol and not a guaranteed delivery protocol  
- **Solution:** We can change the SIP protocol to TCP (for this client) if this happens frequently for a particular client  
- If it still doesn’t solve the problem then we should look at the NAT and Firewall 

	Calls drop after being placed on mute/hold  
	

- This is mainly due to RTP timeout(setting on our PBX) or UDP timeout \- basically it is assumed that either party is not available because they left the call but BYE packet was lost and hence the call is cancelled by PBX or UDP connection is closed by client’s router because there was no UDP traffic  
- Please reach out to the development team with this observation  
- **Solution (Developers):** Try changing Silence Suppression and VAD settings on Phones. This will send periodic RTP packets to keep connection open

  Calls drop during peak time (Try to gather more information around source numbers, carriers, providers (plivo/telnyx), VHTelephony Servers


- Could be Adit PBX Load, Telnyx, Plivo or upstream carrier  
- If PBX load related then it would affect different clients on that server during peak times but not the same client  
- If it is affecting the same client then could be Telnyx/Plivo issue 


2. ### Audio Quality (Audio In/Out, Robotic Voice, Static, Crackling Sound, Inaudible Audio, Delayed Audio)

	

- Mostly due to Jitter/Delta on client network  
- Sometimes can be due to PBX server load/Telnyx/Plivo/Upstream Carrier  
- **Solution 1:** Change the codec to PCMU/PCMA. It requires lower bandwidth compared to OPUS 48000  
- **Solution 2:** Change the Jitter Buffer on the phone to “Fixed Buffer” with 100ms jitter. Don’t increase this more than 100ms otherwise the client will experience voice lag  
- **Solution 3:** Check if this happens during peak hours and which PBX server is affected. Further investigation to be done by Developers   
- **Solution 4:** Check client’s available bandwidth (download and upload). Do an actual file upload/download instead of doing standard “speed test”. If that is not possible then do it with multiple sites. Preferably during the time when they are facing problems

3. ### One-way Audio/Audio cuts out completely from one/both sides but call is still connected

- Need to be extra-careful in differentiating one-way audio from delayed audio. True one-way audio issues are rare. The user could just say “hello, hello” 3-4 times and then hang up where they could have received audio if they had stayed on call. These are not one-way audio issues but delayed voice  
- **Investigation:** Ask the users to stay on the line for a few more seconds (10+ seconds) to check if they are still not receiving any audio  
- Mainly due to NAT/Firewall issues  
- UDP packets are blocked by NAT/Firewall on the client (clinic) side  
- **Solution:** Recheck all NAT/Whitelisting/Firewall settings  
- **Solution:** Enable STUN based NAT on the phones. Both Grandstream and Yealink phones have this option    
- **Solution:** Increase UDP Port Range \- allowing more UDP connections  
- **Solution:** Implement QoS to prioritise RTP traffic  
- **Solution (for large practices):** Implement VLAN with QoS to prioritise Voice Traffic   
- **Solution:**  If it affects multiple clients at the same time, then it could be due to the PBX load but it’s unlikely. Please gather as much information as possible (time of call, provider, carrier etc) to form a pattern and reach out to the developer
