# Kickoff Call Process

## Purpose

The Kickoff Call serves as an introduction and helps set the client’s expectations for the rest of the onboarding process. This time must be utilized effectively to establish a trusting, collaborative relationship between the Onboarding Specialist and the client.

## Before the Kickoff

Within one business day of the Kickoff Call, research your client. This is your first opportunity to get to know your client, their office, and their processes. Review the information available in CRM on the Account, Deal, and Tech Stack records, as well as what has already been updated in the Tech OB record.

Note any questions you have as you research and any points of clarification you’d like to discuss in more detail. Have recommendations ready for configuring the platform to meet the client’s stated needs and goals.

Talk to the AE as well. They should have provided notes in CRM for you, but they have also worked with the client throughout the signup process and may have some insights that will be helpful.

Here are some things you should take note of in your research:

* Do they have 3rd party IT? Do we have contact info for this 3rd party?  
* Are they using Adit Voice? What about Practice Analytics? Pay? RCM? Call Intelligence?  
* What modules or features do they appear to be interested in?  
* What EHR/PMS software do they use?  
* Look through their website. Do they currently offer online booking, patient forms, or payments? Make a note if you think their website is outdated or difficult to navigate; they may be a good candidate for our marketing services later\!

Confirm that the necessary account setup has been completed. These should have been done in the Get Started phase (before Onboarding), but if any are missing you may need to follow up:

* CRM is accurate \- POCs have been added as Contacts on the Account/Deal; Deal name is the practice name, etc.  
* Organization/Location is set up in the Adit App and named correctly.  
* Business Hours set up.  
* Receptionist Email added.  
* Logo uploaded.  
* Outbound number selected.  
* Server app installed and syncing.  
* GMB page connected.  
* Users created.

Confirm that all required items for VoIP are set up or in place:

* In CRM:  
  * Check phone tracking (in TO-record), make sure they have been delivered or will be before the call.  
  * Check that phone info (Phones records) matches GDMS.  
  * Confirm we know who their current phone provider is and if they are porting any numbers. Do we know how to do call forwarding for this provider?  
  * Review the IT Details on the Deal level, confirm all numbers listed on the Invoice / CSR match what we will be porting over. If additional numbers are listed on the document but not added in the porting record, review with the client and get added if required.   
* In Adit App:  
  * Check that phone info (in Lines) matches GDMS.  
  * Make sure each line has an actual practice user associated with it, not the admin user with the @adit.com email.  
  * Configure the basic Call Flow (simulring all lines for 45 seconds, then go to voicemail with an appropriate message) for any landline that is currently in service. This will be their temp number as indicated in CRM (if they are porting their landline) or their permanent phone number (if they are not porting a landline).

Send an email to the client reminding them of the meeting and include the Google Meet link. Make sure their IT is copied on that email if they will need to attend the meeting as well. If any of the account setup items in the list above are missing, remind the client to bring that information with them to the meeting. If necessary, include the Phone Info template as well.

## Kickoff Call

The Kickoff Call is, first and foremost, a conversation. Use the time to get to know the client, their needs, and their goals for their practice; this information will be invaluable as you build a relationship with them over the next few weeks. The Adit platform is overwhelming; do not expose the client to all of it in this first call.

This time is also a key opportunity to get the client excited about using our platform. Match their enthusiasm. Avoid negative answers \- for example, if they ask about a feature or function that we can’t do, approach the answer with positivity (“Yes, but we have to do it in a slightly roundabout way…”) instead of just telling them we can’t do it.

Below is a standard agenda that should work for nearly all Kickoff Calls. You don’t need to follow it precisely, but you can use it as a guideline to ensure you touch upon all of the important talking points the client needs to know. The sections highlighted in orange are examples of the types of questions you should be asking during this conversation; work them in naturally and allow the client to answer each one thoroughly. Getting to know your clients in this way is the primary goal of the Kickoff Call.

#### Kickoff Agenda

* ##### Introductions

  * If there is anyone in the call that you haven’t already met, ask them to introduce themselves\! What is their role, how long have they been at the practice, etc.

* ##### Review Onboarding Process

  * Let the client know what to expect during the onboarding process:  
    * Other teams/people they may hear from: tech support teams, PA team, VoIP team, etc.  
    * Expected timeline: Full onboarding should take about a month. They will be up and running with our core platform in about 10 days; after that, it’s just about making sure they are comfortable and using the other parts of the platform before we shift them to our CSM team. The goal is to ensure they are all set up and using Adit thoroughly before their billing starts.  
    * That there are three major planned meetings (including this one).  
    * Total amount of time they will spend on calls/meetings with you is about 6-8 hours total over the course of the entire onboarding process.  
  * Explain the structure/flow of their specific onboarding, which will vary based on their package.  
    * Phase 1: Core Platform (weeks 1-2)  
      * Today: Kickoff Call and phone install.  
      * Tomorrow: OB will do their preliminary configuration (call flow, Pozative, eFax, auto-reminders, patient forms).  
      * (Scheduled Date): Config Call, where you will review what you’ve set up for them, make any necessary changes to that configuration, and teach them how to update their settings when they need to in the future.  
      * (Scheduled Date): Staff Training on core platform and day-to-day use.  
        * Invite anyone in the office who will be using any part of the Adit platform, including the phones.  
        * Training will be recorded and the recording provided to the client so team members who could not attend live can watch. They can also use it to train new members of their team.  
    * Phase 2: Adoption (weeks 2 through 6\)  
      * Analytics training with PA team (if their package includes Analytics).  
      * Adit Pay evaluation with Pay team.  
      * Conversations about remaining modules, including configuration and training.  
      * Additional Q\&A for staff, if needed.  
      * Regular check-in calls with OM.  
      * Once they have been trained, the active part of onboarding is over and the rest of the process is simply making sure they are knowledgeable, comfortable, and using the platform.

* ##### Phone Install

  * Let the client know that you will now help them install their phones. If they need their IT person for this, make sure they are added to the call.  
  * Make sure the client has watched the 1-minute “How to connect your Adit phones” video that you sent them previously.  
  * Install the phones. For detailed instructions, see How to Install Adit Phones.  
    1. Plug in one of each phone model.  
    2. Wait for them to update.  
    3. Test the installed phones.  
    4. Set up call forwarding.  
    5. Caller ID verification, see Caller ID Verification  
    6. Have the client (or their IT) plug in the remaining phones.  
       * Continue with Call Flow and training while you wait for the rest of the phones to update.  
    6. Configure their Call Flow.  
    7. Teach the client how to use the new hardphones.  
       * Hold / unhold  
       * Transfer (attended/warm and unattended/cold)  
       * Intercom / call another office extension  
       * Check voicemail  
       * Park a call and pick up a parked call  
       * Conference call / three-way call  
       * Call override  
    8. Test the remaining phones and the entire system.  
  * If you run into any issues with the phone installation or testing, immediately message your VoIP tech in your Pod chat.

* ##### Get to Know the Client

  * This is the most important part of the Kickoff Call.  
  * Ask open-ended questions and let them talk.  
    * What are their goals for the practice?  
    * How large is the practice? How many operatories, doctors, hygienists, assistants, support staff?  
    * Where in their community are they located? An office inside of a busy shopping mall will have different needs than one in a very rural community.  
    * What kind of patients make up the majority of their patient base? Elderly patients may prefer to communicate differently than young families or college students.  
    * Why did they choose Adit?  
    * What parts of the Adit platform are they the most interested in or excited about?  
    * What parts of the Adit platform are they not interested in using, and why? (i.e. Pozative because they don’t want negative reviews, OS because they don’t want patients to be able to schedule just anything.) You will be able to use their fears/concerns to provide customized configuration options that will allow them to use that feature without the downside that they anticipate.  
    * What did they like or dislike most about their previous software, if any? Think of ways we can match the things they liked, and solve for the things they disliked.  
    * What parts of their day do they find the most tedious or frustrating? Is there a feature within our platform that will solve that problem for them?  
    * What else do they want us to know about their practice, their style, their personality, or their staff?  
  * Be sure to ask any questions that you noted while you were researching.  
  * Gather information about their current setup, if they have one, or what they would like to set up:  
    * How do they communicate with their patients?  
    * What timing/methods do they like to use for appointment reminders, recalls, unscheduled treatment?  
    * Do they offer online appointment booking, patient forms, or payments?  
    * What patient forms do they use regularly?  
    * How many people are at the front desk? Who is involved with patient communication?  
    * Do they have any unusual setup requirements, such as staff working remotely or additional locations?  
    * Do they have any critical deadlines that we need to follow, such as end of service with another vendor?  
  * The conversation you have had up until this point should have provided you with most of the information you need to do a preliminary configuration. If you need more info, you can ask them a limited number of direct questions about the Core features:  
    * Auto Reminders  
    * Patient Forms  
    * Pozative  
    * Online Scheduling  
  * If the client is particularly interested in a feature that is not part of the Core platform (see previous bullet point), you can answer any questions they have about it and help maintain their enthusiasm while reassuring them that we will cover it in the second half of their onboarding. We want to get them comfortable with our Core operations before we introduce more advanced features.  
  * If they say something in any of the above conversation that you think will lead to a specific use-case, ask them to go into more detail about that specific subject.

* ##### Confirm Next Meetings

  * Confirm the date and time of their Config Call (which should be the next day) and their Staff Training (which should be the day after that).

* ##### Final Thoughts

  * Recap anything that the client still needs to send you, and anything you will be working on for them.  
  * Remind them that our goal is to get them set up as quickly and smoothly as possible to ensure minimal disruption to their patients.  
  * Ask if they have any questions. Listen and answer thoughtfully.  
  * Thank the client for their time\!

## After the Kickoff

All of the post-kickoff work is done by the OB Specialist.

#### Send Follow Up Email

Send the client a follow-up email promptly after the call concludes. Thank them again for their time, reiterate the importance of their participation and cooperation in the onboarding process, and remind them of the scheduled dates of their upcoming meetings. Send the phone guide again, if necessary.

You should also recap the conversation you had and give a quick bullet-point list of things that were important to them to show that you were paying attention to their needs and goals.

#### Update CRM

Make sure that the Account and Deal in CRM are updated with any additional points of contact they provided in the call, including their IT if necessary.

Make sure the **Sched Config Call Date** (for the config call) **Sched Staff Training Date** (for Staff training) are correct on the TO-record. Fill in any notes in the **Notes for OB** field and update the **Next Touchpoint Date** to the next date you will need to take action on this client. If the Kickoff portion of the call was completed, update the **Kickoff Call Complete** field to Yes. Update the **Phone Install Result** and **Call Flow Configured** fields based on the result of your call.

If the Kickoff Call was completed **and** the Phone Install was successful during this call, click the **Phone Install Successful** transition on the TO-record in CRM. This will update the TO-record stage to Pending Config.

If the Phone Install failed or was otherwise not completed on this call, but the Kickoff portion was completed, the VoIP team will be notified so they can step in and communicate directly with the client to resolve any underlying issues. They will update you when the phone install has been completed successfully.

If the client is not using Adit Voice and did not complete a phone install, the transition will be **Kickoff Complete**; in this case, the TO-record stage will move directly to Pending Config.

If neither the Kickoff Call nor the Phone Install was completed successfully, be sure to reschedule both parts. The TO-record stage will remain Pending Phone Install or Pending Kickoff until they are completed successfully.

#### Planning Next Steps

Shortly after the Kickoff Call, take some time to summarize your thoughts on the client and the information that they provided during your conversation. Think about how to incorporate our tools into their workflow and how we can demonstrate the value of our product and the cost to their business of not using it. You will need to do a lot of critical thinking here, but it will help you plan how to approach training and future adoption conversations with this client. Include this information in your notes in the TO-record.
