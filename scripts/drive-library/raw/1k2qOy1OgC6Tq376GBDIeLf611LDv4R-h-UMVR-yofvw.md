# **SECTION 1 — Connecting Your Adit Phone**

Adit supports three hardware phone models. Follow the steps below for your specific model to complete the initial setup.

| 📱 Model GRP2613 — Corded Desktop Phone |
| :---- |

Connecting your GRP2613 is a simple 4-step process.

| 🎬  Tutorial Video:  [Watch on YouTube — GRP2613 Connection Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fCC7qMsIQZU) |
| :---- |

1. Find the ethernet cable currently connecting your computer to the wall socket or router.

2. Unplug the end connected to your computer and plug it into the LAN port on the back of the phone.

3. Connect an additional ethernet cable from your computer into the PC port on the back of the phone.

4. Connect the 12V DC plug to the power jack on the phone, and plug the other end into a wall outlet.

| 📱 Model GRP2616 — Corded Desktop Phone (Expanded) |
| :---- |

Connecting your GRP2616 follows the same 4-step process as the GRP2613.

| 🎬  Tutorial Video:  [Watch on YouTube — GRP2616 Connection Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fCC7qMsIQZU) |
| :---- |

5. Find the ethernet cable currently connecting your computer to the wall socket or router.

6. Unplug the end connected to your computer and plug it into the LAN port on the back of the phone.

7. Connect an additional ethernet cable from your computer into the PC port on the back of the phone.

8. Connect the 12V DC plug to the power jack on the phone, and plug the other end into a wall outlet.

| 📱 Model DP720 — Cordless Handset (with DP750 Base Station) |
| :---- |

Setting up the DP720 cordless phone requires pairing with the DP750 base station. This is a 5-step process.

| 🎬  Tutorial Video:  [Watch on YouTube — DP720 Connection Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9JMWqpsHX3s) |
| :---- |

9. Insert batteries into the DP720 handset and place it in the charging base. Plug the base into an outlet. Press and hold the End key to power on the handset.

10. Connect the micro-USB connector into the power port of the DP750 base station and plug the other end into a wall outlet.

11. Connect one end of an ethernet cable into the internet port on the DP750, and the other end into your router.

12. Once the DP750 is powered on, the radio icon should start blinking. If it does not blink, press and hold the radio button on the back of the base station for 7 seconds until it begins. This starts the subscription pairing process.

13. On the DP720 handset, press the Subscribe button. The two devices will begin pairing automatically.

# **SECTION 2 — Using Your Adit Phone**

The following reference covers day-to-day call actions for each phone model. Where steps differ between models, they are documented separately.

| 📱 Model GRP2613 — Call Action Reference |
| :---- |

### **Hold — Placing & Picking Up**

* Press the Hold button to place an active call on hold.

* Press the Hold button again to resume the call.

* A call on hold can only be picked up from the same phone that placed it.

*📌 Note: When managing multiple calls simultaneously, use the flashing line buttons on the phone screen to switch between them.*

### **Hold Music**

Hold music is managed through the Adit platform, not the phone hardware.

* Navigate to: Settings → Numbers → Hold Music

### **Transferring a Call**

* Press the Transfer button on the phone.

* Dial the 3-digit extension or full 10-digit phone number for the transfer destination.

### **Calling Another Phone in the Office**

Simply dial the extension number of the phone you would like to reach.

### **Checking Voicemail**

**✅ PREFERRED:** Use the Adit platform's Visual Voicemail feature instead of checking directly on the phone.

In the Adit platform, voicemail can be accessed from:

* The "What's Happening" section on the Home page

* The Call Pop icon in the top Menu Bar

Visual Voicemail lets you:

* Quickly identify which voicemail you need without listening through all messages sequentially.

* Access archived voicemails and eFaxes in the same location.

To check voicemail directly on the GRP2613 (not preferred):

* Press the Message (envelope) button.

* Press the round Select button in the centre of the direction pad.

* Voicemail messages will begin playing automatically.

### **Parking a Call**

* Press the Park soft key. The call will park into the designated slot.

* A red light will appear across all phones in the location to indicate a parked call.

### **Picking Up a Parked Call**

A parked call can be picked up from any desktop phone at the same location.

* Press the steady red light on any phone to answer the parked call.

### **Making an Outbound Call While a Call Is Parked**

* Press an available extension button first (e.g., Ext 1).

* Then pick up the handset.

*📌 Note: If the handset is picked up without first pressing an empty extension, the phone will pick up the parked call by default.*

### **Receiving an Inbound Call While a Call Is Parked**

* Press the inbound call extension FIRST, then pick up the handset.

* If the handset is picked up without first pressing the inbound extension, the phone will default to picking up the parked call.

*📌 Note: Do not leave a patient parked for more than 60 seconds. If a call remains parked beyond 60 seconds, it will return as an inbound call to the phone that originally parked it. While a call is parked, none of the phones will ring for new inbound calls — the first available extension will blink instead.*

| 📱 Model GRP2616 — Call Action Reference |
| :---- |

The GRP2616 operates identically to the GRP2613 for all call actions. Refer to the GRP2613 section above for full steps. Key actions are summarised below for quick reference.

### **Hold — Placing & Picking Up**

* Press the Hold button to place a call on hold; press again to resume.

* A held call can only be resumed from the same phone.

*📌 Note: Use the flashing line buttons to switch between multiple active/held calls.*

### **Hold Music**

* Settings → Numbers → Hold Music (managed in Adit platform).

### **Transferring a Call**

* Press the Transfer button, then dial the 3-digit extension or 10-digit number.

### **Calling Another Phone in the Office**

Dial the extension of the phone you wish to reach.

### **Checking Voicemail**

**✅ PREFERRED:** Use Visual Voicemail in the Adit platform (Home page or Call Pop).

To check directly on the GRP2616:

* Press the Message button → Press the round Select button → Messages play automatically.

### **Parking a Call**

* Press the Park soft key to park the call. Red light appears across all phones.

### **Picking Up a Parked Call**

* Press the steady red light on any phone at the same location.

### **Outbound / Inbound Calls While a Call Is Parked**

* Outbound: Press an available extension first, then pick up handset.

* Inbound: Press the inbound extension first, then pick up handset — otherwise the parked call is picked up by default.

*📌 Note: 60-second park limit applies. Parked call returns to originating phone after 60 seconds. Inbound calls show as blinking extensions, not rings, while a call is parked.*

| 📱 Model DP720 — Cordless Handset Call Action Reference |
| :---- |

### **Hold — Placing & Picking Up**

* While on an active call, press the right soft key to place the call on hold.

* Press the right soft key again to resume the call.

### **Hold Music**

* Managed via the Adit platform: Settings → Numbers → Hold Music.

### **Transferring a Call**

* Press Options on the handset.

* Select Transfer.

* Dial the 3-digit extension or full 10-digit phone number for the transfer destination.

### **Calling Another Phone in the Office**

Dial the extension number of the phone you wish to reach.

### **Checking Voicemail**

**✅ PREFERRED:** Use Visual Voicemail in the Adit platform (Home page or Call Pop).

To check directly on the DP720 (not preferred):

* Press the Message button → Press the round Select button → Messages play automatically.

### **Parking a Call**

* Press the left soft key and select Blind Transfer.

* Enter 901 or 902 and press Transfer.

* The call is now parked.

### **Picking Up a Parked Call**

Once parked, the call can be picked up from any phone in the same location.

* On a cordless handset, dial 901 or 902 and press Dial to pick up the parked call.
