## **Practice Analytics, area by area**

> * Set Goals / Preferences: The place where the practice sets and edits production and other KPI goals (daily, weekly, monthly) under Preferences (3 dots \> Preferences \> Set Goals); supports per-provider goals, default goals preset by Adit, and a weekly template or advanced settings so goals can differ by day of week, applied for the next 6 months. (PA-0308 · 50:55; on about 762 calls)  
> * Yesterday / Past Huddle: A Morning Huddle view of a completed prior day (previous 24 hours, midnight to midnight, or any selected past date) showing completed appointments, new patients, production, collections, unscheduled future appointments, and reappointment counts; many numbers are blue links that open related reports. (PA-0001 · 07:43; on about 741 calls)  
> * Preferences / Team Settings: Add or remove providers and hygienists (by EHR ID), map them to operatories, and set provider working days/hours by weekday including lunch breaks and weekly templates, so PA can track provider and operatory performance and calculate production-per-hour and utilization. (PA-0680 · 46:22; on about 350 calls)  
> * Patient List: Create custom patient lists with filters, save, refresh, edit, and delete them; run bulk actions such as SMS, email, follow-ups, appointment/form/review/recall/payment requests, and reminders; export to Excel/CSV; also use prebuilt popular lists. (PA-0001 · 09:42; on about 282 calls)  
> * Operatory Utilization: Shows how much time each provider spent in which operatory, and how much each operatory is utilized, underutilized, or overutilized; includes available/occupied/lost chair time, hours worked/busy/lost, and projected lost revenue; requires providers and hours to be mapped to operatories. (PA-0452 · 31:34; on about 265 calls)  
> * Default Practice Analytics landing page showing today's/next-24-hour schedule with appointment count, expected/scheduled production, production goal, new patients, unscheduled treatment, patient AR/outstanding, unscheduled hygiene and unscheduled family; often also month-to-date/projected production and a patient list with filters. (PA-1034; on about 188 calls)  
> * Provider Hours: Enter per-provider working days and hours (weekly templates, lunch blocks, days off); used to calculate production per hour, chair time, utilization, opportunity, and provider performance metrics. (PA-0680 · 48:19; on about 159 calls)  
> * Collections: Shows collections received and pending from insurance and patients, typically split by payer, with AR/claims aging buckets (0-30/31-60/61-90/90+ days). (PA-0003 · 11:29; on about 150 calls)  
> * Provider Performance: Per-provider production, collections, visits, production per visit/hour/day, operatory/opportunity time, treatment acceptance, new patients, reappointment, cancellations/no-shows and utilization; requires providers (and often hours/operatories) to be set up first. (PA-1231 · 44:12; on about 147 calls)  
> * Follow ups: Internal staff reminders/tasks for patient outreach; create and assign manually or by automation by type (unconfirmed appointment, incomplete form, hygiene recare, unscheduled broken appointment/treatment, past due balance/claims, post-op); assign to self/team, set due date and notes, log attempts/outcomes, reassign or extend, complete; notes can sync to the EHR journal; team performance tracking. (PA-0001 · 21:46; on about 140 calls)  
> * Preferences / Set Goals: Set practice-level and provider-level production/collection goals (standard or advanced per-day with weekly template), plus the production calculation basis (procedure/entry date vs payment date) and gross vs net. (PA-0757 · 05:01; on about 132 calls)

> * Patient Card: Per-patient synopsis opened from a patient name, showing details, past/future appointments, activity log, forms, hygiene status, EHR balance/ledger, remaining benefits, unscheduled production, family, notes, reminders, and medical data. (PA-0975 · 04:37; on about 120 calls)

## **Definitions**

How each number, label, filter and rule is defined, as the PA team explained it on calls. Grouped by the PA area it belongs to.

### **Morning Huddle**

> * **Morning Huddle (default landing page)**: Clicking Practice Analytics opens the Morning Huddle by default; it is the start-of-day view of the day's schedule, production, new patients, goal, and opportunities, used by office managers and staff and often kept open through the day. (PA-0001 · 01:36; on about 268 calls)  
> * **New patient count/flag (NP)**: Count and list-view flag of patients coming in today who are new; a new patient is anyone scheduled for the first time in the EHR, shown as yes/no and used to filter or sort the list. **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0007 · 10:10; on about 169 calls)  
>   New patient definition differs by system: some say any patient coming for the first time; PA-1036 31:28 says a patient is new if no completed appointment exists in the past (not necessarily the same as ChiroTouch); PA-1048 35:45 says if a patient has any appointment in the past, not new.  
> * **Unscheduled treatment**: Patients coming in today with a treatment plan in the pipeline but no future appointment scheduled; shown as a count and dollar amount, with treatment codes on hover or in list view. (PA-0001 · 03:45; on about 133 calls)  
> * **Past due AR / patient outstanding**: Patient-side outstanding balance for today's patients; excludes insurance, and red amounts indicate what the patient owes. (PA-0001 · 05:50; on about 125 calls)  
> * **Unscheduled family members**: Count of family members of today's patients who are not scheduled for an appointment; these members can be contacted to schedule and the list shows which patient has how many unscheduled family members. (PA-0003 · 34:49; on about 121 calls)  
> * **Scheduled production**: Dollar production expected from the treatment codes attached to the day's scheduled appointments, using the practice's EHR fee settings; shown per patient and as an overall day total. (PA-0003 · 33:26; on about 108 calls)  
> * **Sync timing**: Adit re-reads the EHR and PA counts update progressively through the day; typical sync is every 3 to 5 minutes, with some practices reporting up to 10-15 minutes. **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0006 · 07:29; on about 98 calls)  
>   Dentrix syncs every two minutes (PA-1345 20:38, PA-1400 15:39); Open Dental and Eaglesoft sync every 4–5 minutes; one Dentrix call says 4–5 minutes (PA-1421 04:33).  
> * **Unscheduled hygiene**: Count of today's patients who have not yet been scheduled for their next hygiene appointment; reads from tomorrow onward and should drop to zero by end of day. (PA-0003 · 35:18; on about 88 calls)

### **Yesterday / past-day Huddle**

> * **Yesterday / past-day Huddle absolute data**: Shows absolute completed data for a 24-hour period or last working day, not progressive/live data like today's Huddle; includes completed appointments, new patients, production, collections, and unscheduled future appointments. Also called yesterday huddle, past-day huddle, yesterday view, or yesterday Morning Huddle. (PA-0006 · 21:22; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Unscheduled future appointments / reappointment report**: Patients who came in on a given date and left without scheduling any future appointment (hygiene or restorative). Count is usually all appointment types and can be filtered to hygiene; clicking the blue link opens the reappointment/recare report, and patients who later reschedule drop off the list. (PA-0006 · 22:31; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Blue highlighted links**: Blue highlighted items, words, titles, or numbers in the Huddle are hyperlinks to the underlying report or patient list; e.g., appointments link to visits, new patients to new patients, production to production, and unscheduled future appointments to reappointments. (PA-0034 · 16:13; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Production widget / production breakdown**: Breaks production into hygiene vs doctor/dentist portions; blue \= hygienist, yellow \= dentist; can toggle gross/net. May include collections, new patients, production per visit, and hygiene reappointment; for chiropractic splits adjustments, evaluations and management, therapeutic sales, and other; for optometry splits by sales, refractions, medical, ancillary contacts, and opticals. *Applies to: All; Chiropractic; Optometry and eye care.* (PA-0006 · 24:45; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Collections / collections widget**: Shows dollar amount collected from patients and insurance on the selected day; can drill to patient list and see bifurcation. Collections are measured midnight to midnight and include after-hours posting; chiropractic collections tracks all collected that day from insurance and patients. *Applies to: All; Chiropractic.* (PA-0042 · 12:01; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Production per visit / doctor-hygienist production per visit**: Average dollar value per visit for doctor and hygienist: total production divided by number of visits, sometimes based on procedure codes. Chiropractic production per visit is total chiro production divided by number of visits. *Applies to: All; Chiropractic.* (PA-0006 · 24:45; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Hygiene reappointment filter / hygiene reappointment**: Shows patients rescheduled specifically for a next hygiene appointment; it depends on a hygiene treatment/procedure code on the next appointment and reads only the hygiene part even if another procedure was scheduled. It can read zero or miss patients when hygiene codes are not entered; a patient is considered unscheduled hygiene if no hygiene ADA code is attached to the next appointment. *Applies to: All; Dental.* **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0006 · 22:31; on about 30 calls)  
>   One call disagrees on whether this metric is hygiene-specific.  
> * **New patient metrics**: Shows total patients who came in, new patient count, new patients vs total patients, production from new patients, and whether new patients were reappointed/rescheduled for hygiene (NP hygiene reappointment). NP stands for new patients; new patient reappointment timing is captured on a yesterday basis; new patient percentage shows new patients versus total patients and production associated with those new patients. *Applies to: All; Dental; Optometry.* (PA-0042 · 12:22; on about 30 calls)

### **Schedule view**

> * **List view**: List view lists the day's appointments with time, patient details, procedure/treatment codes, provider, appointment status, pending forms, scheduled/unscheduled production, next appointment, family/balance, and new-patient marker; clicking a patient name opens the patient card. (PA-0015 · 13:05; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Appointment sync timing**: Appointments created or scheduled in PA/Adit are written to the EHR appointment book, typically within 2 to 4 minutes depending on the EHR and call; some calls report 2-3 minutes and others 3-4 minutes. (PA-0005 · 43:21; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Appointment status**: Appointment status shows completed, confirmed, canceled, broken, scheduled, unconfirmed, and no-show; PA reads broken/cancelled/no-show status from the EHR after the practice tags it there. (PA-0007 · 13:49; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Schedule month view**: Month view shows day-wise scheduled production, completed production, and goal for the month, with options to view the entire practice or a selected provider and to move across past/future months. (PA-0029 · 61:47; on about 29 calls)  
> * **Scheduled production**: Scheduled production is calculated by reading the fee attached to procedure/treatment codes on scheduled appointments; without a code, PA cannot calculate or show production. PA currently shows it as a gross figure from the codes/fees, while some practices compare it to net or insurance-adjusted amounts. **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0036 · 00:20; on about 28 calls)  
>   PA scheduled production is described as gross from procedure codes/fees; Dentrix can show net post adjustments, and the practice says their Dentrix figure is gross. Open Dental fee schedule can also affect amounts.  
> * **Treatment code requirement**: Adding treatment/procedure codes to scheduled appointments lets PA identify the next appointment type and expected/scheduled production; without codes, appointments are uncategorized and PA cannot show treatment type or production. (PA-0036 · 00:20; on about 28 calls)  
> * **Schedule day view**: Day view shows the day's appointment list like Morning Huddle, including patient details, procedures, provider, status, pending forms, scheduled production, and can be used for a selected past day. (PA-0015 · 13:05; on about 28 calls)  
> * **Schedule week view**: Week view shows provider-level data for the week: days worked/availability, scheduled and completed production, and goal/under-over comparison. (PA-0104 · 33:25; on about 28 calls)

### **Provider view**

> * **Provider view**: Shows providers/doctors scheduled for the day or selected day/time with per-provider data: appointments, appointment count, new patients, unconfirmed appointments, scheduled/unscheduled production, hours, patient list, and expected production. Clicking a provider filters to that provider; for Orthodontics/Dentrix the morning huddle is 24 hours midnight to midnight. *Applies to: All; Dental, Orthodontics, Chiropractic; ChiroTouch, Dentrix, Open Dental.* (PA-0006 · 19:53; on about 125 calls)  
> * **Provider filter**: Clicking a provider's name filters the patient/appointment list to that provider; the top-right all-providers filter selects providers for list/provider view but does not change summary metrics. (PA-0162 · 26:24; on about 24 calls)  
> * **Provider view attribution**: How Provider view attributes appointments and production to providers; *Applies to: All; Dental Dentrix.* **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0098 · 55:29; on about 20 calls)  
>   Attribution source differs: PA uses treatment-table provider vs practice says/expects appointment provider.  
> * **Provider hours**: Provider hours come from the EHR (Open Dental, Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Crystal, ChiroTouch, OfficeMate, etc.); PA does not calculate them, and they can be removed via table settings. Missing hours leave blank or \$0. For Dentrix Ascend, provider hours come from Dentrix, but Adit provider reports require manually adding providers; they can be built from a template (e.g., hygienist Monday–Friday, last patient at 4, lunch break, back at 1). *Applies to: All; Dental Dentrix Ascend.* (PA-0003 · 36:50; on about 16 calls)  
> * **Provider view colors**: Appointment colors: doctor/restorative appointments are yellow (or orange in some Dentrix), hygiene appointments are blue, and white is empty chair time. **\[Conflict\]** (PA-0241 · 39:59; on about 5 calls)  
>   Doctor/restorative color differs: yellow vs orange.  
> * **Production per hour/day requires provider hours**: Production per hour/day metrics require provider hours for that month; without hours they show \$0 or are not shown. *Applies to: All; Dental Open Dental.* **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-1130 · 03:04; on about 4 calls)  
>   One says metrics show \$0 without hours; another says the metric is not shown.  
> * **Completed vs future attribution**: Scheduled/future appointments show the provider attributed to the procedure code; completed visits may update to the provider who actually completed the appointment (e.g., hygienist). (PA-0263 · 04:55; on about 3 calls)  
> * **Create appointment from PA**: Double-clicking empty chair time in Provider view creates an appointment that writes to the EHR appointment book in about 2–3 minutes. (PA-0241 · 39:59; on about 2 calls)

### **Operatory view**

> * **Appointment sync to EHR**: Appointments scheduled from operatory view write to the EHR appointment book, typically in about 3-4 minutes. (PA-0015 · 16:12; on about 70 calls)  
> * **Color coding: blue \= hygiene**: Blue appointments/blocks indicate hygiene appointments. (PA-0012 · 11:26; on about 70 calls)  
> * **Color coding: yellow \= doctor**: Yellow appointments/blocks indicate doctor/dentist appointments. (PA-0007 · 19:52; on about 65 calls)

> * **Operatory view colors: blue hygiene, yellow doctor**: Blue appointments are hygiene; yellow appointments are doctor appointments. *Applies to: Dental, Open Dental / Dentrix / Eaglesoft / Curve Dental / Dentrix Ascend / Tracker / ABELDent.* (PA-0540 · 12:38; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Schedule from operatory view by double-clicking empty chair time**: Double-clicking empty/white/gray chair time in the operatory view schedules an appointment. *Applies to: Dental, Dentrix / Eaglesoft / Open Dental / Curve Dental / ABELDent / Crystal PM.* (PA-0540 · 12:38; on about 30 calls)

### **Provider Performance dashboard**

> * **Production per visit**: Total production divided by number of visits, calculated separately for dentist and hygienist; includes completed visits even if no production on the appointment. Default is gross production, and drill-down can show gross and net. *Applies to: All; Dental (all EHRs).* **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0007 · 26:19; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Provider setup and mapping**: Requires providers/doctors/hygienists added in Preferences/Team Settings and mapped to operatories; provider list is pulled from EHR. Appointment provider and hygienist fields should both be populated because PA ties appointments to both; only added providers show, and incomplete setup blocks or zeros provider data. *Applies to: All; Dental (all EHRs).* (PA-0006 · 48:37; on about 30 calls)  
> * **Provider Performance dashboard and provider review**: Per-provider dashboard shows production per visit/hour/day, total production, visits, collections, cases, new patients, reappointments, cancellations/no-shows, and operatory utilization, using same data as practice performance filtered to selected provider/location. Clicking a provider name or Review Performance opens a provider-specific dashboard with detailed KPIs and underlying reports pre-filtered to that provider and time frame. *Applies to: All; Dental (all EHRs).* (PA-0013 · 62:42; on about 28 calls)  
> * **Provider utilization, operatory/chair time, and lost hours**: Busy hours versus total workable/scheduled hours, plus lost hours, available/occupied chair time, and time per operatory; occupied/busy time is read from patient status changes (e.g., ready/seated to completed) and requires marking patient out/checkout and completed. Available chair time can be misleading when a provider has multiple chairs; use occupied chair time. *Applies to: All; Dental (all EHRs).* (PA-0012 · 29:05; on about 28 calls)  
> * **Production by treatment code**: Shows production amount and procedure count/frequency for selected treatment codes; up to 5 codes at a time. Only codes actually used/completed in selected timeframe appear; if a code does not show there is no data for that provider/timeframe. Codes must be attached under provider in Dentrix for PA to fetch them, and one practice reports D4346 appears but D0180 does not. *Applies to: All; Dental (all EHRs).* **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0029 · 18:02; on about 28 calls)  
> * **Date range filter and provider dashboard date range**: Date range selector at top right defaults to week, with month/year and historical periods selectable; last week/last month is recommended for accurate/complete data, while current/next week is progressive and may be inaccurate until complete. Provider dashboard allows week or month only; week view shows entire week and weekly goal, not a single day, and longer/custom periods require individual reports; ongoing current week data appears once week ends. *Applies to: All; Dental.* **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0113 · 12:40; on about 28 calls)  
>   General date range supports month/year/historical, but provider dashboard date range is reported as week/month only with custom periods needing reports.  
> * **Diagnosis, treatment acceptance, and cases**: Diagnosed means provider proposed a treatment plan; accepted means patient accepted part or all and was scheduled, with dollar amounts. Counts patients seen, diagnosed/presented, accepted/scheduled, compares proposed vs accepted dollars, and shows restorative, perio, and hygiene cases; acceptance counts when at least one procedure is completed same day or scheduled for a future/next visit after diagnosis, and diagnostic rate is percentage of new patients diagnosed with a restorative or elective procedure during first visit. *Applies to: All; Dental (multiple EHRs).* **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0012 · 19:22; on about 26 calls)  
> * **Provider hours setup, source, update, and missing-data behavior**: Provider hours define start/end seeing patients, tied to operatories, and are used to calculate production per hour/visit/day and utilization; remove/deduct lunch and uncheck non-working days. Hours update from tomorrow onwards, past days must be updated manually, and saved templates apply forward about 6 months and must be reapplied; without hours, production per hour/day and operatory/opportunity time are unavailable, though one version says no production data shows at all. *Applies to: All; Dental (all EHRs).* **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0014 · 21:53; on about 25 calls)  
>   Source of provider hours is disputed; missing-hours impact is also disputed.

Detection generally requires a hygiene treatment code on the next appointment; without it PA cannot tell if the next appointment is hygiene. Definition varies by view/filter. **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0003 · 10:37; on about 29 calls)

> * **Production by treatment code**: Shows production and occurrence/procedure count by individual treatment code. Up to 5 codes can be selected at a time; can toggle gross/net and production/procedure count. A code missing means it was not completed in that time frame. (PA-0177 · 33:29; on about 29 calls)

The practice disputed the figure shown versus Dentrix, so the exact metric needs screen-share confirmation. **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0084 · 00:17; on about 25 calls)

### **Growth dashboard**

> * **Growth dashboard: overall definition**: Shows historic practice data, generally the last 3 years (36 months), with month-on-month comparisons of patient and production metrics; includes new, recaptured/reactivated, and lost patients, production growth, fluoride/perio counts, and year-over-year comparisons. (PA-0514 · 39:45; on about 49 calls)  
> * **Lost patients (combined rule)**: A patient is lost when they have no visit/appointment for 18 months, or when the practice marks them inactive/status change in the EHR; both are counted. (PA-0527 · 30:03; on about 49 calls)  
> * **Active patient base**: Patients who have come into the practice within the active/churn window (generally 18 months dental, 30 months optometry). **\[Conflict\]** **\[Needs confirmation\]** (PA-0507 · 06:05; on about 36 calls)  
>   Window differs by industry (18 months dental vs 30 months optometry);
